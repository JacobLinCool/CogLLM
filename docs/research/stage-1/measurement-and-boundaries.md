## The first batch of reproducible comparisons

- **Contradictory judgments for R09.** First, verify the composite accuracy. Check the contradiction condition across two versions of the same source family. Align the target term with its containing sentence to each complete version. Record reader-level position errors or first-discovery timestamps as needed.
- **Time limit for R20.** With reading-choice results and free recall as the two endpoints, after grouping readers by source family, estimate the conditional difference at 30/60/90 seconds. Use the same conditions for eye-tracking replication, retaining the sample denominator at each endpoint.
- **Overlap verification for R08 and R16.** R07–R08 have been confirmed to share materials and observations. R09–R16 were conservatively flagged as having common task sources. actual text ID/content comparison outside the main text serves as evidence confirming overlap.
