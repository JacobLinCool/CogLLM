# Reading Load in Research Papers and Expository Text

The objective is for CogLLM to estimate from the article alone where typical readers begin to experience difficulty, its intensity, and their comprehension after reading the full text. This includes passages that explain concepts, methods, or evidence throughout. Keep one causal forward pass. Include optional prereading knowledge where existing human data can validate its use. Authors and editors use response estimates at locations in the original article as evidence for comparing rewrites.

## Writing Choices and Reader Responses

| Writing choice | Text controls and manipulations | Human endpoint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explanation order | Hold propositions and wording constant. Compare giving a definition, benchmark, or causal premise before a dependent statement with postponing the explanation. | Compare reading effort, reported difficulty, or comprehension at observed stops. With postreading observations only, test effects on passage-level comprehension and inference. [Audit of Abel's expository order manipulation](stage6/abel-interleaving-audit.md) |
| Sentence construction | Hold propositions, terminology, and qualifications constant. Compare syntactic embedding, the order of main clauses and modifiers, or splitting a long sentence. | Separately test sentence reading ease, self-rated comprehension, and objective comprehension. Validating within-sentence positions requires local responses. PLABA plain-language rewrites vary multiple features and support testing the rewrite as a whole. An individual syntactic effect requires matched human contrasts. [PLABA-EVAL method](https://lrec.elra.info/lrec2026-ws-readixtsar-04) |
| Coherence and cues | Use semantically equivalent pairs to study subheadings, causal connectives, pronoun reference, or links between adjacent sentences. Fix one primary manipulation per round. | Kerwer provides original texts and postreading comprehension ratings for the same body text with and without subheadings. [Original method and Table 4](https://doi.org/10.1525/collabra.18898) · [Project data alignment](stage6/kerwer-article-holdout.md) |

One forward pass produces representations for all prefixes. Use readouts that match observed local ease or effort, comprehension of text read so far, and article-level outcomes. Outputs can include token and sentence estimates. Validation precision follows the human observations. Sentence-end ratings constrain sentence-end distributions. Postreading comprehension or confusion can constrain differences between article versions. Replacing or truncating later material should preserve estimates on the visible shared prefix. Where possible, report the magnitude of ease improvements alongside comprehension, so that a difficult passage that supports learning can be evaluated on both outcomes.

Resolution of an earlier question by a later clue remains a semantic phenomenon that can be tested at specified spans. Exposition can also produce difficulty within a sentence, require readers to maintain a main clause or comparison in memory, introduce cross-sentence integration demands, or affect understanding of an entire argument. Fit each response on its own human scale. Use readouts with passage-level observations or fixed aggregation rules to validate passage outcomes. Test how much sentence means, peaks, and spans of high difficulty explain human responses. Explanatory passages without explicit questions are primary evaluation material.

## Available Contrasts

**Kerwer's abstracts with and without subheadings provide a development diagnostic for text structure.** The versions share body-text order and wording. The observed postreading comprehension rating effect was +0.359 points, while averaging the existing CogLLM sentence-end readout yielded about 0 points. Keep this known mismatch to test whether full causal prefixes and the relationship between headings and body text provide a transferable signal. Compare length and position, sentence-only input, prefix input through the current sentence, and a paragraph readout. Align identical sentence endpoints by source character coordinates and keep headings visible in the prefix. Primary endpoints follow the original 1–8 postreading comprehension scale and the direction and magnitude of the version effect. The 1–5 postreading confusion scale can use a separate head. Kerwer's results have already informed diagnostics. Further validation needs a sealed article family or a separate study unused in development decisions. **PLABA-EVAL tests the effect of local rewrites.** Retain the existing splits for aligned biomedical-abstract sentence pairs and evaluate human reading ease, self-rated comprehension, and the change from rewriting separately. Qwen improved predictions of self-rated comprehension out of fold. The plain-language versions improved ratings by 0.723 points on average, compared with 0.341 points estimated by Qwen. In the next round, label alignable changes to syntax, concept order, and bridging explanations in advance. Keep versions that change multiple features as whole-rewrite comparisons. With no corresponding human results, minimal authored rewrites can test only model prefix localization and effect direction. [Existing out-of-fold results](backbone_transfer/results.md)

Abel's topic-grouped and interleaved versions, along with counts of human inferences, can test global effects of article order. Public materials are English translations. Readers' paragraph-level thoughts were not released. Use the study to guide the next group of order manipulations, prioritizing original stimuli and position-aligned measurements. Subsequent rewrite comparisons hold the intended propositions, qualifications, causal direction, and numbers fixed and compare response curves and postreading estimates. ## Supervision and Backbone Adaptation

The literature database records 46 empirical studies. Its coherence and reader-knowledge worksheet covers 12 of these, including narrative and instructional interventions. Full-text, trial data, and usage terms vary by study. Available data can already train readouts for target-word difficulty, sentence ratings, and passage comprehension. Sentence ratings or postreading averages alone leave the within-sentence effort trace unidentified. Model features and published effects can suggest candidate traces. Validation of their location and intensity requires human responses at corresponding stops. [Literature registry](reading-evidence.md) · [Discourse studies and data availability](stage-1/coherence-and-knowledge.md)

Use source articles or stimulus families as the main material unit. PLABA-EVAL has 1,826 sentence ratings from 39 source abstract and rewrite pairs. Jointly grouping by article and reader yields 25 connected groups. Kerwer has 1,947 complete responses from 12 sets of research abstracts. OneStopQA has 1,940 open-book comprehension responses from 20 source articles in the training or test splits. Fit original endpoints on the measurement scales available per trial. When studies share an original or simplified text, assign the entire family to the same fold. Keep data unused in development for validating the fixed readout on the same endpoint. Several ratings on the same study material provide repeated observations. [Sources and grouping](stage-2-data.md) · [Kerwer data](stage6/kerwer-article-holdout.md)

First compare surface features, a frozen Qwen linear readout over the current sentence, and a frozen Qwen prefix readout from one article pass. For finer modeling of article structure, use the sentence-start, sentence-end, and previous-sentence state from the same visible prefixes. Retain separate supervision for local and passage heads. Then compare the frozen backbone and a small LoRA update with identical readouts, inputs, precision, sentence boundaries, splits, and losses. Cross the sentence-level trial loss with and without an effect loss on rewrites in the training folds. In an earlier PLABA comparison, adding effect supervision alone increased the frozen Qwen estimate of the plain-language gain in self-rated comprehension from about +0.341 to +0.557 points, compared with +0.723 for humans. Both supervision and backbone adaptation need evaluation. [Completed effect comparison](stage-6-holdout-plan.md#incorporating-the-effect-of-rewriting-into-the-development-of-the-control-group) · [Previous training findings](findings-and-gaps.md#訓練方法目前知道什麼)

Base LoRA selection on response-distribution error and rewrite effect magnitude for unseen material groups. Test localization where local human observations exist. When evaluating held-out differences between heading conditions or original and plain-language versions, condition fields are used for splitting, computing training effects, and scoring. Check that scores agree before versions diverge. Before fine-tuning on L4, measure backpropagation memory and verify prefix agreement at the chosen precision. Inference uses one causal forward pass. The L4 comparison with final-layer rank-4 LoRA is complete. The frozen model has lower distribution errors on both sentence rating scales. LoRA's point estimate for the average plain-language rewrite effect is closer to the human effect, while pairwise error in self-rated comprehension increases. Neither configuration meets the prespecified magnitude criterion for Kerwer's same-body subheading effect. ## From Sentence Ratings to the Reading Process

First test a falsifiable claim. In materials and studies held out from development, CogLLM can reproduce where and by how much a change in the preceding text affects responses when the reader reaches the same critical span. Where a study measures later interpretations, the model can also reproduce their distribution. The first contrast uses prior reference and disambiguation. In the existing English holdout, the disambiguating region is identical across versions. Changing the prior context from one referent to two reduced the human rate of first lookback in that region by 4.58 percentage points. The Qwen readout estimated an increase of 0.05 percentage points. These materials have informed error analysis and now serve as a development contrast. Kerwer remains a postreading diagnostic for article structure. [Existing lookback holdout](semantic_tracking/context-readback-transfer-results.md) · [Kerwer comparison](expository_lora/results.md)

Using frozen Qwen and a small readout, compare a direct readout with a readout of equal capacity that explicitly tracks a provisional interpretation and reinterpretation after a cue. Give both the same causal prefixes, observation labels, paired loss, and training budget. Use a time-to-event likelihood for the first lookback on the presentation region. Preserve interval uncertainty within that region and score right censoring when no lookback occurs by the final observation. Use separate observation heads for interpretation choice and postreading ratings. The paired loss constrains human condition differences within the same family and stop while retaining trial distribution error. Some comprehension items in the public SAP data distinguish the syntactic interpretation readers retained. Before integration, verify item wording, presentation stops, and material overlap. Keep original sentences, rewrites, and presentation variants together whenever they share stimuli. [SAP methods and comprehension accuracy](https://tallinzen.net/media/papers/huang_et_al_2024_jml.pdf) · [Author's data](https://osf.io/b6rqh/) · [Interval likelihood and single-forward design](semantic_tracking/semantic-load-curve-plan.md)

Seal material families and verifiable reader groups first. Where suitable, hold out a whole new study. Compare against baselines using current text, position, lexical features, and Qwen surprisal. Check the observed first-response stop, false positives before the cue, the prespecified acceptable range for the condition difference, and postreading answers that distinguish original and revised interpretations. Replace continuations to verify agreement over visible shared prefixes. If observations are at sentence or multiword-region level, localization is valid at that interval granularity. To test what the pretrained weights contribute, add a random-weight model of the same architecture and a readout of the same capacity. Use matching data and search budgets. [Prespecified lookback criteria](semantic_tracking/context-readback-transfer.md)

A separate mechanism test turns variables from candidate human theories into states that can be manipulated. For example, swap the current interpretation between paired texts and test whether later interpretation choices and lookback readouts change selectively in the hypothesized direction. Compare against misplaced or unrelated state swaps. Use several kinds of human observation to constrain candidate theories in advance and test divergent predictions on new materials. This checks whether the model realizes a specified abstract mechanism in a way consistent with human behavior. Interventions add experimental executions. Standard inference still uses one causal forward per article.
