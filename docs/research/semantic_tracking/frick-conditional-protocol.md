# Frick's condition of intra-sentence antecedent similarity

In the retrospective holdout of 20 story sets from Frick's Experiment 1, four-category position calibration improved predictions of early reports, while the difference in reporting probabilities between the two conditions at the target sentence remained too small. This round retains the same frozen Qwen3.5-2B causal sentence-end representations and seven features. Fit a conditional likelihood on matched target sentences and test whether it increases the effect magnitude while preserving accurate probabilities across the whole passage. Readers press a key after reading a full sentence. The observation is the first inconsistency report at a sentence end. [Target-sentence diagnostic and conditional likelihood](frick-target-hazard-diagnostic.md) · [Original four-category position results](frick-mass-calibration-results.md)

## fitting

A pair is formed only when both versions of the sentence are identical character-for-character and belong to the same story's target sentence. Pairs are constructed exclusively from the target sentences of the training source family. Earlier or later sentences participate in fitting directly via the complete first event and the four position terms. For the two risk sets of the target sentence, with counts $R_{01},R_{02}$, and fixed total first report counts across two groups $K$, the number of reports is estimated using the difference in logits of two logistic hazard models for the inconsistent version. This conditional distribution is fully normalized over its feasible integer support set. The conditional negative log-likelihood for each story is divided by the product of the two risk set counts for its target sentence, then aggregated using equal weighting across stories. [Conditional distribution formula](frick-target-hazard-diagnostic.md#the-next-pre-specified-control)

A single seven-feature logistic head simultaneously fits the sentence-wise risk set's negative log-likelihood, the cross-entropy across four position classes, and the pairwise conditional likelihood. The denominator for sentence-wise likelihood and position terms reuses the prior round's values, while coefficients retain a 0.10 ridge penalty and the intercept is unpenalized. Four position class weights are denoted as $lambda$, and conditional term weights as $gamma$. This design does not employ individual predictions from the prior model as training offsets. The existing four-class calibration model serves solely as a sealed snapshot baseline and is initialized via its equivalent phase-only solution for the new conditional model in this fold. All models continue to use causal features from a single document, predicting position-wise, with target sentence position, conditions, and pairwise results utilized exclusively for training and evaluation. [Original phase-only likelihood](frick-mass-calibration-protocol.md)

## Pre-specified nested held-out

The outer 20-fold split holds out a complete story for each of the two versions. The remaining 19 folds are shuffled and cyclically assigned to four inner held-out folds. For each fitted pair of readout, no-issue, prefix, source family, random seed, matching, nats, held-out, anchor span, response distribution, documentation, and retention constraints, the direction of comparison, feature scale, and coefficients are derived solely from the training stories within that specific fold. The selection process utilizes full predictions from the inner held-out folds, with equal weighting applied to each story and the two conditions within that story.

First, fix $gamma=0$, and among the four classes in $lambdain\{0,0.25,1,4\}$, select the one with the lowest cross-entropy. The candidate must satisfy that the inner-layer full-document NLL of the initial event does not exceed the readout of equal weighting by more than 0.05 nats/document. This step reconstructs the previous round's four-class calibration comparison. Next, fix the selected $lambda$, and among the four classes in $gammain\{0,0.25,1,4\}$, select the one with the smallest absolute error for the first report under the target sentence condition. The candidate's inner-layer NLL must not exceed the readout of both the phase-only and equal weighting by more than 0.05 nats/document, and the estimation bias of the early report point for both conditions must fall within ±10 percentage points. In case of a tie, select the smaller weight. If no qualified candidate exists in the second step, retain the fixed phase-only readout and record no qualified candidate. Aggregate the outer-layer results only after the sealed snapshot outer-layer prediction.

## Scoring

We continued with 10,000 cross-samples of readers and stories, seed 735972, and a 95% interval. We compared the four types of calibration of the sealed snapshot, seven-feature equal weighting, and the original prefix readout. The original acceptance criteria still required the target sentence condition difference to be positive, the model minus human complete interval to be within ±10 percentage points, and the early report bias to pass within ±10 percentage points for each of the two prior conditions. The full report NLL relative to the seven-feature equal weighting and the original prefix model must have an upper bound of the interval smaller than +0.05 nats per story. We also report the target sentence magnitude and NLL difference relative to the four types of calibration. This is a retrospective diagnostic of the same experimental material. The corpus only records the first contradictory keypress, without tracking the resolution of the same question.
