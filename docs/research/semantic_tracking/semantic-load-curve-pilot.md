# per-token unresolved question curve's first-round control

The fixed Qwen3.5-2B official snapshot performs a formal causal forward pass over each document on Colab L4 in FP32. For each native token, we store the character coordinates and the final layer's 160-dimensional fixed random projection. Truncation or post-text replacement requires causal verification. GPU encapsulation includes the body and conditional-free IDs, while labeling, splitting, and conditional control remain on the host. Each training condition is sampled once. Using the same L4 instance, the configuration, download, and release are capped at 24 minutes. The readout fit uses a sealed snapshot representation executed on CPU.

## Each of the three observations is scored

The primary supervision is the SCARECROW contradiction selection covering ExpMRC text evidence positions. Blott 2023 provides verifiable end-of-sentence judgment responses, serving as the third observation. SCARECROW is post-selection. the model's open score is trained on this signal's question candidates. ExpMRC answer ranges are annotated by human, representing the temporal proxy of text evidence. Training anchors are formed by matching at least two different question content terms with preceding text segments. During corpus testing, no input of questions, answers, human anchors, or manually identified divergence positions is provided. Blott's end-of-sentence responses are used solely for its own binary readout. ExpMRC's public dev split is fixed at 70/30 by article hash, constituting a retrospective held-out set. The original QASPER contains only one training and one held-out example each for length and alignable anchor pre-checks, reserved for data statistics. All subset raw splits, observation types, and missing masks are listed in the data manifest.

Each dataset is divided by its respective valid observation weights. Contradiction selection and end-of-sentence application use binary negative log-likelihood. Evidence spans are projected to source tokens, fitting the negative log of the interval quality of the first extracted evidence probability while retaining the condition that the anchor span is visible. The cumulative survival probability at each step prior to the first interval quality event is multiplied by the probability of at least one occurrence within the interval. The estimated endpoints are labeled evidence and cannot be back-inferred to a human subjective resolution of time from this likelihood.

## Readout and matching conditions

Each observation head computes a 24-dimensional tanh adapter from the same Qwen projection, optionally with fixed embeddings and linear skips. Evidence heads additionally employ position-biased readout vectors cosine-anchored to anchor spans and gated by token count. For comparison A, each task fits an independent adapter. for comparison B, adapters are shared while readout heads are decoupled, with an ablation reporting B's performance when sharing is restricted to only the selection and evidence heads. Initialization uses 21, 47, and 101. Regularization, primary task data, and optimization budget remain consistent. We report the per-endpoint held-out negative log-likelihood, sampled via material family pairing. Training splits are grouped by source article, and held-out predictions are computed from weights in a sealed snapshot. Prior held-out results serve as a baseline for comparison.

The first-round curve uses each token containing a character as a query candidate position, leveraging the selection head dependent solely on read content to provide an opening quality. Adjacent candidates share a limited interval of opening probabilities to avoid attributing full credit to every token in a continuous selection. The unresolved quality following each candidate is the cumulative product of the evidence head's per-position survival probability, which is summed across positions to yield the curve. Since candidates serve as proxies for query positions, multiple candidates may share the same query. thus, the curve reports the model's unresolved quality. Mirror overlap, query identification, and human scales are reserved for subsequent validation. This version adopts a simplified state diagram that maintains the "ended" state after the first round.

## The same three continuations as in the preceding text

Fixed at 12 topics, with three variations per topic. Within each topic, the "unlocked" and "delayed" variants share identical answer sentences and maintain interrogative sentences. The "sudden topic switch" and "delayed" variants share the original text before paragraph separation, with the topic switch occurring afterward. All topics are independent of the human training set, with material and event coordinates fixed and hashed prior to the first model output. Text is held out from training, and no human data is used.

Tokens with identical prefixes must share the same coordinates and scores, and no curve deviation caused by continuation should appear before the divergence. Additionally, account for the remaining mass of open candidates at each time point within the shared prefix to exclude newly introduced candidates after topic switching. The primary comparisons are: early resolution versus delayed resolution at the end of the segment. sudden topic switching versus delayed resolution immediately after the end of the shared second segment. and the window closest to the answer or transition. For each, report the direction, magnitude, matching interval, and whether the position falls within the pre-specified clue range. The transition position is controlled relative to the original text. this round's curve directly validates whether the old query persists, while the independent human transition probability belongs to the next endpoint.
