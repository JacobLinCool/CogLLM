# Readers' practice history and story-line held-out results

Qwen readout replicated the direction of the block practice group being more influenced by prior context, but the effect magnitude was insufficient. In the matched comparison of two stores' redo readers, the human groups' context effect differed by **78.0 percentage points**, while Qwen readout was **14.3 percentage points**. The sequential probability NLL decreased from the baseline of **0.630 to 0.619 nats**, with a difference of −0.0109 having a 95% reader matched confidence interval of [−0.0258, +0.0043]. The pre-specified significance and magnitude acceptance criteria were both not met. ## Execution and Validation

Freezing the same Gradio candidate Qwen3.5-2B processes 2,346 unique text prefixes or option sentences on a single L4 node, totaling 68,492 tokens. Each text undergoes one FP32 causal forward pass. all 6,221 source reading stops pass the tokenization prefix check. An additional 12 FP32 causal controls are applied, with forward and readout phases taking 30.0 seconds and the extraction phase 73.9 seconds, respectively, while GPU peak memory retention reaches 7.52 GiB. Re-running the inference after locally unlocking the manifest sealed snapshot. CPU matching and scoring replay sum to approximately 0.52 seconds with a peak of 138 MiB, yielding bit-identical results, and five groups of NLL and primary effect clues are independently recomputed. L4 has been verified and released. ## Post-hoc comparison of readout selection and cue identification

The fixed-projection Qwen representations are capable of identifying story cues. In the original material, 50 fictional names were retained for validation. center-point fitting and multi-class probing were applied to the representations of other original names. Both methods correctly identified 99/99 end-of-scene sentences, 345/345 current events, and 200/200 option events. The material shared the same author template. This result localizes the content identifiable by representations, indicating that held-out variations in character vocabulary do not encompass new syntax or story elements. [Cue Identification Score](../../../research/results/beukers-state-diagnostic.json)

Reintegrate the extracted coffee shops, event paths, and candidate identities into the sealed snapshot structure for readout, then re-score the same batch of 875 trials. The model correctly labels all trials with ground-truth labels, reproducing the structural baseline of **0.476 nats** and an inter-group effect difference of **+60.2 percentage points**. All predictions were written to the sealed snapshot file prior to post-hoc re-connection with human choices. Results indicate that the original direct readout did not leverage available structural signals, and concrete improvements are possible within this fixed template. The post-hoc replay does not provide independent evidence of choice generalization, and the magnitude difference with the human effect remains beyond the established range.
