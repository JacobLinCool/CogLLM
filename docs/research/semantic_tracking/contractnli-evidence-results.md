# ContractNLI's independent evidence localization results

**Frozen per-token readout passes one of four pre-specified criteria.** Across 47 held-out documents and 487 propositions with human evidence, the per-token interactive readout achieved an AP of 0.114, paragraph interactive readout 0.105, and vocabulary matching BM25 0.409. The per-token AP exceeds paragraph readout by 0.009, with a 95% confidence interval [0.004, 0.015]. When the same proposition is paired with a different set of evidence, the performance of the two Qwen readouts is comparable. [Fixed acceptance criteria](contractnli-evidence-protocol.md) · [sealed snapshot results](../../../research/results/contractnli-evidence-l4/results.json)

## Specify the evidence ranking for the specified hypothesis

47 full-text articles were processed via a single causal forward pass, while 17 public hypotheses were each extracted once and shared across all articles. A fixed Qwen3.5-2B backbone with QASPER head was used, trimming trailing whitespace from the original candidate endings while preserving character-level alignment. Among 4,318 original candidates, 102 contained only whitespace, 9 could not be precisely aligned with the tokenizer, and 4,207 were evaluable. All 487 hypotheses supported by evidence and 718 non-overlapping evidence pairs of support/rebuttal were retained. Higher AP indicates that human evidence is more concentrated at the top of the ranking, with a maximum value of 1. Pairwise scores for the same document also range from 0 to 1. When comparing two sets of evidence under the same hypothesis, the metric assesses whether the evidence for one hypothesis ranks above the evidence for the other. Each of the two reversal directions accounts for half of the total, comprising 1,436 directions. The per-token relative paragraph readout difference is −0.0013 [−0.0098, 0.0067], and the relative difference to BM25 is −0.2094 [−0.2476, −0.1706]. This comparison uses human-annotated evidence sets to define which positions should shift with the hypothesis, without evaluating the model's judgment on the validity of the hypothesis itself. ## Remove the shared position scores from the article

After the primary validation, the average of the 17 hypothesis logits per reading stop in each article is treated as a shared position score. From each hypothesis's original logit, this score is subtracted, and the result is transformed via the logistic function, then evaluated using the same candidate and resampling scoring. This diagnostic procedure applies all fixed hypotheses and does not compute the shared score using human answers. [Diagnostic Program](../../../research/semantic_tracking/contractnli_centered_diagnostic.py) · [Diagnostic Results](../../../research/results/contractnli-evidence-l4/centered-diagnostic.json)

After removing shared scores, the matched score increased from 0.527 to **0.854**, a difference of +0.327 [0.291, 0.360], which is also higher than BM25's 0.737, with a difference of +0.118 [0.073, 0.164]. AP increased from 0.114 to 0.216, yet remains lower than BM25, with a difference of −0.193 [−0.223, −0.164]. Results indicate that representations contain signals capable of distinguishing propositions between two sets of manually annotated evidence. Finding evidence in the entire unannotated corpus remains more challenging. This serves as a supplementary cross-validation on the evaluated data, utilizing the same 17 fixed propositions. A single new proposition does not have an identical shared score that can be directly subtracted. [Difference interval](../../../research/results/contractnli-evidence-l4/centered-diagnostic.json)

## Probability of reading complete marked evidence

Among the 799 propositions, 487 have manually selected evidence, and 312 are marked as not mentioned. Event NLL is computed using the endpoint of the last annotated span, and not mentioned is evaluated via the final remaining probability. NLL units are nats/proposition, where lower is better, with an ideal value of zero. The original readout per-token is **8.530**, the original ExpMRC transfer calibration is **29.581**, and the QASPER mixed annotation fit time calibration is **3.272**. The original ExpMRC calibration yields a final average remaining probability of 0.002 and an NLL for not mentioned of 57.831. The QASPER time calibration adjusts these to 0.451 and 1.173, respectively. After adding a fixed remaining probability term, the overall NLL becomes 3.279. These probabilities correspond to using only sealed snapshot weights and are supplementary results. [Conditional Probability and Brier Error](../../../research/results/contractnli-evidence-l4/results.json)

Manual annotation specifies only the evidence to be adopted within the full text. For instance, when two consecutive segments each provide identical evidence, the reading stop definition still takes the last marked segment. This experiment can verify where the text appears. when humans detect contradictions, form questions, or determine that a problem has been resolved, they still require a report at the corresponding location. [Observation definition vs. held-out](contractnli-evidence-protocol.md) · [Original annotation rules](https://aclanthology.org/2021.findings-emnlp.164.pdf#page=2)

## Reproducibility and Resources

Data preparation executed `research.semantic_tracking.contractnli_prepare`, tokenization validation and remote inference used `research.semantic_tracking.contractnli_forecast`, local scoring used `research.semantic_tracking.contractnli_report`. The same prediction was scored twice with bit-wise consistency. Additionally, an independent program recalculated AP, paired scores, four per-token probabilities, and intervals, with a maximum numerical difference of \(3.55\times10^{-15}\). [Data preparation](../../../research/semantic_tracking/contractnli_prepare.py) · [Scoring program](../../../research/semantic_tracking/contractnli_report.py) · [Reproduce manifest](../../../research/results/contractnli-evidence-l4/run-verification.json)

Formal L4 readout processed 131,859 tokens, 4,271 no-issue reading stops, and 12 prefix checks passed. Forward and extraction of the body and hypothesis took approximately 56.92 seconds. the entire inference stage took approximately 134.07 seconds. GPU peak retention was approximately 9.38 GiB. Local scoring took approximately 1.04 seconds with a peak of 114 MiB. The first run missed the sub-command and did not execute the model forward. Both instances have been released, and the formal job's pre- and post-accounting show 734.86 and 734.80 CU, respectively.
