# Pairwise sentence matching training slightly increases the target sentence effect

In Frick's 20 story-hold-out groups, after incorporating the matching target sentence condition likelihood, the first report's prefix conditional difference increased from **17.15 to 18.95 percentage points**, with an increment of +1.80 and a 95% confidence interval of [+0.77, +2.98]. The human difference was **48.27**. The model minus human interval [−40.05, −18.46] exceeded the ±10 percentage point magnitude threshold. [Original Diagnosis](frick-target-hazard-diagnostic.md) · [Fixed Specification](frick-conditional-protocol.md) · [Scoring Results](../../../research/results/frick-conditional/results.json)

We readout the seven existing causal features, incorporating the conditional likelihood of four position cross-entropy and the target sentence as the first event into the overall loss. The conditional likelihood fixes the sum of the two risk set sizes and the first report for each training pair, using the difference of the two target sentence logits. A 20-fold split by story held-out. within each fold, training pairs, feature scales, and inner criteria are recomputed on the training data. All folds select position term weights of 4 and pair term weights of 4. Observations from other sentences are used for the overall loss and position cross-entropy. This time, we use already evaluated material to diagnose the learning objective, while independent corpus generalization requires separate evaluation. [Model Conditional Likelihood](../../../research/semantic_tracking/frick_conditional_fit.py) · [Fold Selection](../../../research/results/frick-conditional/fits.json)

The goal sentence prefix bias for the consistent and inconsistent versions was +3.65 and +3.25 percentage points, respectively, with both intervals falling within the ±10 threshold. The overall NLL was 1.5939 nats/document, with a relative four-position calibration increase of +0.0202 [0.0041, 0.0370]. The relative seven-feature equal weighting readout difference was −0.0508 [−0.1829, +0.0878], with the upper bound of the interval exceeding the +0.05 non-inferiority limit. Results were retained as paired training holdout controls. [Itemwise and comparisons](../../../research/results/frick-conditional/results.json)

The sealed snapshot shows that the four products are bitwise reproducible. After independently removing the held-out story targets and human response information, all 584 reconstructed logits are identical. Using the same frozen Qwen end-of-sequence state, single-threaded fitting and scoring take approximately 1.57 seconds, with a peak memory usage of about 111 MiB.
