# Cross-study held-out results for Blott's re-interpretation of the term "semantic blott"

Fixed the end-of-sentence understanding score for Gradio candidates, without passing human behavioral validation via English sense reanalysis. Among 44 out-of-fit sentence frames, the unambiguous version was more frequently judged by humans as "makes sense" than the reanalysis-needed version, with a difference of **16.33 percentage points**, 95% confidence interval **[10.55, 22.70]**. The model's estimated difference in understanding scores was **0.09 percentage points**, interval **[−0.25, 0.42]**. The model's effect on the human difference was −16.24 percentage points [−22.73, −10.34], exceeding the pre-specified ±5 percentage point tolerance. NLL is the negative log-probability per keystroke, measured in nats, with 0 as the ideal value. The NLL improvement with Qwen is −0.00107 nats/keystroke, 95% CI [−0.00234, +0.00015]. None of the three thresholds—improvement, direction, and magnitude—were met. Both groups used 2,256 judgments from 2021 for fitting. The held-out set included 1,558 reader-pair matches from 108 readers, totaling 3,116 keystrokes. Of the original 63 sentence frames, 19 were excluded due to sharing key nouns and cues with 2021 materials. The uncalibrated comprehension scores show a human sentence-pair ranking agreement of **65.91%**, with an ease of 52.27%, negative character count of 48.86%, and negative word count of 51.14%. A t-test was performed. These are secondary ranking results. For 44 pairs, the pre- and post-word comprehension scores for 2023 were aligned. the difference in pre- to post-word change, without ambiguity requiring re-interpretation, was +0.015 points on the model's original 1–5 scale. Simultaneously swapping the head noun and final word in both versions allows the difference to describe how the model output updates. In the human condition, keypresses were entered only after viewing the complete sentence, without pre-word comprehension, reader-identified sources of confusion, or a confirmed point of resolution. [Model endpoints and computation](../../../research/results/blott-format-transfer-l4/forecasts.json) · [Original experimental procedure](https://doi.org/10.1080/23273798.2022.2159992)

The same Gradio candidate ran one causal forward pass for each of 88 sentences on Colab L4. Three batches extracted 350 registered stops across 1,299 tokens. All 12 independent causal checks passed. FP32 forward passes and readout took 0.91 seconds. Extraction with model setup took about 41 seconds, and peak GPU reservation was about 7.44 GiB. The first run completed remote extraction and timed out during download. A second run used the same data and model. Checks verified download hashes, the seal, and bitwise agreement between two CPU scoring runs. Independent recomputation from keypresses confirmed point estimates and intervals for all 11 metrics. Both L4 releases were verified. Local scoring ran without loading the model.
