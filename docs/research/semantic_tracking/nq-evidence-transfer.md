# Evidence at readout in the Natural Questions independent validation

Fixed Natural Questions official validation Hugging Face release `e8103d566bef4154c2c12b17c6095ec5275840cc`. Excluding unannotated indices of IIRC original text and visible link titles from the previous verification, sorted by SHA-256 of Wikipedia revision IDs per `cogllm-nq-v1:`, retaining the top 72 page versions. Verified that each question in the same version shares original text and source tokens, keeping only truly distinct questions. The model uses the text order of non-HTML tokens from the original file, with sentence segmentation and reading stop verification identical to ExpMRC. Full articles must comply with the shared interface's 16,000 Unicode character limit and not exceed 6,144 Qwen tokens. The total budget for all articles and questions is capped at 120,000 tokens. complete valid materials are retained in fixed order, and processing halts upon exceeding the total budget with the next group. Data or reading stop failures are explicitly listed, as these filters depend on the original text and question. [Unannotated Index Verification](nq-evidence-source-audit.md) · [Official Data](https://ai.google.com/research/NaturalQuestions/download)

Four model groups fully adopt QASPER's fixed head and ExpMRC-fitted sealed snapshot calibration parameters. Qwen3.5-2B FP32 performs a single causal forward pass per document, sharing the document state for that document. Each prompt is encoded separately, then read out using the sealed snapshot. Raw predictions are first sealed snapshot, with human annotations retained for the scoring stage. The primary endpoint is the visibility of the complete human long-answer support region, with five annotations weighted equally. Annotations lacking sufficient support regions are scored by the probability after the observation window. Positive examples are matched against raw tokens and byte coordinates, then placed into the first aligned reading stop containing the complete region. When coordinates cannot align, the cause and denominator are recorded. [Original Measurement](https://aclanthology.org/Q19-1026/) · [sealed snapshot calibration](evidence-calibration-comparison-results.md)

- The primary validation metric is the post-calibration per-token NLL difference between post-calibration items and stems, with the 95% paired article group interval upper bound required to be below zero. We also compare post-calibration paragraph and position, cumulative Brier scores, and first-reading stop probabilities across groups, while reporting the original uncalibrated scores.
- Comparisons of same-text and same-reading-stop must have at least two non-empty reference points each in early and late conditions, with all non-empty reference segments strictly separated. Only certified reading-stop comparisons where the early segment is fully reached and the late segment has not yet begun. The comparison with higher cumulative intensity in the early segment wins. ties count as half. This is relative to similarly calibrated stem readout article group pairings. Files that cannot be paired are still included in valid interval scoring.
- Clustered by normalized Wikipedia article titles, retaining the title of the same article and all related revisions within the same cluster. Re-sampled 10,000 clusters with the previously fixed seed. Long answers containing tables or lists maintain their original coordinate mapping, while text-based answers report their applicable scope. This evaluation assesses reference evidence retrieval given a query.
