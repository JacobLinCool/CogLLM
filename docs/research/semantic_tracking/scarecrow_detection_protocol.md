# # Body Localization and Severity Specifications for Self-Contradictory Fragments

Dong et al.'s SCARECROW prompts annotators to view the news headline and the model's continuation, then review the selected continuation errors in terms of type, span, and severity. "Self-contradiction" has available prior context pointing to the annotation. Severity levels 1, 2, and 3 sequentially describe increasingly severe impacts on text quality or understandability. This evaluation uses existing public continuations, estimating per-word whether trained annotators would mark that location as self-contradictory and its severity. [Original study §§4.3–4.6, Appendix B.1](https://aclanthology.org/2022.acl-long.501.pdf#page=6) · [Author release](https://yao-dou.github.io/scarecrow/)

## Body, reading stop, and split

For each author-released model, perform a causal forward pass of Qwen3.5-2B on generated continuations, extracting the 128-dimensional sealed snapshot projection at the readout position that aligns precisely with the end of tokens in the native tokenizer. Third-party news articles are used locally solely for article source grouping. For cases where news articles are required, the referenced text occurs after the selected text, or the source cannot be placed in an earlier continuation based on publication coordinates, mask the affected positions of the annotator. For all other valid read tokens, calculate four counts based on the author's selection coverage: unselected, degree 1, 2, and 3. Unselected indicates the annotor did not report this issue. If multiple retained selections by the same annotor overlap on the same token, adopt the most severe level. Each original token boundary will be re-verified for its exact UTF-16 and Qwen coordinates. The author has displayed the full text. the evaluation of end-token scores is a post-hoc fragment localization.

Reuse the prompt family split for earlier text that has already been performed. The remaining unused prompt families are sorted by fixed hash `sha256("scarecrow-detection-v1:" + group_id)`, with the first one-fifth held-out. All news beginnings that are identical or identical standardized continuations are merged into a single group. Unlabeled contradictory continuations are retained, and the denominators and results for the old and new families are reported separately. The earlier held-out text is marked as having already undergone evaluation. this time, it involves a new endpoint on the same material and a supplementary validation on the unused families.

## Readout and validation

Compare the three groups, each using only its visible prefix at the end of the same word. The constant group uses the training distribution. The surface group adds word length, read count, whether the same word repeated previously, distance from last occurrence, case and character type of the current word and the preceding word, and vocabulary diversity over the preceding 32 words. The Qwen group uses the same surface features plus the current projection and the difference between adjacent end projections. The state difference for the first word is set to zero. Training data is used to compute center and scale. surface features are normalized per dimension, and each of the two vectors is normalized by the square root of the total variance. All reads provide no artificial markers, candidate answers, model names, or paragraph lengths.

Each group comprises three Bernoulli factors: the probability of a word being selected $p$, the probability of a degree of at least 2 given selection $s$, and the probability of a degree of 3 given a degree of at least 2 $t$. Output the four probabilities $(1-p,p(1-s),ps(1-t),pst)$. For each factor, minimize negative log-likelihood with exact counting within its own training denominator, add a fixed $0.003\lVert w\rVert_2^2/2$, and do not penalize the intercept. After model training, all words will produce a distribution, and scoring is applied only at points with human observations.

The first criterion uses within-passage average precision on held-out passages containing both positive and zero-vote words. A word is positive if at least one eligible annotator marked it. The second criterion is the NLL of the full four-category distribution over all eligible annotator–word pairs. The separate severity criterion is the NLL of the three-category severity distribution conditional on a mark. The primary comparison subtracts the surface-feature model from Qwen. For localization, the full distribution, and severity, performance differences are evaluated with 95% intervals from 10,000 paired prompt-family resamples. The prespecified equivalence bound for the 90% interval of the model's overall word-level mark rate minus the human rate is ±1 percentage point. For mean severity conditional on a mark, the bound is ±0.15 levels on the original scale. These are operational precision criteria for this dataset. Passages with no marks are included in probability scoring. We also report the marking probabilities that each model assigns to all-zero-vote passages.
