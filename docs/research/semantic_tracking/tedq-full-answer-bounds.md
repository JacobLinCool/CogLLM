# The identifiable scope of TED-Q fully answered

TED-Q ceases tracking a query upon its first rating of 3, with the maximum scale of 5 representing a fully answered query. Among the 2,412 queries in the author's published table, **359 received a 5**, and §4 reports an additional 367. The denominator and counts herein are based on a fixed version of the published table. Additionally, **517 queries** were first rated 3 or 4. while the reader and excerpt indicate a subsequent reading stop, the system did not re-ask the same query. Holding observed ratings, if an additional rating were applied to this segment, the proportion of fully answered samples would range between **14.88% and 36.32%**. The endpoint is defined as the earliest of at most two reading stops presented after the query, where the query receives a score of 5 at least once. The lower bound assumes all missing second-round scores are below 5, while the upper bound assumes they equal 5. Both scenarios retain the published observations. Queries ending at the end of the excerpt are truncated at their actual final reading stop, with no additional appendage. The range in the table is determined by the missingness mechanism, not a sampling confidence interval, and does not estimate the impact of extended tracking itself on readers. Among 1,257 reading stops where the same reader simultaneously evaluated two queries, **13 instances** showed the older query receiving a 5 while the newer query received only 1 or 2. If completion is defined by the reader's report at that moment, even this stricter threshold cannot fully replicate the fixed rule of prioritizing the newer query. The data cannot determine token ordering within the same segment. Model supervision can treat "first report at least partially answered" as an observed interval event. To validate complete answering or subsequent confusion, unreviewed reading stops at 3–4 minutes must be retained as missing. The 3–4 minute threshold represents the condition for ceasing subsequent annotation collection for this item and should not be interpreted as a rule for the model to clear the query. This analysis recalculates only the author ratings, reading stop indices, and associated fields. Verbatim transcripts and individual queries from the data were not used in the model.
