# IIRC phrase localization on held-out passages

**Qwen improved coverage for single and two distinct phrase types, both exceeding the predetermined comparison.** For unseen text passages, the single-target overlap coverage was 9.67 percentage points higher than the form baseline among the top five proposals, and each of the two reading stop targets achieved coverage from different proposals that was 6.67 percentage points higher. The absolute value of simultaneous coverage for both targets remained at 12.71%. ## Data and Models

Regarding the IIRC inquirer, after reading an English Wikipedia article with links, they posed a question and selected the link expected to contain the answer. Among the 4,754 official training articles, 7,195 questions were matched to unique character spans. Of these, 7,017 targets fell into phrase candidates restricted to one to eight consecutive word spans. Using the same candidate and non-overlapping selection rules, the form baseline employed signals such as length, relative position, and case. The Qwen group additionally included phrase start-end representations extracted from a single causal forward pass. Inference relied solely on article body text, with held-out targets, links, and questions appended after the prediction sealed snapshot. ## Coverage of unseen articles

| Evaluation of the first five proposals | Formal baseline | Add Qwen | Qwen minus baseline, percentage points with 95% interval |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Single objective, 858 | 28.21% | 37.88% | +9.67 [+5.85, +13.56] |
| The two objectives are covered by two distinct proposals, with 944 pairs. | 6.04% | 12.71% | +6.67 [+3.51, +10.13] |
| The single-target precision boundary, 858 | 21.79% | 31.93% | +10.14 [+6.65, +13.65] |

The coverage threshold for the first and second items is the union of character overlap ≥0.5, where higher values are preferable. A 10,000-pair resampling is performed using the source articles. The formal held-out set comprises 430 articles, with single-target observations derived from 372 of them and paired observations from 216. An independent program reconstructs the targets and pairs from the author's sealed snapshot files, and recalculates the mean, difference, and interval. the maximum difference from the formal scores is 5.55×10⁻¹⁷. The primary metric applies to targets selected from questions posed at the end of paragraphs. The time of first doubt and subsequent resolution require separate observational data. ## Execute and sealed snapshot

Colab L4, Transformers 5.17.0, and FP32 Qwen extracted 5,184 segments totaling 1,464,005 tokens, excluding segment ends, with 12 prefix checks passing. The model's forward and readout cumulative time was 605.11 seconds, with a CUDA peak retention of approximately 8.50 GiB. The full extraction command execution took 667.88 seconds, while the two matching commands took 20.52 seconds. L4 has been confirmed released. The main run allocation, confirmed from assignment to submission of stop request, was 739.67 seconds, during which two additional account activity assignments occurred, reducing the total balance from 758.18 to 756.88 CU. The previously pre-checked list verification encountered a type error, and that resource has also been released.
