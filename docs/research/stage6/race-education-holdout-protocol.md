# The educational attainment of English open-book responses, held-out specification

Fitted to the OneStopQA responses from the same Prolific experiment, we test whether education improves the four-choice probability across different articles and different reader keys in the RACE trial. Each dataset contains 1,940 responses from the same group of 970 readers. RACE comprises 216 articles. The `education` public data are identical across two corpora for the same reader: 406 with a university degree, 276 with a high school diploma, 256 with a higher degree, and 32 with no data. These classifications describe education level. the original data do not include pre-knowledge tests per article. [Original study §4.3](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.507.pdf#page=6) · [Existing RACE split and causal features](../stage-6-race-run.md)

Using the original `submission_ind` as the reader key, stratify according to the aforementioned four categories. Within each stratum, sort the UTF-8 string `race-education-v1:` concatenated with the reader key by SHA-256 hash, then split into five folds by taking the remainder of the index divided by five. For fold i, fit the OneStopQA trial using the remaining four folds of readers, and predict the RACE trial for fold i. Reconstruct the prompt hash using the fixed original corpus text, question, option order, and source code. Sum the OneStopQA sealed snapshot counts and correct answer positions. the RACE trial must adhere to the material ordering of the sealed snapshot. The existing L4 FP32 causal states are linked solely by prompt ID. The full-prompt logits from the original five material folds of the sealed snapshot are re-emitted as average predicted probabilities against the existing RACE reference, serving only for alignment verification and reference. The original reference shares training and test readers. all contrasts in this study are re-fit to maintain reader stratification.

For each fold, we first adopt the L2 objective from the sealed snapshot of OneStopQA, fitting the frozen pre-forward row-L2 normalized states with four linear softmax classes and a penalty of `0.002 / 2 × ||W||²` with four free intercepts. Calibration uses out-of-fold logits from the four internal folds, with each internal fold's coefficient estimated from the remaining three folds. Logits refitted using the original four folds are used to predict external RACE. All learning relies solely on the corresponding OneStopQA trials. The readout is fixed as follows.

- Surface models utilize only the `log1p` blank token counts per position and per option, the normalized English proportion of regular words appearing in the stem within options, and the proportion of stem-appearing words in options. The three surface softmax slopes are shared across positions with a fixed identical L2 regularization, while the four position intercepts are free. Additionally, the uncalibrated readout is reported with a refit.
- The empty background readout calibrates text logits with `softmax(t*z+b)`, where `t` is constrained to `[0,4]`, and the final `b` is fixed to 0. The fitting objective is the average NLL at selected positions plus `0.01/2 × ((t−1)²+||b||²)`. This distribution is directly adopted to represent the empty background.
- The additive background is fitted separately for each known education group with a position offset of `b_g`, keeping `t` fixed. The interaction background is fitted separately for the `t_g` of that education group, with the same range. Both are fitted to the same target using the group's OneStopQA calibration trial, with the center of the penalty being the collapsed background parameter. Education blank trials follow the blank background distribution. Readers keys are used solely for data grouping. Correct answers are used only for scoring and are not provided to the aforementioned models.

sealed snapshot RACE prediction hash re-scoring. Primary metric is the NLL of selected positions across 1,940 RACE trials with equal weighting, with improvement measured as empty background minus interactive background. Perform 10,000 independent resamples of articles and readers, seed `771304`, where the product of the two-group sampling frequencies serves as the trial weight. Apply the same pairing method for weighting and fixed prediction in each iteration. Support an incremental value of the background if the lower bound of the two-sided 95% confidence interval exceeds zero. Additionally report the interactive additive effect, observed education subgroup, group-wise and correct/incorrect Brier scores for interpretation. This endpoint represents the probability of selection when the subject can simultaneously view the passage, question, and options. It can serve as an experimental comparison of education levels in English comprehension responses, while eye-tracking, mid-task confusion locations, readers' actual prior concepts, and individual cognitive load for general texts require independent endpoints.
