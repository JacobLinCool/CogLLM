# # First-Day Belief Shift Acceptance in the Anglin Study 1

This round of evaluation assesses whether CogLLM, after joint training, can replicate cross-study effects of the same proposition's group belief change following two evidence readings. Anglin's evidence material family was held out as a whole. Conditions, directions, and Table 1 from prior literature can be used to pre-specify comparisons. Model weights were selected from development data, and individual records were only submitted to the scoring program after a sealed snapshot at the three reading stops. [Original Paper Study 1](https://doi.org/10.1525/collabra.133914) · [Whole sealed snapshot](holdout-plan.md)

## Materials and reading stop

Eight text passages each correspond to a randomly assigned process. Two studies yielded results supporting and opposing the deterrent effect, with two presentation orders. The first-day questionnaire forms the visible instructions, followed by study summaries, attention items, methods, four sets of critiques and rebuttals, evaluation questions, and belief options. Table values, labels, legends, and result explanations from four slit-screen diagrams were transcribed from a fixed PDF to their original positions. Layout spacing was merged, internal Quatrics timers, print interfaces, and contact information prompts were removed. The model viewed the text transcript only, without visual layouts of the diagrams or individual pre-existing positions, ratings, or open-ended responses. [First-day questionnaire](https://osf.io/download/juam5/) · [Author analysis code](https://osf.io/download/zwfqc/) · [Data and CC BY 4.0 license](https://osf.io/gfp48/)

Readout points T1, T2, and T3 correspond to the eight options of the same proposition in the original questionnaire. T1 is before the first evidence, T2 is after the first study and evaluation prompt, and T3 is after the second. Each process proceeds forward once, reading from the same causal representation at three points. each reading stop must pass through separate prefix splitting and independent suffix causal verification. The complete process involves approximately 3,200 tokens, with Anglin's dedicated limit set to 4,096, while the original Qwen configuration supports 262,144. This limit applies only to Anglin, and all materials are fully retained. [Fixed Qwen configuration](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-2B/blob/15852e8c16360a2fea060d615a32b45270f8a8fc/config.json) · [Preparation and scoring program](../../../research/union_training/formal_anglin.py)

## Readout and scoring

Apply the trained Rodger policy effect to paired readouts and back-standardize to the original scale of the training sample. T1 is uniformly the pre-read readout, while T2 and T3 serve as post-read readouts relative to T1 using the same paired offset. Project onto an 8 equally spaced categories using a 0–100 Gaussian marginal. Set the category center to `100 × (k−1) / 7`, with the midpoint between neighboring centers as the boundary, inclusive of tail probabilities at both ends. Compute within-group mean differences using distribution expectations from 1–8. Connect Rodger's 0–100 slider to Anglin's eight-point scale under this fixed assumption, without using Anglin responses for calibration. [Rodger paired supervision](../../../research/union_training/paired_observations.py) · [Anglin scale and timepoint](../anglin-study1-audit.md)

Incorporating the 312 participants from the original paper's Table 1, weighted by their eight actual presentation trajectories. The primary comparisons are the T3−T1 differences (negative for two oppositions, positive for two supports) across the two consistent conditions, and the T2−T1 and T3−T2 differences (varying by evidence direction) for each of the two mixed conditions. For each comparison, we compute the difference between the model and the paired mean difference of the same group, resampling participants within each presentation trajectory 10,000 times, and reporting the two-sided 95% percentile intervals. We report whether the direction of each comparison aligns and whether the interval fully falls within the pre-specified practical tolerance of ±0.5 points. A comparison passes this six-item validation only if it satisfies all six directions and tolerances. The T4 score the following day is used solely to reconstruct the inclusion criteria in Table 1. Scoring ranges are limited to the initial belief, confidence, and post-delay changes, which are reserved for models with corresponding scales and time points.
