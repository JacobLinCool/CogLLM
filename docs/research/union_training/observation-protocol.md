# Joint readout training on human observations

This experiment maps 24 previously integrated sources to 62 training blocks under the original studies' observation rules. The source tables record stimuli, visible content, response scales, denominators, scoring positions, eligible shared projections, and original references. The original observation determines where and through which output a gradient flows. ## Data and shared states

We retain the registered development groups and formal holdouts. The packet reconstructs the source hashes and Qwen token boundaries recorded in past receipts. Only development materials and their observations go to Colab. Frozen Qwen3.5-2B runs one causal forward pass per unique input. Six shared projections use the same hidden states to support processing, lexical, interpretation, retrieval, belief, and subjective appraisal endpoints. Each measurement head has access only to its registered projections and coordinates. Training pathways define these projections. Their relationship to human states still requires validation. ## Observation likelihoods

- **Sentence ratings.** We fit ordinal ratings with cumulative logistic probabilities. PLABA retrospective sentence ratings use the states at the ends of both the target sentence and the document. The issue labels use each actually observed set of boxes checked. An empty set is an observed response with no flags.
- **Lexical ratings and span selections.** We use the state at the target word or selection location and, where required by the study, the end of the visible content. SCARECROW models selection and subsequent ordinal severity as one distribution. Its composite likelihood weights overlapping word votes by material family.
- **First events.** Frick's actual sentence-end exposures define the risk set. The probability of the first inconsistency keypress combines the current hazard and prior survival. We censor readers who have yet to report at the last exposure. Sentence judgments after the first report have a separate conditional distribution. The exposure grid is sentence-level, with within-sentence token reaction timing unmeasured.
- **Comprehension choices.** OneStopQA and RACE fit the option a human selects once and derive correctness from the answer key. Published correctness-only endpoints use binary distributions over the released observations. The OneStop rereading block forecasts correctness from the visible passage prefix and marginalizes over question assignment.
- **Paired responses.** Rodger's pretest and post-test correspond to the baseline and evidence endpoints for the same input. We fit the two ratings and their covariance jointly. The paired predictions yield the difference. The Turkish comparison retains both sentence ends and the display order. Choice depends on the relative scores at those locations and a display-order bias.
- **Continuous variables and study aggregates.** We use original scales or the logarithmic units used by the study. When only count, mean, and variance were published, we use them as sufficient statistics for a Gaussian likelihood. CLEAR uses a marginal observation model with published standard errors. Covariance across CLEAR document scores is unavailable. We use contrasts recomputed on fitted training responses for diagnostics.

Some sources provide only marginal data. For others, this packet lacks complete within-reader sequences. For Qiao, we use marginal likelihoods for repeated ratings at each verified evidence stop. For Oliver, the reveal and recall endpoints retain known valid response paths and each stage's timing. The available observations support human endpoints at different time points and scales. Validating the onset and resolution of the same spontaneous issue in a text requires linked human observations.

## Joint optimization and execution

We train one model by joint likelihood. Each epoch processes every training row, averaging first within an observation block using its actual response denominator, then equally across blocks within a source, and finally equally across sources. This defines the training distribution across heterogeneous reading responses. We estimate continuous-scale means and variances from training blocks alone. The L4 budget is 12 epochs, with the best checkpoint selected on the material-grouped development data. We record observation losses and gradient magnitudes at every epoch and flag a selected checkpoint at the final epoch as budget-limited. The complete formal holdout stays separately sealed throughout. [Joint training code](../../../research/union_training/fit_observations.py) · [Colab entry point](../../../research/union_training/colab_observations.py)

Before extracting the production model representations, we verify the training paths on all observations using synthetic representations. The checks cover coordinates, numerical differentiability, probability normalization, and the designated derived fields. Production results also record the model version, number of stops, causal checks, hardware, predictions, and hash receipts.
