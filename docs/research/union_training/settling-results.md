# Full joint training fine-tuning and whole held-out validation

Frozen Qwen3.5-2B completed full observation joint fine-tuning, sharing 24 public human datasets, 62 observation blocks, and 164,220 causal reading stops. Early stopping based on the pre-specified development loss criterion was reached at round 55, while round 31 yielded the lowest validation score and was selected as the sole formal held-out candidate. ## development set early stopping and replay

Restarting AdamW momentum from round 12, continuing for 43 rounds and 6,106 updates. The source family equal weighting development NLL was 1.19914 at round 12, 1.17078 at round 31, a decrease of 0.02836. Learning rate was reduced according to schedule after evaluation at rounds 39 and 47. The last eight rounds did not meet the 0.001 improvement threshold, triggering early stopping at round 55. The score at round 55 was 1.20351, and the best weights from round 31 were formally retained as the candidate. This represents an early stopping decision under a fixed development set and learning rate schedule. [ sealed snapshot rules ](../../../research/results/union-observations-settled-l4/policy.json) · [ full history ](../../../research/results/union-observations-settled-l4/fitting.json)

The independent program recomputed training and development predictions for 169,025 instances across 57 development blocks, and validated two decisions regarding learning rate scheduling and early stopping. Compared to round 12, the development NLL decreased for 52 blocks and increased for 5. The CompLex vocabulary complexity NLL rose from 1.1730 to 1.5985. Subsequent position evaluation corrected the misaligned Frick pairing of the 13th sentence to the 14th sentence used in the original study. After correction, the mean standardized absolute error for 36 development pair effects decreased from 0.11943 to 0.11827. ## The entire human held-out set

First, fix the weights at round 31 and the prediction specifications for each study, then perform a causal forward pass on each of the 164 held-out stimuli and 216 Paape calibration stimuli. Each of the three material sets contains 12 causal prefix checks. Formal responses are retained locally. each study's predictions are scored only after the sealed snapshot. This L4 has been verified and released. [Materials, reading stop, and release manifest](../../../research/results/union-observations-formal-l4/extraction-verification.json)

Round 31 candidates could not consistently replicate human responses on the held-out set. MECO represents discourse-level group accuracy, Anglin assesses first-day belief following text transcription, and SAP's two reading modes evaluate reading time and eye movements at disambiguation points and subsequent words. Anglin's confidence and next-day responses, as well as SAP's final interpretation, lack corresponding readout data and are left unassessed. These results are currently insufficient to validate the per-token cognitive load curve for arbitrary texts.
