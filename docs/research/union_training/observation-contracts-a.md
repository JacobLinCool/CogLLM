# Human observation and joint training specifications (first 12 groups)

For each group, the visible content in the study, the target under evaluation, and the report time point are fixed before determining the probability model. The shared signals in the table follow local processing requirements and the preceding interpretation state. Backreading, review ratings, and vocabulary extraction are explained by their respective observation heads. To estimate when the same query achieves resolution, it is further necessary to link the reader, the query, and repeated observations of reading stops. [Observation Model](../observation-model.md)

The precision routing of this round is determined by endpoint registration. CompLex and CWI read the annotated target end and the actual reconstructible full-text end. Frick uses the original sentence-by-sentence path, while Blott, Frick, and Oliver use the marginal time likelihood of each observation stage for their positive time. The complete reader dependency across stages is retained in the measurement specifications of each study. [Endpoint registration for this round](../../../research/union_training/observation_registry.py) · [Execution specifications for this round](observation-protocol.md)

## 1. AmbiStory

- **Observation Position**: Readers read the entire specified version and rate the plausibility of the story for the specified sense. The three versions include a short version ending with a common ambiguous sentence and two versions with endings. Each entry is the rating of the story at the end of the sense, along with the `nonsensical` flag for same-vote. The release does not provide a connected sequence of the same readers across the three versions. [Original study §§3.2–4.2](https://aclanthology.org/2025.starsem-1.12/) · [Material Alignment](../stage6/ambistory-context-update.md)
- **Loss and state.** Compute a five-level ordinal likelihood at the end of the story for the specified sense. With the agreement flag, use `P(rating) × P(flag | rating)` or a ten-class joint distribution. Estimate the group-level effect of the ending from rating distributions across versions. This can update the compatibility of the specified sense at the end of the story, and the relationship between its earlier occurrence and later support. The position of first detection or reinterpretation remains unobserved. [Original release](https://github.com/Janosch-Gehring/ambistory/tree/705f1a3784b62f1aa42435b9e107dba901547441)

## 2. Blott 2021、2023

- **Observation location** 2021 full-sentence display and final judgment `Sense`. In 2023, this batch used full-text presentation conditions, with 4.5 seconds of full-sentence display followed by a binary choice button. The millisecond values for 2023 were sourced from the response screen. In 2021, the original study had each reader view only one version of each sentence frame. In 2023, the same reader could rate both versions of the same sentence frame across different blocks. [2021 original study and methods](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10101418/1/BlottRoddFerreiraWarren_JEPLMC_2020_AcceptedManuscript.pdf) · [2023 full-text presentation conditions](https://doi.org/10.1080/23273798.2022.2159992) · [original denominator and timepoint](../semantic_tracking/blott-format-source.md)
- **Loss and State** At the end of each sentence, evaluate `Sense` Bernoulli likelihood. Positive reaction times in 2023 can be explained by a log-time distribution conditioned on keypress. Keypress and time within the same trial remain dependent. It updates end-sentence interpretation and coherence, while 2023's time updates the keypress decision endpoint. Stimulus provides disambiguation cues at position. timing of end-sentence reanalysis still requires word-region observation. [2021 denominator check](../stage6/blott-coherence-holdout.md)

## 3. CLEAR

- **Observation Position** The teacher compared two complete excerpts and selected the one expected to be easier for students to understand. The public workbook retained the relative Bradley–Terry ease coefficient and standard error for each excerpt, without raw comparison edges, raters, or sentence judgments. [Original Study Study 1](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/s13428-022-01802-x) · [Workbook Field Verification](../stage6/clear-reading-ease-source.md)
- **Loss and Status** After excerpting or full discourse aggregation, fit discourse ease using coefficient sampling approximation. Use published standard error squared plus estimated residual variance. Coefficient covariance is unpublished. this constraint must be retained when handling standard error via marginal likelihood. Updated is the teacher's relative ease rating of the article. Student actual comprehension and intra-sentence difficulty loci require other data. [Fixed Author Workbook](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scrosseye/CLEAR-Corpus/dbf1cf0df30bf6be8b0691d2c078b75043ca6199/CLEAR_corpus_final.xlsx)

## 4. CompLex

- **Observation Position** The annotator reads the entire sentence and judges the lexical complexity of a specified word or phrase. The observation coordinate includes the start and end of the target span and the sentence end. The accessed value is the average of five-level responses mapped to 0–1, with one public value per target. [Original Study §3.1](https://aclanthology.org/2021.semeval-1.1.pdf#page=3) · [Fixed Data Version](https://github.com/MMU-TDMLab/CompLex/tree/4b31c2bd34da4c1265f9db3d727d95ee23099f14)
- **Loss and State** Estimate average vocabulary difficulty using target span and visible full sentence. use average observed likelihood for continuous values. When raw counts or variance are missing, use the average working likelihood. Ordered distributions require ordered counting. Update vocabulary judgments and context support, retaining the condition that annotators may use target words followed by subsequent text. [Original Target and Candidate Input](../stage-5-complex-run.md)

## 5. Context Readback

- **Observation Position** Paape and Vasishth manipulated the number of reference objects in the preceding text and the subsequent disambiguation construction. Bilingual self-paced reading events belong to the current display segment. The risk set for entering the first reread is entered only when the first forward pass is reached, the preceding segment has been seen, no backward pass has yet occurred, and the next action is valid. Following the original geometric approach, each segment is terminated at its end, inclusive of the disambiguation and the immediately following segment. [Original Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jml.2026.104748) · [Events and Stimuli Files](https://osf.io/6uv37/) · [Risk Set and Effect](../semantic_tracking/context-readback-transfer.md)
- **Loss and State**: In valid segments, the first backward look-ahead conditional binary likelihood. If there is a per-trial path, the distribution of the first event is derived from the hazard rates and survival functions of each effective readout stop. The previously manipulated conditional difference is recalculated per construction and actual segment. Updates to readout behavior and local processing under prior-condition adjustment. A single readout gesture cannot specify which interrogative has been resolved.

## 6. Currie

- **Observation Position**: Sixth-grade students read eight-sentence English narratives or expository texts sentence-by-sentence, reporting only at the end whether the passage makes sense. Correct responses for contradictory texts equate to reporting contradiction, while for consistent texts, the correct response is the opposite of reporting contradiction. Near/far conditions are matched by genre and topic. Each reader views only one version per topic. [Original Study Measures and procedure](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7986958/) · [Released Materials](https://osf.io/sj28g/) · [Original Responses and Alignment](../semantic_tracking/larrc-consistency-source.md)
- **Loss and State** At the end of the eighth sentence, report the Bernoulli likelihood of an explicit contradiction, retaining the reader and material dependency. When comparing near and far conditions, compute the expected difference in reports from the same distribution. Update consistency judgment and context integration at the end of the section. The coordinates of the original contradictory sentence can be used to design a check. however, whether the reader has detected it in that sentence remains unobserved.

## 7. CWI 2018

- **Observation Position**: Annotators view the entire five-to-ten sentence HIT and circle target spans that may hinder comprehension. The current WikiNews corpus load reconstructs complete HITs with the same source family in sequential order, preserving target start and end positions. There are ten native and ten non-native annotators per group. The original interface layout was not included in the reconstructed corpus. [Original Study §3.1](https://aclanthology.org/W18-0507.pdf#page=2) · [Construction and Original Fields](../../../research/core_features/background_prepare.py)
- **Loss and State** For targets with valid denominators, use binomial likelihood separately for the two groups. The target and visible HIT are jointly used to update the selection head. Votes within the same HIT may be correlated. precision evaluation is performed per HIT or article family. Published binary labels and synthetic probabilities can both be derived from the same vote count. Update the context difficulty and group calibration for target words or phrases. The selected position marks the referenced content.

## 8. Czech Garden Path

- **Observation position.** Experiment 2 displays the full sentence simultaneously. In Experiment 3, each keypress reveals one word and masks the preceding word. After each sentence, one question probes the actual object, the initial misinterpretation, or the possession relation. Answers are no, yes, or an explicit don't know. The data record no missing answers. The Experiment 3 source also gives word-by-word reading times. Some repeated copies of the sentence-final answer differ across word rows. Use the authors' analysis rule to select one answer per trial. [Original Experiments 2 and 3](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0288817) · [Source data and word alignment](../semantic_tracking/czech-garden-path-source.md)
- **Loss and State** At the end of the sentence, present three similar but fitted answer distributions according to the presentation mode and actual question type. Each trial contains only one question. the three question types cannot be merged into a single reader's joint interpretation label. Update the end-of-sentence word interpretation and misinterpretation retention. If raw word-by-word time is appended, update the local processing with the likelihood of each presentation segment's time. The disambiguation target's position is a material property.

## 9. First Readback

- **Observation Position** In English discourse and German materials, bidirectional self-paced reading was employed, where the original display unit may contain multiple words. The first forward view of a segment, provided the back-read button has not yet been pressed, constitutes the risk opportunity for the next valid keypress to form the first back-read. English also includes an end-of-trial acceptability judgment. [English Original Study](https://doi.org/10.5070/g6011182) · [German Original Study](https://doi.org/10.5070/G601139) · [Actual Display Segments and Risk Sets](../semantic_tracking/first-readback-transfer.md)
- **Loss and State**: At the end of the original segment, use the first readout condition's binary likelihood. The risk set excludes actions following the first readout. remaining actions are only meaningful when applied to independent readout endpoints. Observed end-of-document acceptability is additionally fitted with a Bernoulli distribution, dependent on the readout retention for that trial. Updates are local processing and readout behavior, while end-of-document judgment updates the end-of-document evaluation. Re-analysis of identical keypress records provides only a single readout observation.

## 10. Frick

- **Observation position.** Experiment 1 displays one complete sentence at a time. Each keypress asks whether the current sentence agrees with prior text. The first sentence requires `K` under the task rules, so the response path starts at the second sentence. The two text-only arms differ in prior context and use the same target sentence. The public spreadsheets and existing processing can link each reader to their entire response path. [Original Experiment 1](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13421-026-01900-7) · [Original response paths and timing](../semantic_tracking/frick-consistency-source.md)
- **Loss and State**: A path decomposes into "the first sentence occurrence `D` or not reported until the end" and "the keypress following the first occurrence `D`". The first event uses the reading stop hazard rate and survival function to generate probabilities, while subsequent keypresses are fitted conditioned on the already occurring `D`. If positive keypress times are retained, the same observation's conditional time distribution is also used. The marginal of the first sentence occurrence `D` is the first event distribution. The consistency report of the current sentence with the preceding text is updated, and `D` after which `K` only evaluates new sentences. [Original path reconstruction in the program](../../../research/union_training/path_observations.py) · [Endpoint constraints for contradiction resolution](../semantic_tracking/frick-resolution-negative-control.md)

## 11. Kerwer

- **Observation Position** Readers read the full randomized English abstract version and rate comprehension ease on a 1–8 scale, and confusion/frustration on a 1–5 scale. Each reader views only one arm within each source family, though different arms may be viewed across topics. The published text can reconstruct versions with or without the small heading, but the original questionnaire layout was not published. [Original study Method, Table 1](https://doi.org/10.1525/collabra.18898) · [Author publication](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/A9QSY) · [Reconstruction rules](../stage6/kerwer-article-holdout.md)
- **Loss and State**: The three ratings are ranked by ordinal likelihood at the end of each abstract. Ratings within the same reader and trial are retained as dependent. when possible, they are modeled using joint or conditional distributions. Version differences are recalculated by re-reading the same abstract. Conditioning on observed background conditions is performed only if a pre-test reading is available for that study. Updating discourse comprehensibility and end-of-abstract sentiment evaluation.

## 12. Oliver

- **Observation Position**: Upon reading the definition, first select KNOW, TOT, or DON'T KNOW. Only initial TOT and DON'T KNOW proceed to a wait process of up to 15 seconds to switch to KNOW, followed by the reveal of the target word and confirmation. After five minutes, a retest may use the same or an alternative definition, having already undergone the initial reveal. The wait and reveal process contains no new paragraph token coordinates. The public data key is self-report, with no pre-reveal oral transcripts. [Original Study §§3.2.2, 4](https://doi.org/10.1525/collabra.200) · [Author Public Data v3](https://doi.org/10.5683/SP2/SP6BYO) · [Branch and Definition Coordinates](../semantic_tracking/oliver_retrieval_protocol.md)
- **Loss and State**: From the definition, estimate the initial joint path at the end, decomposed into the first keystroke, the report within 15 seconds of applicable branches, and post-reveal confirmation. When the positive wait time is used, the 15-second no-change is the right upper bound of the waiting stage, and the initial KNOW is structurally unmanifested. Retesting must retain the revealed and actual versions and align with the initial test based on reader and item linkage. The updated components are vocabulary extraction, report accessibility, and post-reveal confirmation. Its timeline is the trial stage and milliseconds.
