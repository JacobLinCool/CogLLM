# Full Joint Training and L4 Resume Verification

The full joint training run completed using 62 observation blocks from 24 human data sources, with 27,167 inputs and 164,220 observation stops. We saved all 18 batches of frozen Qwen3.5-2B representations locally. Before training, we merged them in source order and verified that each document and observation stop appeared exactly once. Release was confirmed for all three Colab L4 instances. [Batching and resumption protocol](observation-resume.md) · [Full-run verification](../../../research/results/union-observations-full-l4/run-verification.json)

## Recovery during the run

After the first L4 returned batch 1, the controller passed the wrong list type to the bundle validator, which caused the session to be released early. After correcting the call, we reconstructed batch 1 from the 6 downloaded shards and verified its 9,531 observation stops. The second L4 extracted batches 2 through 15 and disconnected while packaging batch 16. Once the controller confirmed that the second L4 had been released, it provisioned a third instance automatically and extracted only batches 16 through 18. We restored the first 15 batches from local storage before merging and training. Rerunning the resume command on the completed run directory verified the archived results directly. All 18 final batches passed SHA-256 verification. Across 206 prefix checks, the largest relative root-mean-square difference was 0.00000870 against a threshold of 0.001. ## Joint training

With the backbone frozen, we trained for 12 epochs and made 1,704 readout updates. Each epoch used 135,819 training observation rows. An independent verifier checked 169,025 training and development predictions and recomputed losses for 57 development blocks and scores for 36 existing paired effects. From epoch 1 to epoch 12, development negative log-likelihood (NLL), weighted equally across sources, fell from 1.32764 to 1.19914. Lower NLL indicates higher average log likelihood for the observed outcomes. The best value occurred at the last scheduled epoch, so this run left convergence unestablished. After realigning two Frick target-sentence effects, the mean standardized absolute error across 36 effects was 0.11943. This unitless score averages the absolute effect errors divided by their corresponding scales. Lower is better, with zero indicating an exact match on every effect. The formal study-level holdout remained locked during this run.
