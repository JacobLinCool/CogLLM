# Vocabulary references in the English learner readout

The CEFR-J Wordlist version 1.5 was compiled by Yukio Tono at Tokyo University of Foreign Studies. Copyright belongs to Tono Laboratory, TUFS. The authors permit research and commercial use with attribution. Word levels A1–B2 in the readout use this source. [CEFR-J](http://www.cefr-j.org/download.html) · [Source release and terms](https://github.com/openlanguageprofiles/olp-en-cefrj/tree/d4e45b75b38f27b30dfc5c44d8c571aec7e7092f)

Levels C1–C2 use the Octanove Vocabulary Profile C1/C2 version 1.0, by Octanove Labs. Octanove releases the profile under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International. The entries under `octanove` in the adapted lookup retain that license. The entries under `cefrj` retain the CEFR-J attribution and terms above. We case-folded each headword, split slash-separated alternatives, kept complete English words, and stored the earliest level for each form and English Snowball stem. The fitted classifier is supplied separately. [Octanove source](https://github.com/openlanguageprofiles/olp-en-cefrj/blob/d4e45b75b38f27b30dfc5c44d8c571aec7e7092f/octanove-vocabulary-profile-c1c2-1.0.csv) · [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

English Zipf frequencies are supplied by the installed `wordfreq` 3.1.1 library. Its frequency tables are separate from this bundle. The word judgments came from Paetzold and Specia's first user study. The fitted bundle contains aggregate source stem counts and model coefficients. [Frequency resources](https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq) · [Original word study](https://aclanthology.org/C16-1069/)
