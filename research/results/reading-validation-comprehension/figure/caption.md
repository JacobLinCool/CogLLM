**Predicting judgments about a revision and the answers readers give after reading it.** **a**, In SimpDA 2022, judges rated six revisions of each of 60 English sources on simplicity and meaning preservation. Blue circles show how often each model selected both preferred versions when the panel preferred different drafts on the two dimensions (441 pairs, 59 sources). Orange squares show the corresponding rate when the same version won both judgments (398 pairs, 60 sources). Giving the ReadMe++ ease ordering to both questions forces it to choose a single winner. Whiskers resample the responding judges and source paragraphs. **b**, In the separate ATS comprehension study, readers answered three questions after seeing one version of a OneStopQA paragraph. Within each question we compared two revisions whose observed answers differed. Blue circles show the percentage for which a passage-only method ranked the version with the correct answer higher. Orange squares show how often it ranked the version where a reader supplied an answer above the version where a reader indicated insufficient information. An answer given can be incorrect. Comparisons include 3,281 pairs over 176 questions for correctness and 2,517 pairs over 157 questions for availability, from 30 articles. The dashed line indicates 50% pairwise agreement. Whiskers are 95% article-bootstrap intervals. The two panels score different tasks.
