**Reader ratings and comprehension on expert-written science summaries.** Panel a shows how often source-trained PLABA sentence ease or the investigators' four-formula grade orders two summaries in the same direction as the same reader's responses. The numbers of unequal pairs from top to bottom are 85, 75, 81, 90 and 103. Panel b holds the summary fixed and compares responses from two readers with independently reported term familiarity, elicited while viewing the original abstract and before seeing the summary. The counts of unequal pairs are 54, 61, 55, 64 and 51. The dashed line represents chance agreement. Points are equal-weighted averages within reader in panel a and within article in panel b. Bars are 95% percentile intervals over 10,000 crossed reader and article bootstrap samples. In total 49 readers saw three summaries each from 89 distinct articles.
