**Text predicts some of the wording that readers find difficult, while a reader’s measured prior improves prediction of scientific term familiarity.** **a**, Within the same researcher and abstract, each model ranks two designated terms by the researcher’s postreading familiarity. Blue points summarize 10,694 unequal pairs from 83 abstracts and 11 researchers. Orange points show a secondary comparison restricted to 1,838 pairs that had the same prereading rating and different postreading ratings (81 abstracts, 11 researchers). The dashed line marks 50% concordance. The reader’s own prereading rating ties by design on every matched pair. The word-form baseline combines length, position and frequency. The local phrase head was trained on reader-named biomedical phrases, and the lexical head was trained on CompLex difficulty ratings. **b**, Recovery of 94 expressions that PLABA readers named when the sentence is supplied. A hit requires a character intersection-over-union of at least 0.5. The persistent unresolved level, word-form baseline, CompLex lexical head and supervised local phrase head use the same candidate spans. Blue and orange points show the top one and top five nonoverlapping proposals. Whiskers in both panels are 95% bootstrap intervals from 10,000 draws. Panel **a** resamples abstracts and researchers. Panel **b** resamples 12 reader–article connected groups and uses out-of-group predictions on previously examined study material.
