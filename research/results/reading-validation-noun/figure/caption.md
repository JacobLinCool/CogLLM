**A structural edit can change perceived difficulty and cloze accuracy in opposite directions.** Each point is the mean within-family change after splitting a designated noun phrase (blue circles) or replacing two nouns with pseudowords (orange squares) in 90 families of English health-information sentences. **a**, The mean change in rated difficulty on the five-point human scale. **b**, The mean change in four-noun cloze errors, in percentage points. Whiskers give 95% intervals from 10,000 bootstrap samples of families within phrase-length strata. Model intervals condition on the fitted fold predictors; they exclude refitting uncertainty. People denotes the released human means. CogLLM and Surface are predictions for previously unseen families from the full text and surface-only heads. Condition supplies the experimental labels. The zero line indicates equal outcomes for the two versions. The four-word pseudoword condition in the public file has identical outcomes between split and unsplit versions for every family. The companion report includes an independent refit excluding the affected stratum and describes additional source limitations.
