# Source calibration for a low-volume editorial alert

The default causal head emphasizes coverage. In a separate experiment, target at least 70% word-level precision for a small set of character-introduction alerts. The earlier source threshold search on 11 titles and the full-movie screening results are already known. This design trades training titles for independent calibration titles and does not change the default writer bundle.

Within the original 44 training titles, sort by SHA-256 of the fixed prefix “editor-selective-v1/” plus original group identifier. Train a fresh 128-feature ambiguity-aware Qwen readout on the first 25 titles. Reserve the other 19 and all 11 original development titles exclusively for calibration. Compute feature moments on the 25 fitting titles. Use the registered marginal loss, title weights and .01 slope penalty. The article remains a single causal pass. At inference, a word can be flagged only if it begins with an uppercase letter and its case-folded whole word has not appeared earlier in the document. Both conditions are observable at the current word.

Consider fixed model-score cutoffs .10 to .95 by .05. For each cutoff compute the minimum attainable true alerts within every calibration title over every compatible placement of each human span. Aggregate the numerator over the total number of alerts. Require at least 20 source alerts across at least five calibration titles. Compute one simultaneous lower bound by subtracting the 95th percentile of the maximum downward bootstrap deviation over all eligible thresholds, resampling complete titles 10,000 times. Among thresholds reaching a lower bound of .70, choose the one covering the highest number of guaranteed true alerts. If none qualifies, declare that the source cannot support the requested precision and make no selective recommendation. This is a corpus-sampling bootstrap and its bound depends on comparable calibration and deployment populations.

Evaluate any qualifying threshold once on all 39 previously studied movies. Report partial-identification precision and recall ranges, alert count, per-1,000-word burden, movie coverage, and movie-bootstrap uncertainty incorporating location uncertainty. This movie check is retrospective. The independent calibration rule has a separate fitted head; the source-screening threshold of the released writer remains part of its own experiment.
