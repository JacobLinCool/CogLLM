# Which scientific reviewer concern does an author's revision address?

Readers of scientific papers often report concrete gaps in the explanation. ARIES links authentic reviewer comments to paper edits and includes manually checked positive and negative alignments. We will assess whether the question-specific evidence readout, already trained on QASPER, IIRC and ExpMRC, can assign a revision to the appropriate earlier comment. A double-matched pair uses two comments on the same paper, two distinct revised passages and the manually judged cross-negatives. These observations identify which comments an edit addresses. They leave the time of a reader's realization and whether the reviewer was satisfied unobserved. [Study](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.377/) · [Data and ODC-BY license](https://github.com/allenai/aries#dataset)

## Source and construction

We reserve the complete official ARIES manually annotated test split for evaluation. Synthetic train and development alignments play no role. We take the comments' original target text; omit author replies, reviewer identities, editor IDs, and article metadata from the model. Each example first displays two comments in a fixed hash order and then one original or revised passage. The two comments and their order are identical across the pair of revisions and both source versions. Edited passages concatenate the target section's marked paragraphs in their published order. We omit empty revised passages and multi-paragraph noncontiguous patches. Candidate pairs require two distinct human-positive edits such that each edit is explicitly marked negative for the rival comment. Use at most eight contrast pairs per paper by SHA-256 rank with one pair per unordered pair of comments. We exclude comments longer than 200 model tokens, passages exceeding 600 model tokens or complete inputs over 1,024 tokens. Syntax, reuse rights, source versions and exclusions will be recorded. Only statistical summaries, hashes, and code will be released here.

## Locked decisions

The model encodes each displayed sequence once with a causal mask. The hazard for each earlier comment is the locked readout from the previous QASPER study. The primary score ranks the correct concern against the competing concern on both revised passages. Divide each passage's integrated softplus hazard by its number of visible tokens when comparing revision to source. Within a passage the normalization preserves the ranking. Secondary outcomes report the change in correct-minus-rival evidence from source to revision on groups with both original paragraphs available, and the mean correct-minus-rival score on passages consisting entirely of additions. A zero-length original has no estimate and is excluded from the version comparison. A nonzero edit alone leaves the corresponding reader response unknown. Compare on identical cases against the trained lexical-only and released joint readouts, a position comparator, and content-word BM25. Bootstrap entire papers on 10,000 paired draws, reporting interval width and all denominators. An incremental scientific improvement requires positive lower confidence bounds against both pretrained joint and lexical readouts on the primary endpoint. If the data contain too few double-matched papers, publish the supported denominator and report a secondary single-target comparison where the labeled positive has a same-paper explicitly negative rival comment.

Weights, thresholds and source inclusion rules precede score access. Deterministic code will verify citation/paragraph alignment against the authors' loader. This test addresses high-level reader feedback on scientific writing; elicitation occurs after review and the identity of the two reviewer requests is given.
