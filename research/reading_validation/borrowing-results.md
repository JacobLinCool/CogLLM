# Separately calibrated borrowing across 24 human studies

The relation between two kinds of response can be estimated without forcing them into a single target scale. We compared a shared set of state projections with study-specific projections, then estimated how strongly each endpoint should combine the two predictive distributions. Both models used the same cached causal Qwen representations, response likelihoods, article families, initial predictions and update order. The original 62 measurements included ten likelihood designs. One article needs one backbone pass. All borrowing takes place in the observation-specific readouts. Values are held negative log likelihood per observed trial in source-standardized units. Average across endpoints within a study, then equally across the 23 studies with development outcomes. Both seeds improved over complete sharing in 16 of 23 studies; the paired mean reductions were .01162 [.00218, .02754] and .01395 [.00284, .03084] nats. Against source-private fits the reductions were .00368 [−.00168, .00954] and .00232 [−.00227, .00747]. Against fixed equal weighting they were .00172 [−.00028, .00544] and .00222 [−.00010, .00641]. The two seeds use the same human observations and do not give independent study replications. These results support access to study-specific readouts as protection against forced sharing. The more ambitious claim that learning each borrowing weight outperforms a fixed equal mixture across new studies remains uncertain. Of 135,598 original source-training rows, 95,362 fitted expert models, 20,788 selected epochs, and 19,448 fitted borrowing. All 33,208 original development rows were kept separate. The 27,159 exact article-exposure identifiers had disjoint material and partition assignments. The five outcomes in the single-family Qiao study contribute source training and have no external development rows; 57 endpoints were scored. The three densely observed token tasks in the original 65-component experiment were not retrained here. The 48-dimensional archived projection and previously analyzed development partition limit transfer claims. All four experts stopped after independent tuning plateaus. The two shared checkpoints were selected at epochs 196 and 198; the two private ones at epochs 187 and 186. No final checkpoint was selected at the numerical budget.
