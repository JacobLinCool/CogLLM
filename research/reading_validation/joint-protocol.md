# Reading difficulty across populations and measurement scales

We test whether a frozen causal encoder and a difficulty readout can transfer across human studies at the position where a rating is defined. The experiment uses the pinned Qwen3.5-2B checkpoint. We retain the existing issue-tracking readout. Additional readouts receive the final state at the rated position in the same backbone pass. Joining a second prediction to a causal state leaves earlier predictions unchanged.

## Human endpoints and isolation

The new study is the English release of [ReadMe++](https://github.com/tareknaous/readme/tree/b7b6d0808ad2e12c77867a0563ba7b229cbaf911). Three annotators supplied a final CEFR difficulty level per sentence. The paper describes [presentation and voting](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12225862/). Some annotators could consult context. For this experiment the input is the unmodified target sentence in isolation. Predictions concern judged sentence difficulty. The three public workbooks have 2,243 training, 283 validation and 296 test rows before overlap checks. We checked the headers, text and grouping fields during preparation. The test ratings and prediction errors have not been inspected. A separate candidate source, SR-Crowd, did not supply a downloadable archive at the URL in the paper when checked.

We train auxiliary directions on the original training folds of three public studies: English PLABA-EVAL sentence processing ease (five ordered levels, reversed for difficulty), English CLEAR teacher passage ease (continuous, reversed), and German StudentAnnotations self-rated sentence difficulty (five ordered levels). PLABA contributes the state at the displayed sentence end, with earlier article text visible to the model. The original readers could inspect the full document. CLEAR contributes only the passage-end state. Each source retains its response scale and original development split. CLEAR and StudentAnnotations are auxiliary observations with different units and populations.

We seal an outcome-free packet on the GPU. Remove identical whitespace/case-normalized test sentences and repeated full paragraphs from training and validation across conflicting partitions, keeping test membership. Also exclude exact target-text duplicates from auxiliary training when comparing to the new data. Publish the overlap accounting and the hash of every input and forecast. The new test ratings are joined locally after all forecasts and development settings are sealed. Isolated sentences and documents in this public release could have appeared in backbone pretraining.

## Estimation and comparisons

Train on one causal state per rated position. Use a surface-only control with word count, word lengths, punctuation, numerals and word frequency. Use the same features alongside frozen Qwen states in the encoder readouts. Use equal total weight per study, training-side scaling, ridge shrinkage and source-specific observation scales. Ordered endpoints fit cumulative threshold probabilities, and CLEAR fits its standardized continuous judgments. Fit an independent endpoint head, a shared slope with endpoint-specific offsets and a convex combination of the two forecasts. Select the combination weight per endpoint from development responses by minimizing its proper scoring rule, allowing the independent model to receive all the weight. The existing issue head receives zero gradients from these ratings.

We will report two different predictions:

1. **Transfer with no ReadMe++ ratings.** Train a shared difficulty direction using only the three auxiliary studies. Measure Spearman correlation with held-out ReadMe++ labels, and paired improvement over the equally trained surface-only direction. The input is the sentence alone. No target-study calibration is available for this prediction.
2. **Joint training with the ReadMe++ training fold.** On the official test sentences, compare the source-adaptive model to a target-only Qwen readout and a target-only surface readout. The primary metric is the ranked probability score (mean squared cumulative-probability error over five cut points, lower is better). Report paired differences, score distributions, rank correlation, mean absolute error on the 1–6 scale, and per-domain estimates where defined. Use paired group bootstrap resampling by domain for 95% intervals. The official split includes domains shared with training. The fully study-held-out ranking test is the first comparison. Report the three existing studies' development results as development diagnostics, including the independently fitted controls. A gain over surface features cannot establish a gain over independent training.

Use a fixed ridge strength of 0.01 per study-average observation loss after feature scaling. Use at most 128 training-only orthogonal components of the final state to bound variance and facilitate reproducible CPU optimization. All model arms get the same text, terminal coordinates and numerical stopping criterion. Archive optimizer residuals, mixture weights, issue-head hash and runtime. Precision checks compare batched backbone extraction with single, unpadded execution before fitting. A zero validation blend, a worse test result, a nonconverged fit, or failure to beat a baseline is a reportable result.

This protocol was written before looking up target test ratings or scoring a new candidate. Other parallel experiments evaluate online transition behavior, attribution, and local word difficulty on different measurements. A sentence difficulty rating cannot adjudicate their token-level timing.
