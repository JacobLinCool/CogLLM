# Direct native word readout for reference failures and independent human ambiguity

Gemma's matched 48-coordinate probes missed the prespecified all-word character advantage. This registered follow-up tests whether its original middle and final coordinates retained information that a projection learned on two earlier tasks discarded. SNaC character and event/object movie results are known. Its separate unclear-coreference annotations (CorefE) and external Phrase Detectives judgements have not been scored in CogLLM. At registration we inspected source schemas, annotation definitions and sizes. [Previous architectures](transition-architecture-results.md) · [Original SNaC taxonomy](https://aclanthology.org/2022.emnlp-main.29.pdf) · [Phrase Detectives](https://github.com/dali-ambiguity/Phrase-Detectives-Corpus-2.1.4)

## One observation, one position

Use the existing SNaC word and book/movie folds. Each endpoint has its own ambiguity mask. An annotator's CharE, RefE, or CorefE string contributes a positive label only to source words it overlaps in one uniquely locatable released segment. A multiply located or missing string invalidates that segment for that endpoint. The three losses observe unclear character introduction, missing event/object reference, and unclear pronoun referent. Train on books only. Compute thresholds at the 95th percentile of negative words on development books. Test once per endpoint on movies at the corresponding threshold and report every direction. Include all eligible words and the prespecified personal-pronoun subset for CorefE. Use existing pretrained English NER/POS plus lexical and native-surprisal controls, using the same article folds. The original NER model can see the rest of the sentence.

For each native word end use the entire RMS/layer-normalized middle and final hidden vectors, centered/scaled using training words only. Scale each block by the square root of 48 divided by its hidden width; the expected squared feature norm is comparable to the matched 48-coordinate block. Keep all original 16 causal lexical controls, native word surprisal, and 15 antecedent controls. Middle and final native coordinates form W; conventional controls use task-specific weights V. Full words and expert documents remain single original-text forwards. No huge native arrays are required outside Colab.

## Source fits

Evaluate on Gemma, with available matched backbone replications labeled as secondary. For each target use a binary cross-entropy averaged within each training book title, then across titles. Average the three task losses equally, with intercepts unpenalized. Standardize other predictors with positive training variance. Set lambda to the existing fixed 0.01. The independent fit uses lambda times the sum of squared coefficients divided by twice the number of tasks. The shared fit replaces W's penalty by lambda times (squared Frobenius norm plus squared nuclear norm) divided by four times the number of tasks. This gives the independent penalty for a single task and favors a small number of jointly learned directions while allowing opposite signs. Use one optimizer and fixed convergence criteria, verify gradients, objective and weights, and publish all failures. No validation or test selects new feature dimensions or weights. Fit the earlier learned pair of middle/final span projections with the same book-balanced single-task objective as an additional within-backbone control. Compare AP from direct independent vs the old projection, direct shared vs independent, and direct shared vs NER/POS. Primary new SNaC statistic is CorefE AP among personal pronouns. Also report character and event word results, as well as whole-word CorefE. Resample whole movies 10,000 times for paired intervals. A positive on one target alone cannot establish joint-training improvement across targets.

## Independent interpretation task

Use the unscored Phrase Detectives 2.1.4 gold partition, with human-authored English fiction, art history and Wikipedia passages that fit the native 2,048-token limit for every compared candidate. Reconstruct the annotated visible token stream with a shared deterministic punctuation/spacing policy and preserve word coordinates. Use single-word personal-pronoun markables from he/him/his/himself, she/her/hers/herself, they/them/their/theirs/themself/themselves, and it/its/itself. Include only first independent reader interpretations (annotation mode a); omit validation and expert clicks. Include unique players once per target and omit a target if that player gives incompatible duplicate original choices. The independently adjudicated, gold CRAC identity chain collapses alternate mentions of the same referent. Source file joins and unmapped targets are counted explicitly.

In the primary external analysis require at least four unique readers choosing a previous entity and a matchable gold entity ID for every such choice. The human target is P(two distinct such readers choose different antecedent entities), computed without replacement from observed counts. Associate this with the book-trained CorefE score by averaging within-document rank concordance over pairs of eligible pronouns with unequal disagreement rates and then over documents. Ties in model score get half credit. Use 10,000 document-cluster bootstrap resamples. Compare shared, independent, earlier projector and lexical/surprisal controls; report pooled Spearman as descriptive. A secondary disagreement measure additionally treats discourse-new, nonreferential and predicative readings as distinct options. Gold-doc membership, score emission and masked response assembly are independent. This recognition task elicits explicit interpretations; its disagreement measures ambiguity and does not directly measure effort.
