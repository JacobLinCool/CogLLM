# Calibration and span generation from the fixed T5 specialist

The selected T5 adapter almost always generated the clear-case label on movies. Greedy-output performance is already known. This diagnostic tests whether that result arises from the default decision rule, a weak learned ranking, or inaccurate localization once an error category is supplied. Keep the completed rank-eight T5 and all 1,840 updates fixed. The comparison is a retrospective repair to the specialist's decision rule and does not replace its prespecified greedy result. [Original comparison](transition-t5-protocol.md) · [Fixed recoverable specialist](transition-t5-adapter-protocol.md)

Take the same exact 716 book-development and 1,661 movie input strings, sentinel, tokenizer, 1,024-token rule and 80-token decoder limit. For each sentence reuse one encoder call. At the start of the decoder compute the total log-probability of the label string “false” versus “true” through its last subword, excluding EOS. Condition these two scores on their sum to obtain a character-error classification score. Independently prefix the decoder with the exact “false character” string and obtain its greedy textual spans under the same decoder limit. Run this constrained prediction for every dev and movie exposure and store the classifier probabilities, generated strings, and visibility of the measured sentence; no target label, word mask or forced gold span is available to either stage. Applying a cutoff later must not change the recorded forecast.

Fit thresholds on the separate original book development observations only. For sentence classification select the cutoff maximizing F1 over all 716 fully measured development sentences. For exact word localization multiply the sentence score by an indicator that the conditional generator named that word, using the published greedy comparison's literal matching rules. Select its cutoff separately by word-level F1 on the same book observations. In both cases use every distinct positive score as a candidate, include predicting none, and break F1 ties in favor of higher thresholds. A predicted word without a matching generated span stays unmarked. Archive the two source decisions and source performance before opening the 39 movie human labels. Keep score ties intact.

Evaluate held target sentences where every original human word can be interpreted and all identifiable words individually, reporting AP/AUROC, precision/recall/F1 at frozen thresholds, raw confusion counts, false positives and source-to-film transfer. Also report whether the supplied target sentence remained visible after the original right truncation. Report forced-span word recall with no classification gate as a localization bound for this decoder's fixed predictions. On genuinely identical coordinates compare the served marginal and direct Qwen word scores, NER plus surprisal and preexisting greedy T5, showing thresholds and units. The forced specialist receives a full target sentence and an explicit character-category prefix; the deployed causal observer marks the text as it is read. Do not interpret the result as measuring the original published full-parameter checkpoint.
