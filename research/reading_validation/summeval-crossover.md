# Does a draft-level signal recover a change in human preference for the same two systems?

We register this diagnostic before opening any SummEval ratings. Holding the news story fixed removes source-story difficulty, yet two systems can differ systematically in their average quality. To measure sensitivity to the content of a particular draft, hold the pair of originating systems fixed as well. Use the unchanged 50 confirmation stories, existing sealed predictions and their three expert coherence grades. The ten future stories remain closed. No new fitting or comparison-system selection.

For systems A and B and stories i and j, form a contrast only when experts favor A over B on i and B over A on j, or vice versa. Require two distinct forecastable wordings within both stories, and a nonzero difference of the two three-expert means in each. Use the actual official three annotations for each system/story row. The observed difference of these within-story preferences has a fixed sign; give half credit to identical predicted differences. An input-free system identity score receives exactly half credit. For every unordered pair of source stories, average the resulting decisions across all eligible pairs of systems and then average across source-story pairs. Recompute means with multiplicities from a bootstrap of 50 whole stories. The same story may occur in many pairs and receives a single multiplicity across all its comparisons. Exclude comparisons of a story with itself after resampling. Report the number of eligible source-story pairs, system pairs and crossing comparisons.

Two simultaneous contrasts assess the paired ballot model. The first compares the preselected article-only form/native mixture to chance. The second compares the preselected mixture to the soft-choice form/surprisal component. Report the absolute three-task shared head, observed independent crowd scores, published G-Eval forecasts and a single independent expert as references. The crowd condition obtains the outcome for a different population at evaluation time. The human reference holds one of the three experts out at each of the four official rows, selects crossing remaining-pair outcomes, and tests which story the isolated expert favors relatively more. To preserve the joint four-cell dependence without treating independent rounds as independent stories, average over the Cartesian 3⁴ draws in each observed 2 × 2 system/story layout, weight each contributing layout equally within the source-story pair and resample stories. Give model and expert agreements for the exact same human draw geometries. Preserve all originally registered analyses. Evidence here concerns a change in judgments across distinct news drafts, independent of fixed story and fixed producer effects.
