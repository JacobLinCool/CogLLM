# How much should a new reading outcome borrow from other studies?

With both observation experts already trained, calibration data from other studies supplied little information about the appropriate sharing weight for a particular outcome. A target-calibrated observer gave the best mean among the original seven methods on two previously analyzed partitions. Its advantage over fixed equal sharing was small. An independently trained model accepted on a third set of materials allowed a more direct test of that finding. ## Which measurements went into each loss?

The archived human responses comprise 62 endpoints from 24 sources. On the two earlier partitions, 57 endpoints from 23 sources had held material. The single-family Qiao source supplied training observations. The observation models retain categorical responses, selected options, ordinal ratings, joint ordinal–binary responses, hurdle ordinal outcomes, ordered choices, first-event paths, grouped Gaussian observations, Gaussian point estimates with known error, and paired Gaussian responses. Phrase judgments, votes, answer choices and time estimates contribute at their measured support. An observation spanning a sentence contributes through that sentence's registered coordinates. The same score cannot identify where an unobserved feeling first appeared between two such coordinates. [Endpoint inventory and likelihoods](../union_training/observation_registry.py) · [Data and split audit](borrowing_data.py)

For each endpoint, two readouts consume the same one-pass Qwen coordinates. The first readout shares state projections across sources, with each permitted projection receiving gradients from its registered outcomes. The second readout learns a separate projection per source while retaining the same output likelihood. They start with identical forecasts. For trial outcome y, the combined density qₐ(y) is proportional to the private density raised to 1 − a times the shared density raised to a. Category probabilities are renormalized on the measured choices. Continuous outcomes retain Gaussian precision, sampling variance and paired covariance as appropriate. Family-specific calibration averages the resulting trial likelihoods within each material before summing materials. A repeated-vote table represents its actual denominator. For the retrospective word-marking endpoint its registered per-material evaluation weighting remains in force. All gains below compare proper losses in the same observational units. ## Calibration from other reading studies

For each target, estimate a Beta distribution for the sharing coefficient from calibration observations of all other contributing studies. Every source has equal influence and splits this mass between its endpoints. We evaluate its transported prior mean, and its posterior mean after adding the target's own calibration families. The two expert readouts were previously fitted to their own source, so this tests transfer of a sharing coefficient to a trained observation channel. All three comparisons were specified before computing the transferred observers' evaluation scores. The initial expert scores were already available. ## Do individual inputs identify the better expert?

A separately specified observer shifted the mixing odds toward the expert whose two independently trained replicas agreed more for that input, relative to their usual disagreement on calibration inputs. It retained the original response likelihood. Aligning the cue with the correct text gained −0.000064 [−0.001370, +0.001121] over the endpoint coefficient alone and +0.000096 [−0.000787, +0.001042] over reassigning the same multipliers to other eligible inputs. Simultaneous intervals cover the two comparisons on the retrospective pair of partitions. This test does not support using between-seed instability to locate reader difficulty within an article. ## A material-based alternative

The constant Beta penalty gives the same resistance to changing the sharing coefficient regardless of the separation between the predictive distributions. A second, fixed method resamples paired calibration families and uses the experts' own predictive divergence as a neutral one-family penalty. It produced a larger prospective internal point improvement on the third partition. A global parent-article audit subsequently identified two partially exposed OneStop families under the source-specific role assignment. Refit results with these parent articles quarantined and independent uncertainty under linked sources accompany the full method and all material partitions.
