# A pronoun, an inferred event and a later contradiction

Jones and Bergen created 30 English item families where properties of two objects make one a more plausible participant in a later event. In Experiment 1, people chose which object a critical pronoun referred to after reading the event. In Experiment 2, different people read a passage with the same critical sentence. A later continuation contradicted one possible interpretation. Corresponding passages replaced the ambiguous pronoun with the name of the object consistent with that continuation. The publication reported a slowdown immediately after the contradicting region. The existing CogLLM evidence inventory had read the source, methods and published result and classified it as public and unprepared; it had not trained or evaluated CogLLM on these trials. This is an independent transfer of weights to an effect whose published direction was already known. ## Predicting interpretation

For every identifiable personal pronoun emit the source pointer to all eligible earlier words. Its score **at the pronoun** sees only words that have been read. Many items introduce the property that disambiguates “it” in the following predicate. Prespecify a delayed interpretation from the same forward: hold the original prefix candidate set and all surface controls at their original pronoun positions. Read the native headwise attention and middle/final similarity at the end of the critical predication, and apply the same frozen weights. In the standalone sentence this is the final word. In the passage use the last word in the fourth of the seven source-marked *critical* regions. Emit both forecasts for every pronoun and each subsequent word up to that endpoint in the offline diagnostic, with no marked antecedent candidates or reference-word labels supplied to the scorer. This later reading uses newly visible context. It represents an experimental transfer of the source pointer to predicate positions. Do not present it as validated source-game behavior at those positions.

Only at evaluation time use the original named alternatives to recover the latest complete mention of each object before the critical pronoun and compute the relative probability assigned to NP2. Preserve original articles and possessives where present. If the author’s spelling or determiners differ, use manually audited item-identity variants, documented before any model association, and reject unresolved ambiguity. Check that the order swap reverses the numbered object consistently. Use the raw model probabilities with no Jones-specific calibration. Compare full-state readout with the exact source POS/lexical observer, always choosing NP2, the authors’ normed syntactic/world-knowledge choices as human comparison anchors, and their published GPT-3 completion-likelihood estimates. Report binary cross-entropy and agreement with a randomly sampled actual person, average the two orders within an item, and bootstrap whole items and readers. Quantify how often the predicted physical object stays consistent when its mention order is reversed. The two primary observations for Experiment 1 are the frozen delayed head's individual-choice loss and its paired change relative to the lexical observer.

## Predicting the experimentally isolated slowdown

Reproduce the authors’ participant and trial exclusions using their actual preprocessing scripts. Record any differences between script and paper. For each complete passage compute native Qwen token surprisal, summed within the seven marked continuation regions. Keep the exact same native token probabilities/context and no additional encoder forward. A reference likelihood comparator uses the two already-provided explicit-NP versions for a matched item and order to evaluate the Qwen probability of the following event words under each possible explicit interpretation. Label this **counterfactual comparator** separately because it combines two distinct presentations.

Region 4 contains potentially contradictory information; Region 5 is the prespecified primary timing endpoint given the published spillover. Keep the other five regions as secondary timing checks. Within an item, order and continuation object, difference the ambiguous and matched explicit condition after accounting for trial pace at pre-continuation regions 1–3. Difference the two continuation objects to obtain the counterbalanced ambiguity-by-continuation contrast. Score whether its direction agrees with the model’s prior preference for the object the continuation contradicts, and evaluate the signed association with that preference. As a genuinely out-of-family forecast comparison, leave one complete physical-item family out at a time. Compare a fixed ridge combining order, release-measured linguistic bias, explicit/ambiguous lexical repetition and the current and three prior regions' raw surprisal interactions, against those same covariates plus the delayed head’s prefix belief. Fit all scaling, coefficients and any intercept on the remaining families only. Report mean held-family squared error and the paired difference. Preserve the separate item-family and participant grouping in uncertainty analyses. Keep the original normed physical preference as a human positive-control predictor. A prediction at the marked contradiction or following region does not by itself identify when every individual consciously revised an interpretation.
