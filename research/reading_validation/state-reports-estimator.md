# Does the physical conflict state forecast reports in a different reading experiment?

Align the action-trained forecast with the *actual sentences the participant saw*. When one displayed sentence contains two mechanically segmented clauses, its hazard of an event is one minus the product of their no-event probabilities. Refuse an alignment if an automatically completed unit crosses a displayed sentence. The calibration features describe the latest visible text only: current sentence index/length, posterior hazard of any prior-sentence conflict, its strongest competing prior and posterior concentration, and change in hazard from the preceding display. Fit an intercept and ridge-regularized coefficients for the actual first-response risk sets in Frick Experiment 2. Leave the assessed story and all its versions out of this step. Compare a same-parameter-form calibrated position/length model with the addition of purely lexical competing-source evidence and native state evidence trained on TRIP, and the previously frozen risk-preserving corresponding human-emission estimate. Directly using an action-conflict probability as the probability of a reader pressing a key is a separate, uncalibrated diagnostic. Use the original probabilities, response-unit definitions and participant/story intervals for Experiment 3 and report any unsupported displays. Keep the optional adaptation independent of the twelve-family measurements for this secondary experiment.
