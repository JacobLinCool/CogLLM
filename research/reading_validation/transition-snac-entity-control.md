# Does the word detector identify more than a new person's name?

We added this diagnostic after observing the primary SNaC and capitalization results. We froze this specification before obtaining any tagged held-out movie words or comparing their human labels with predictions. Apply the released NLTK English averaged-perceptron POS tagger and Maximum Entropy named-entity chunker to each complete original reading segment, including punctuation. The tagger may use words later in the segment. It provides a deliberately strong, independently pretrained name-recognition control. Match the source POS and entity annotations back to exactly the same study word coordinates. Add POS and entity types plus first/repeated and distance since occurrence of the same complete person name to the existing surface and Qwen surprisal controls. Fit the same 0.01-regularized readout on the original book split and keep the same calibration rule. Report the AP of this NER/POS control and the already sealed primary model among all eligible movie words, among words inside automatically recognized person names, and among words in first appearances of such names. Report the number of positive words, whole-movie bootstrap intervals and paired differences. An automatic tagger can miss names. We also retain the separately reported capitalization-restricted tests.
