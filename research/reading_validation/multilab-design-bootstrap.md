The source release contains 138 noun trials on two materials for which the stimulus norm conflicts with the reader’s assigned list. The primary analysis excludes these trials at the condition-mapping stage. Resampling the original laboratories could occasionally allocate all nonzero weight to sites without one of the affected condition cells.
