# Reliability of between-family ERP differences

We observed a positive mean physiological manipulation and weak correlation of itemwise N400 differences with model effects. Assess precision of the item differences by separating original readers, while keeping all assignments intact. For each of 2,000 reproducible balanced random reader splits, form independent low-minus-high cell means at the shared category noun, requiring three retained people per condition in each half, and rank-correlate the estimates across the eligible original families. Report the median and the range of split estimates, the proportion positive, and descriptively the Spearman–Brown transform of the median. The spread across random halves is a sensitivity distribution rather than a new independent-subject confidence interval. Analogous before-clue and early-window analyses clarify measurement specificity. Do not correct the observed model correlation for attenuation if the reliability estimate approaches zero. This diagnostic follows inspection of the primary item correlations.
