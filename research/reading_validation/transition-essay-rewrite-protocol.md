# Can learning from a human edit improve held-topic revision choice and detection together?

An essay sentence expert may mark a connection as difficult, assign overlapping reasons, and supply a replacement. Each observation constrains a different mathematical quantity. An absolute judgment supervises one sentence risk. Each coded reason supervises an overlapping latent cause. A revised sentence supplies a preference between two connected passages under an identical preceding text. We test whether one shared scalar risk can learn from all three forms without treating the expert edit as a new reader's comprehension measurement. This protocol follows the failed specificity of the story observer, prior inspection of the released human rewrites, and the completed essay hazard analysis. The topic partition is fixed. These source-known comparisons are retrospective; the separately registered news study remains unchanged. [Original dataset](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.639/) · [Eight topic folds](transition-essay-reasons-protocol.md) · [Earlier story-trained comparison](transition-external-results.md)

Use all 1,352 binary labels and the 203 real non-deletion replacements previously identified by the original text audit. Count nine unchanged wordings as exact ties in a separate all-record bound and disclose the editing instruction; the instruction is never a read replacement in the primary estimate. Verify the original/replacement have identical text before the target up to whitespace normalization, same source topic, and same sentence index. A frozen Qwen article encoding supplies only features at the completed target sentence. Hold one topic for testing and a second whole topic for calibration in each rotation. Topic and essay receive equal weight within each source observation type. Revisions and originals stay together.

Start from the existing topic-specific hazard or lexical probability. A single residual linear readout adds a change to its log odds. Normalize each of the five original feature blocks using only original training sentences. For the lexical arm permit only the six lexical and surprisal coordinates, standardized to the same block norm. Bound the start probability to [10⁻¹⁰, 1−10⁻¹⁰] for finite numerical logits. An original annotation contributes Bernoulli log loss. A revised sentence contributes a forced choice log loss equal to softplus of its risk logit minus the original risk logit. Joint fitting weights those two mean losses equally and employs ridge .01. Compare against separately fitted absolute-only and replacement-only losses applied to the same initial native observer. Use one scalar on [0,1] per arm to shrink its residual on the source calibration topic under that same arm's fitting objective. Assert maximum gradient below 10⁻⁶ and preserve all source and test forecasts.

For revisions the primary outcome is the fraction of 203 unseen-topic expert replacements receiving a lower risk (ties receive half credit). Also report mean probability difference and choice log loss. For original marks report Bernoulli log loss, AP and source-threshold F1. Compute paired 95% uncertainty by resampling entire topics and essays with common weights across all models and both observation types. Compare the joint observer to each separate native objective and the lexical joint control. A joint benefit requires improved or preserved original-probability validity alongside improved ranking of edits; an advantage on only one endpoint is reported as such. Report how often the result holds across topics and the quality of pairs with longer, equal or shorter replacements. The sample provides no independent post-edit human comprehension responses.
