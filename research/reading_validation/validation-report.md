# What can a language model tell a writer about where reading becomes difficult?

The [integrated manuscript](manuscript.md) includes the independent whole-summary judgments, new source-calibrated word probabilities, direct human agreement on question resolution, transfer to other backbones and revised cross-study uncertainty. [Main evidence figure](../results/reading-validation-manuscript/observed-reading-changes.pdf)

## Abstract

CogLLM processes an article once and estimates how specific groups of readers respond as its words appear. The clearest evidence concerns the identity of an earlier referent. Across 2,196 initial reader–pronoun choices, the native word pointer placed its highest-ranked word inside the human-selected earlier phrase 78.4% of the time, compared with 58.7% for a trained lexical, grammatical and distance control. When 68 equal-length paired sentences initially showed identical words, the first word distinguishing the endings shifted belief toward the intended referent on 73.5% of pairs. The first result concerns actual clicks given a backward reference; the second uses the authors' intended alternatives. In leave-one-story-out evaluation on four German originals, training directly on annotated counts reduced mean error in the number of outstanding questions. The gain over duration came from one story. In an independent cohort of 233 readers an unchanged English sentence elicited a 31.7 percentage-point increase in first inconsistency reports after a change in the preceding text. The risk-set model captured 7.7 points. Directly supervising the randomized effect raised a separate estimate to 19.0 points but more than doubled its excess full-path loss relative to the risk-set model. The evidence supports localized measurement in defined reading tasks. Calibration of an unrestricted reader's continuous cognitive load remains unresolved. ## What is predicted

For article tokens indexed by reading position, a causal encoder computes all states in one forward pass. A state at the current token depends only on previously visible text and that token. A readout can estimate the probability of a defined event, such as a reader marking a reference, or associate a new span with an earlier source. Each observed response has its own measurement model. A passage judgment contributes a passage likelihood, a span report contributes a span loss, and a delayed response can constrain when an event occurred within an interval. Missing responses incur no loss. Knowledge is optional at inference. All statements below refer to the sampled groups and languages named in the individual reports.

Two quantities matter for writers. The effort of processing *this word* can rise while what remained unclear from *earlier words* becomes clearer. A difficult definition might do both. For a trackable issue, the model can estimate its probability of opening and its conditional chance of closing at each later position. Summing the surviving probabilities yields an expected **count of outstanding issues of that type**. The units of that estimate remain tied to its training observations. Neither eye movements nor isolated comprehension answers supply labels for continuous general effort. ## Linking a difficult passage to the earlier text

In the reference game, people also marked ordinary noun phrases. Across 9,726 decisions on 1,294 noun mentions, 2,468 choices included an earlier phrase. For each click the model had to place probability on the reader's actual selected words. Context lowered held loss from 2.203 to 1.880 nats per linked decision after equal weighting by mention and work. For a noun that never appeared earlier in identical form, its top earlier word matched the clicked phrase 34.8% of the time versus 22.0% for form alone. The general decision to link, introduce a new item or choose another action showed very little contextual advantage. [Original reader records and held scores](../results/reading-validation-nominal-reference/scores.json)

A separate experiment asked which two actions in an everyday story were physically inconsistent. On 153 held families, CogLLM jointly identified the later breakpoint and the earlier cause in 55.0% of edited stories, against 38.9% for a trained surprisal control. When an earlier edit made an unchanged later sentence inconsistent, the correct edge probability moved in the intended direction in 90.5% of family-weighted comparisons. The writers who made the edits supplied these locations. On an independent experiment with 233 actual readers, the same event evidence failed to predict the magnitude of their spontaneous first reports. [Physical-state interventions, false alarms and actual readers](state-transport-results.md)

For ordinary expository writing, bilingual training lowered held loss on 1,350 expert coherence judgments from 100 English student essays, from .637 to .601. This was the sole favorable simultaneous result in a prespecified family of ten comparisons. Italian passage judgments and Danish cross-language coherence showed why evidence on English sentence boundaries cannot establish performance in another language. In a distinct expert panel, the native readout trained on logical-quality comparisons ordered clarity ratings correctly in 66.2% of within-topic pairs, vs 62.7% for surprisal. Training on yes/no sufficiency votes alone yielded 52.6%. [English, Italian and Danish article coherence](discourse-order-results.md) · [Three expert measures of explanation quality](reason-quality-results.md)

## The proposed unresolved-question curve

A high count means that several particular issues remain open. It is possible to read an unexpected word while simultaneously resolving an earlier issue. The app's generic four-channel research trace and the directly supervised question-count model are different fitted readouts. The generic checkpoint matched both answer identities in zero of eight controlled two-question families. The count model used 222 actual question records in four German stories, including interval closures and questions still open at the end. Its mean squared distance to the observed count interval was 47.80, versus 54.27 for elapsed duration. It reproduced the direction of determinate changes on 66.0% of occasions, versus 65.4% for elapsed duration. The annotated count in *Verkehrsunfall* fell abruptly as the story moved on. The model continued accumulating questions. This failure is directly visible alongside the strongest positive case in *Katze*. [Original generic checkpoint](../../docs/research/adaptive_joint/results.md) · [Complete count experiment, story plots and line-level values](stock-process-results.md) · [Simultaneous closures and their underestimated magnitude](narrative-discharge-results.md)

At an unchanged target sentence in the first-report study, the adapted risk-set model reduced full-path loss by .205 nats [.063, .347] relative to the original frozen semantic readout. Its native version reduced excess premature reports in neutral texts from 14.5 to 2.3 points. The human condition effect was 31.7 points, versus 4.5 points for the original, 5.7 for the native risk-set version and 7.7 for its directional category variant. Supplying the true critical location and condition during a diagnostic produced 32.6 points. In a further fit with direct supervision on the between-condition difference, the estimated effect reached 19.0 points while the full first-report loss worsened from 1.787 to 3.487 nats. A useful author-facing curve would need accurate local changes and probabilities throughout a normal passage. ## Evidence needed for a stronger general claim

Earlier incremental discourse models already related entity predictions to human processing, and cognitive models have accounted for variations in reference interpretation. The present tests distinguish an observed action, its earlier referent and its magnitude at a later reading point. [Earlier incremental discourse prediction](https://homepages.inf.ed.ac.uk/keller/publications/emnlp11.pdf) · [Prior cognitive model of referential choices](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4823268/)

The present experiments use licensed or publicly released observations. No new readers were recruited. For general cognitive effort, the decisive evidence would join, at the same reading opportunity, independently observed location, the identity of the outstanding issue and a response with a meaningful scale. Within a study, a pooled positive result can otherwise be caused by passage length, a common term, or the number of candidate positions. Across studies, the same wording can attract a definition request, a lower ease rating, or a correct answer; these are different outcomes. A general claim should predict the direction, magnitude and localization on independent material and show a gain over a comparator containing every shortcut relevant to that endpoint. External target access and prior project exposure are disclosed at source level. [Historical overlap audit](release-overlap-scope.md) · [Empirical release scope](release-scope.md)

## Interpretation and availability

The repository contains original-scale forecasts, source-specific likelihoods, documented aggregation, split definitions, plotted values and experiment receipts. Source studies retain their own access and reuse terms. Public availability of a file does not imply permission to redistribute its raw passages or reader records. Procedures and authorized upstream sources are linked from each experiment. No participants were recruited for this project. Depending on the experiment, human evidence means a contemporaneous keypress, a selected phrase, a physiological response, a retrospective annotation or a passage judgment. A subword curve can be evaluated at the finer resolution of the model, but the human validation retains its actual observation unit. [Research catalog](../../docs/README.md) · [Author-facing model and examples](../../docs/try-cogllm.md)

These are linked secondary analyses with overlapping studies and some previously examined materials. The source report specifies the sampling unit, previous CogLLM exposure and whether an independent participant group and independent materials were available. The local and revision family has a retrospective multiplicity audit: 11 of 32 contrasts from six source collections retained positive simultaneous intervals. That correction covers its own family only. A preselected, independently repeated whole-article study measuring precise location, same-issue identity and effect magnitude in ordinary explanatory writing would materially strengthen the central scientific claim.
