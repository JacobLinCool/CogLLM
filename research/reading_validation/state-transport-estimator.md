# Two observation models for what a passage establishes

## Incompatibility and reports

Identify printed lexical words from the raw passage and their original sentence span. The physical benchmark supplies entity and attribute labels during fitting. A named entity is anchored at the last word of an exact contiguous mention of its complete source name. All printed one- to three-word phrases beginning and ending in a content word remain candidates when forecasting. For example, the source distinguishes an egg from the scrambled egg produced by an action. For each aligned sentence estimate its visible entities' binary preconditions and postconditions across the 18 source attributes having binary states. Keep unknown preconditions and postconditions as missing. For an attribute with both observed classes, use a regularized binary log likelihood for its known values. Give each paper-defined story family equal weight. Track the availability of a specified value separately from its truth. A missing annotation cannot establish that a lamp is off. Estimate pre/post compatibility using the product of the two predicted Bernoulli state distributions, weighted by the probability that both attributes were specified. Fully causal estimation can use the current completed sentence when reading an action's precondition and postcondition. A reported incompatibility is available at that sentence end; the sentence annotation cannot time it to an internal word.

Fit a discrete survival model over the ordinary prefix, with competing causes indexed by all earlier completed sentences. A plausible story contributes hazard survival at every opportunity. For a person-authored incompatible story, its loss is the survival through the previous opportunity and the probability of the union of accepted source sentences at the first recorded breakpoint. Balance base/edited arms within family. Retain the official development split for calibration. Forecast independent test articles before consulting their fixed outcomes. Compare a genuine position, length and lexical recurrence control, current-word Qwen probabilities, the additional contextual representation and native attention to a given earlier sentence, and the explicit earlier-post/current-pre state compatibility. Use out-of-fold attribute predictions on fitting families for the competing-cause readout. Report the transfer quality for known physical attributes and for the linked cause, with and without an oracle breakpoint, and the subgroup whose changed earlier action causes an *unchanged later sentence* to conflict. Article-level decisions do not require the contrastive story or a list of entities. Exercise the fixed physical detector on the Frick texts before estimating a response calibration on other Frick source families. In the severe distribution shift from household actions to factual news contexts, grade report magnitudes only where the coordinate is supported. These diagnostic families and previously read cohorts are identified as follow-ups. [Outcome and provenance protocol](state-transport-protocol.md)

## Incremental sentence judgments and whole-passage judgments

Each completed sentence produces visible form and word recurrence statistics, with richer arms adding observed complete-word surprisal and causal meaning/attention. In English fit a Bernoulli distribution for each actual reader's incremental coherence mark at an original scored boundary. Give each source article equal weight and retain the numbers of positive and eligible judgments. In Italian estimate the mean of the published 1–5 passage ratings. A paragraph-level likelihood can train the common readout by aggregating its sentence scores using log-mean-exp over the boundaries. The previously visible sentence prefix determines an individual boundary score. Full-passage pooling only enters the final passage readout. Use the squared error of published mean scores on the original scale, with a language-specific intercept and positive scale for the pooled difficulty score. Do not invent individual ballots or concentrate an entire passage's rating at the worst sentence. Fit separate and shared boundary coefficients with the same standardized inputs, and a weighted predictive mixture calibrated on source development groups. Preserve the original 200 Italian tests and the original independent English story and essay panels. Inspect passage error and rank, sentence response loss, and transfer separately. The published Italian adjacent-sentence word-overlap baseline is a required whole-passage comparator. Missing document identities in the anonymous Italian test preclude a reader- or source-document interval there. Use a passage interval with that limitation disclosed. Within the physical training split, three disjoint sets of whole story families provide held-out pre/post-state estimates for the subsequent event readout. Fit that readout on the full first-event likelihood. At the authored earliest conflict also compare its admissible source odds with the odds at the identical coordinates of the plausible twin. This source-only paired conditional likelihood controls the prior probability of selecting a particular sentence. Give this auxiliary loss one quarter of the total weight. Retain the same losses and regularization for all event feature sets. Separately calibrate a probability mixture of the feature sets on the official development families with equal weight for a plausible and an edited story. Compare calibrated and pure arms on the untouched test. Test thresholds and ensemble weights are fixed before grading test outcomes. A forward pass over an article and causal reduction over that article's completed sentences are sufficient at use time.
