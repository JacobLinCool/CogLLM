# Transfer of a textbook term proposer to previously used scientific cue controls

Replay six dozen existing passages after fitting on unrelated textbook data. The papers and corpus labels, stimulus families and published earlier results were already known. This replay diagnoses how antecedent choice changes the result. Every operator and score in this plan precedes the new model's predictions on those families. The primary DEFT and Guo decisions remain in their original protocols.

For the supplied-term version, use the supplied expression as the referent and score the explanatory evidence as each observed word ends. To compare definitions with the earlier bounded resolution criterion, retain the running maximum of the evidence probability after the source has ended. Subword steps with no newly completed word carry the state forward. This model was trained for interval membership and the resulting number has no calibration to the probability of mental relief.

For automatically proposed concepts, select the highest DEFT term span score in each completed sentence using only that sentence and earlier words. Pass each phrase to the DEFT contextual link head and to the independently fitted e-CARE causal contextual head. The e-CARE replay reuses the same Qwen causal backbone and captures native attention to the phrase. Verify agreement of the overlapping state dimensions from the two independently cached encodings; report numeric precision. Map source sentences to annotated issues only when measuring the retrospective matched-pair endpoint. In a separate full article ranking, let all earlier finished sentences compete, count empty or tied alternatives at their declared abstention/tie policy, and score identity independent of their order. Report the existing sham-corrected 5-point threshold, time of first divergence and both-target family criteria on twelve early/late and eight two-issue families. Award source phrase overlap and exact-match credit separately. All report denominators derive from the complete historical set.
