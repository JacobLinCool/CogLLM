# A new held material comparison of endpoint-calibrated and equal sharing

Two earlier split/seed comparisons found a small mean advantage for the source-material-calibrated Beta(2,2) mode over an equal geometric mixture. Their held likelihoods are known. We test this single directional hypothesis on other material families that were assigned to FIT, not TUNE, CALIBRATE or EVALUATE, in *both* prior comparisons. The old trained heads used responses from these families while fitting; consequently this is a new internal material partition in already studied populations. No person participated anew and no previously designated formal reserve is opened.

Freeze the 24 source names, endpoint likelihoods and cached one-pass Qwen coordinates. For each source, consider all material families that were in both previous FIT sets, including its registered endpoints. Sort those common-fit families by SHA-256 of `cogllm-borrowing-third-holdout-v1/`, source name and family. Designate approximately one quarter of the eligible intersection as new held material, at least one when possible; retain at least one family in training. If a source has only one family in the full archive it cannot enter evaluation. Place the other available families in train, early stopping and coefficient calibration using a separately salted SHA-256 ordering and the prior 15%/15% rule. Verify each document/coordinate exposure belongs to exactly one source, family and role. If a registered endpoint would have no fit material, remove its lowest-priority candidate family from the new holdout and assign that family to fit; perform this safeguard using geometry only across all endpoints. Save group assignments and all material counts before reinitializing the experts.

Recompute human-observation scales on the new training rows. Train shared and private experts from scratch for seeds 1729 and 2718 with the identical previously used architecture, measurement likelihoods, optimizer, batching, early-stopping criterion and convergence guard. Keep calibration and held responses out of checkpoint selection. Compare target-specific MAP under the calibration-family generalized likelihood and symmetric Beta(2,2) penalty to fixed 0.5. Freeze coefficients before measuring the held outcomes. Average over the two seeds within study, equally weight observed endpoints within source, and equally weight studies. Compute the primary paired gain in held negative log likelihood per response and 10,000 whole-source bootstrap draws. Report an exact or 100,000-draw paired sign-randomization P, the number of improving studies, and 95% per-study intervals resampling source material where available. EPS and signs mirror the original evaluation. Historical and fresh results stay unmodified.

Secondary fixed comparisons use private, shared, the symmetric Beta posterior mean, the other-study empirical Bayes prior mean and its target-supported posterior. If viable, evaluate the separately registered outcome-free two-seed instability adjustment too, with no alternative tuning or method selection. For all density calculations retain categorical support, Gaussian sufficient statistics, bivariate covariance and known error. Each target remains in its original observation units. Any subsequent multi-split synthesis must account for repeated source populations and state which folds informed method choice.
