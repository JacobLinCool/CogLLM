# Which reader studies provide independent evidence?

Research units differ across these experiments. A given reader can judge hundreds of words, a source sentence can be rewritten many times, and two studies can recruit different readers to rate the same material. The table identifies which forms of uncertainty the data support.

| Reader evidence | Distinct material and unit of inference | Dependence and prior exposure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PLABA readers named difficult expressions and rated biomedical sentences. | The named-expression evaluation comprises 94 reports in 19 article families, distributed over 12 connected components of readers and articles. Each test fold holds out a complete component. Rating fits use 25 connected components across five folds. | Top-one, top-five and whole-article retrieval reuse the same reports. All 78 versions occurred in earlier project training or development. The current phrase head has separate projections and held-group fitting. This is retrospective reuse. [PLABA study and analysis](local-results.md) |
| Kerwer et al. measured whether headings helped people understand scientific summaries. | 166 participants, 1,283 valid article responses, 12 matched texts, 181 identical body sentences with and without headings. | Every human and model heading comparison reuses these 12 DOI families. The head is fitted on PLABA. CogLLM had previously examined these texts and the direction of the human effect. [Heading contrast](local-heading-protocol.md) |
| Guo et al. measured term familiarity and declared information needs. | 11 researchers each assessed the same 100 computer-science abstracts. For the initial word test, 581 terms in 94 abstracts had unique eligible locations. The primary postreading comparison had 83 informative abstracts. | Before, after, background, example and definition endpoints come from the same people, terms and exposures. Intervals cross readers and papers. The postreading association and the knowledge-conditioned prediction of definition need answer different questions. [Term measurements](local-results.md) · [Help choices](local-help-results.md) |
| Maddela and Xu recorded isolated-word complexity for English learners. Alonzo et al. collected comparable ratings from deaf or hard-of-hearing adults. | Two separate cohorts of 11 people each. Both primary analyses used eligible responses for the same 184 target forms absent from CompLex training and development. | Reader cohorts differ, while lexical material and model forecasts are shared. The units support reader and word resampling. Neither experiment supplied a sentence surrounding the word. [Materials and controls](local-word-reader-protocol.md) |
| SimpDA 2022 readers judged relative simplicity, meaning and fluency across source-matched rewrites. | 11 judges, 60 source IDs, six draft versions per source, three judgments per version per property. Intervals cross source and judge. | The three dimensions and all pairwise contrasts share the same people and drafts. The external source texts passed the historical supervised screen. Results for just the opposed simplicity/meaning subgroup reuse the primary judgments. [Source study and outcomes](revision-results.md) |
| Agrawal and Carpuat recorded actual choices on questions following revisions. | The study reports 112 participants, 60 paragraphs from 30 OneStopQA articles and 1,980 observed answers. Evaluation compares revisions on an identical question and resamples complete articles. | All 30 original articles had occurred elsewhere in the CogLLM program. They passed the separate training-exposure check for the frozen evaluation head. No reader IDs link the six passages assigned to a participant. The formulation and answer alternatives also affect success. [Design and article overlap](revision-comprehension-protocol.md) |
| Leroy et al. manipulated noun phrases and measured reported ease and cloze errors. | 334 participants, 11,949 retained trials, 90 source phrase families, 360 variants. The release contains means for each variant. | A given participant could return to a later session. The two outcome means describe the same experimental material. Individual reader trajectories and participant-level uncertainty cannot be reconstructed. [Study and workbook audit](noun-source-results.md) |
| Chan et al. measured ratings, quiz responses, word familiarity and interest in further explanation. | Static Cochrane summary primary cohort comprises 49 readers, 147 eligible visits and 89 distinct summaries. The ordinary simplicity comparison has 40 readers with an unequal within-person pair. | Summary ratings and quiz answers share visits. The separate transfer of Guo definition preferences uses these same readers in the preceding abstract part of the experiment. Each participating study contributes one source of reader evidence despite the number of endpoints. [Static summaries](relay-summary-results.md) · [Abstracts and requested information](relay-protocol.md) |
| Paetzold and Specia studied both unknown words and simpler substitutions. | Authors report 400 readers in their word-identification study and 300 in their substitution-ranking study. The word evaluation counts 40,754 actual responses in 200 held material families. The revision evaluation counts 7,335 actual votes in 296 held families. | The public responses lack stable participant IDs and Wikipedia source-page IDs. Family-level bootstrap resampling addresses materials. The tests share source features and model fitting, and the original study's substitution-screening procedures precede its ranking task. The 296 revision families subsequently calibrated the frozen mixture transferred to SemEval. [Original study](https://aclanthology.org/C16-1069/) · [Independent score replay](nns-results.md) |
| EMTeC recorded postreading ratings and word rereading. | The primary evaluation covers 107 people, 41 prompt families, 4,368 passage ratings, and 377,873 word-observation rows. Crossed holdouts keep people and prompts separate. | Both outcomes come from the same reading episodes. A historical prompt exposed in the 24-study learning packet was removed. Rereading is a directly recorded action with more than one possible cognitive cause. [Includes registered control and exposure](emtec-results.md) |
| SemEval-2012 elicited simplicity rankings from five highly proficient English learners. | Every member of the panel assessed each of the 1,710 test contexts in the original task. Eligibility checks identified 1,234 contexts covering 171 source lemmas and parts of speech. The model uses the same pair of candidate strings across contexts. | Only aggregate panel ranks are available. Intervals can resample target lemma families conditional on these five judges. The supervised overlap check found no cross-source material in the 5,633 generated single-word variants. Candidate-list comparisons within one lemma share words, contexts and panel. [Original task](https://aclanthology.org/S12-1046/) · [Public source and exclusion receipt](../results/reading-validation-semlex/material.json) |

None of these public studies provides shared participant identifiers across sources. Cohort independence across papers with different sampling is plausible, while strictly verifying that no volunteer ever reappeared is infeasible. The backbone's pretraining corpus also precludes an exhaustive audit. The evidence map keeps a previous constituent dataset, a fresh measurement on familiar texts, and a separately collected reader cohort distinguishable.
