# Sharing an inferred source between argumentative links and their effectiveness

An author can make a well-formed claim while failing to support the earlier claim their evidence addresses. PERSUADE supplies both structural links and human effectiveness ratings for the same essays. These are appropriate training measurements for two related, distinct readouts. On every completed sentence, let E indicate that an annotated backward link ends here. If E=1, S indexes one of the earlier sentences. E=0 indexes a separate no-link state. A grade G is the ordinal effectiveness of a human-rated component ending at this sentence. The quality distribution marginalizes over S, so a grade can update the probability of an admissible source. It does not retrospectively assign a single known source where the original record has several.

Initialize the probabilities of E, S|E and G from the article-balanced expert fits. Keep those expert forecasts fixed as offsets and learn low-dimensional residuals on the already standardized sentence/candidate evidence. Compare a conditional grade model with the link and gate distributions fixed, a joint optimization of the two source distributions and grade expert, and a separate predictive pooling step that estimates how strongly each source outcome should borrow. Both conditional models have identical per-source quality capacity and parameter initialization, and see the same source material. All three observational endpoints keep their own proper likelihood. A grade contributes to the mixture of three-category likelihoods; an observed parent span contributes the mass on its possible sentences; an annotated presence/absence contributes its Bernoulli likelihood. Missing grade or unlocalized relation fields contribute no corresponding loss. This is a composite likelihood at positions with several human component judgments. We cannot identify a unique per-component parent from their sentence-level union. Give each contributing essay equal weight separately for each endpoint. The shared loss is the average of those three observation-specific risks, with a fixed ridge on residual slopes. Fit by checked gradients. The infinite-capacity language model is unnecessary for this experiment: all received frozen native features are from one whole-essay forward.

Learn any pooling weight on the two untouched PERSUADE development topics, equally weighting topics and essays, without access to either final panel. Optimize a convex mixture of the actual endpoint's predictive probabilities with a symmetric beta(2,2) prior, including no-event mass and all grade categories. Compare fully shared, source-specific, and fixed 50% pooling to estimated borrowing on all four held prompts. The classifier's calibration for the endpoint 'effectiveness' does not imply calibration for expert 'insufficiency'. On the independent Darmstadt corpus evaluate this latter target as an ordering of paragraphs, with three original raters kept as separate observations and their consensus and majority reported independently. Predefined methods and evaluations were established after source-only surface fitting and before original held forecast grades. The structural data and published corpus category frequencies had been inspected for alignment and code validation.
