# Learning from reader choices, ratings and electrophysiology at the same word

Each of 1,546 positions in 186 English sentences had an exact prefix match across three independently acquired measures. The data included 121,736 cloze response opportunities (121,722 nonempty completions), mean 1–5 predictability ratings and word-level N400 amplitude. We reconstructed integer cloze counts from the investigators’ published smoothed probability, competition and entropy. The response tables determined a unique target count at all 1,726 available positions. Rating sample sizes were verified from the published first and second moments. Cross-study punctuation differences reduced the positions eligible for the joint fit to 1,546. The independent Spanish source included 122,036 valid EEG word presentations in 8,640 contexts. [English EEG experiment](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bandl.2014.10.006) · [English behavioral norms](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-023-02261-8) · [Spanish EEG experiment](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2026.117159) · [Measurement protocol](convergent-adaptive-protocol.md)

The frozen Qwen3.5-2B encoder processed each complete sentence once, with causal states recorded at the final native token of the printed word. Three readouts observed the same causal representation. The cloze likelihood was beta-binomial. The N400 endpoint used a continuous Gaussian response, and the rating likelihood incorporated the source study’s sampling variance. Covariance between readout coefficients was estimated on the training sentences alongside dispersion and noise. The 24 contextual principal components, standardization and behavioral readouts were fitted afresh on each of five sentence-level training splits. The shared full-data fit settled after four covariance updates. Its largest absolute projected likelihood gradient was 0.00000080. The macro-average contextual log-density gain was 0.0296 nats per word (sentence-bootstrap 95% interval 0.0194–0.0402). Holding the same encoded inputs and observation laws fixed, the mean advantage for a learned full covariance over three empirical independent margins was 0.000035 nats (−0.000329–0.000391). Transferred unchanged to 299 Spanish target-word families, the contextual N400 readout reached ρ = 0.165 (0.146–0.183). It exceeded raw Spanish Qwen surprisal by 0.070 (0.052–0.086) and differed from the fitted surface-plus-surprisal comparison by −0.008 (−0.018–0.002). The within-English and external comparisons establish different limits on the incremental information in the native states. ## When physiology is sparsely observed

In a registered internal follow-up, ten training sentences per fold supplied EEG, yielding 80–88 physiological observations. All other training EEG records were removed from the likelihood, standardization and information matrix. The training sentences retained the cloze decisions and expectability ratings. The experiment used the same English held sentences and held physiological responses as the full-data study. It evaluates a budget change on a previously examined corpus. [Sparse-measurement registration](convergent-sparse-protocol.md)

The empirical full covariance improved held EEG log density over the empirical independent margins by 0.0078 nats per word (sentence-bootstrap 95% interval 0.0019–0.0135). Their N400 rank correlations were 0.358 and 0.352. The corresponding improvements for the two well-sampled behavioral outcomes were both smaller than 0.00005 nats, with intervals spanning zero. The same sharing mechanism applied to the smaller surface-and-surprisal representation gained 0.0030 nats over its independent margins (0.0014–0.0046). Under this sparse schedule, the shared surface model reached ρ = 0.385 and held negative log likelihood 1.421, ahead of the shared contextual model at ρ = 0.358 and 1.602. The result separates a measurable benefit of borrowing information from a potential cost of estimating a larger physiological readout with few measured sentences. [Sparse held-sentence scores and fold models](../results/reading-validation-convergent/sparse/scores.json)

A subsequent variance-component model placed the surface measurements in unpenalized fixed effects, residualized the contextual directions, estimated the amount of cross-reader borrowing from the observed EEG panel and integrated coefficient uncertainty. Its held contextual negative log likelihood was 1.423, compared with 1.421 for its equally uncertainty-aware surface-only comparator. The paired contextual gain was −0.0019 nats (−0.0091–0.0051). Its estimated behavioral and Spanish physiological variance components were effectively zero. Ninety-four per cent of held measurements fell inside nominal 95% prediction intervals. Estimating how much contextual information the measured panel could support largely removed the sparse contextual model’s earlier predictive penalty.
