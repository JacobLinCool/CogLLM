# Separating explanation timing from competing-source identity

The first study successfully ranked known source spans at supplied evidence intervals but recovered neither complete lifecycle in its 109 held two-symbol tests. This follow-up reuses the same articles, frozen Qwen projections and annotations. Conclusions on these previously inspected papers and SymDef are retrospective. The training and calibration source papers remain disjoint from every reported test paper. This experiment registers the changed likelihood before fitting it or examining the new predictions.

A potential source is proposed solely from the incremental notation parser. At each token, fit three different conditional likelihoods with their own denominators. A proposal-level Bernoulli head estimates the chance that a particular visible source will receive its first later definition. A token-level categorical head estimates outside, definition onset, or continuation from the current causal language state, its changes and local surface syntax. Its annotations are the union of the first forward explanation spans, including those whose sources cannot be proposed. Each contiguous interval contributes one onset. A conditional softmax attributes evidence to an already-available source from the actual risk set, training only at gold evidence for which the labeled source was proposed. Competing earlier identities remain in the risk set; their likelihood changes when evidence for another source arrives. Parameter sharing through the frozen context and these source features preserves a single backbone pass. The same factoring and risk sets apply to surface-only comparators; the original joint decoder remains available as an unchanged benchmark.

For a new token, combine the probability of a definition onset with the conditional softmax probability of each candidate, incorporating its independently estimated eligibility in the conditional logits. A source may release once per canonical identity. Reset the release detector when probability of being in a definition is smaller than probability of being outside, allowing a subsequent explanation to release another source. Calibrate the single event threshold on the 15 original validation papers. Choose it using the harmonic mean of complete two-source path recovery and ordinary source+time F1, with F1 resolving ties. This matches the requested joint event; the original decoder optimized single-event F1. Report original criterion plus a separately recalibrated original-decoder comparison under the new joint objective. Preserve counts and uncertainty separately for held Symlink papers, the official development split and independent SymDef. Record statistical precision when few distinct papers or paired events support a result.
