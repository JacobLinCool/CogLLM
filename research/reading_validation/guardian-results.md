# Ordinary readers’ appraisal of original news articles

We reconstructed 17 of 18 original news excerpts from the released stimulus spans and publisher text. The retained 510 survey sessions supplied ratings on 17 distinct article bodies. The stimulus-level sampling uncertainty is consequently substantial. The two primary body-only Spearman correlations were 0.320 for difficulty (article bootstrap 95% interval −0.221 to 0.715, adjusted permutation *p* = 0.416) and −0.104 for incoherence (−0.635 to 0.378, adjusted *p* = 0.685). A separately registered ease-of-reading measure correlated 0.368 with reader ratings (−0.229 to 0.755). All three contrasts with the ARI and Coleman–Liau text measures had intervals spanning zero. This independent sample leaves the predictive value of the existing passage scores for adults reading ordinary news unresolved.
