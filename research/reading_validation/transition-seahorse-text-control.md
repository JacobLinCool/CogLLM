# An additional lexical test for summary comprehensibility

Before examining individual SEAHORSE judgments and before native output arrived, we specified a stronger text control. English-specific capitalization, malformed characters, transcription errors or language mixing may affect a whole-summary judgment. The main 15-feature form observer does not encode these patterns. Train a character 3–5-gram TF–IDF logistic model with a vocabulary fit only on the official training strings, a maximum of 30,000 character features, document-frequency minimum 3, sublinear term frequency, unit-normalized rows and fixed regularization C = 10. Balance total source-article training weight and keep average weight one. Also combine its source-trained log odds with the registered native log odds using two nonnegative slopes and one intercept fitted on official validation only. For a matched comparison use form plus the same character observer. The secondary proper-loss contrast compares these two combined models. This addendum preserves the preregistered form and native primary comparisons and all test articles. Report stationarity of each fit. Ratios on newly scored corpora can differ from performance in training.
