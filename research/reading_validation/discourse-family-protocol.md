# Ten planned tests of cross-language coherence and order sensitivity

This family was specified after material identification and fit-only preflight and before grading new held human or intervention outcomes. The individual reports preserve their own preregistered estimands and 95% intervals. The following inventory reports an additional two-sided 95% simultaneous family using paired bootstrap percentile intervals at tails 0.025/10 and 1 − 0.025/10, with the same 10,000 original material-cluster draws. Approximate simultaneous coverage relies on per-endpoint bootstrap validity and does not require independence between tests.

Positive advantages increase the reported estimate. Adjusted lower bounds above zero support their named comparison. Counts among the ten summarize a prespecified family of diverse, partly dependent hypotheses. The original and altered Italian passages in the last two rows reuse the Italian test material; they assess structure and contribute no independent reader observations. Other hypotheses tested during this wider project have separate source material, designs and prior knowledge, and are not covered by this ten-comparison adjustment.
