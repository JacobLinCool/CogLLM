# Which earlier words did a reader actually select?

Phrase Detectives records the specific earlier noun phrase that each player clicked upon encountering a referring expression. A text-only model should assign probability to those earlier words before it sees any later sentence. This study trains on original reader clicks in the previously separated silver source articles. It evaluates the independent gold texts on the original clicked region. The previous studies on these articles tested reader disagreement without predicting the clicked antecedent. The Gemma pronoun-disagreement forecast and the SNaC movie results were known when this new endpoint was specified. [Public study](https://aclanthology.org/N19-1176/) · [Source folds and overlap](transition-game-supervision-protocol.md)

Use the same source articles and gold articles as the game-supervised experiment. A query is a visible one-word personal pronoun with at least four independent, eligible prior-entity choices under that study. Count a player once. A player who made conflicting exact-region choices on a pronoun contributes no click to its region metric; retain queries with at least four unambiguous region voters. Map a selected earlier noun phrase to all overlapping certified visible words. Discard a target with an unmappable prior region and record the reasons. Preserve all earlier certified lexical words as model candidates, including determiners, verbs and nouns never marked by the source. The model receives no gold mentions, entity IDs, full-document parse, candidate list from readers or future words. Score every visible target-form occurrence on gold before matching responses on a separate machine.

Use one frozen Qwen3.5-2B forward per complete article. For each pair of a current pronoun and an earlier word, use its prefix-observable distance, capitalization, repetition, form, and a general-purpose English POS tagger applied to the observed prefix. Compare (1) a trainable positional recency model, (2) a trainable lexical, recency and POS compatibility model, (3) the same controls plus middle/final native-state cosine and mean/maximum native attention across heads, and (4) the controls, cosine and separate attention strengths for every actual full-attention layer and head. Condition simple candidate cues on pronoun gender/number class without a human candidate oracle. Include a uniform over earlier words as a descriptive floor. Keep coefficients shared across query positions. Use a penalized conditional softmax over all earlier word positions. A player who selected region R supplies the loss −log Σⱼ∈R p(j). Average learners within one query and queries within source work. Fit the same .01 slope penalty for every arm, standardizing predictor coordinates on training only. Require stationary fitting; if an alternate numerical implementation is needed, keep the same objective. Do not tune on gold.

The primary endpoints are mean negative log probability of the actual clicked region and top-one earlier-word containment for a randomly selected eligible original voter. Average individuals within a query, queries within an article, and finally articles. Use 10,000 paired whole-article bootstraps. Report top-three containment, best compatible rank, probability mass, distances, clicked-region length and the number of genuine people, queries and documents. Expert entity chains are for secondary evaluation only: ask whether the top word is inside any earlier mention of the clicked entity. Quantify the coverage that a union over alternate mentions introduces. Report primary metrics separately for clicks from people who supplied no source-training assignments, for nonlocal choices at least ten original words back, and for disputed pronouns. The subsets will be descriptive when underpowered. The output identifies a location supported by player clicks, not an unobserved spontaneous question or cognitive-effort magnitude.

The original clicked region contains multiple simultaneously valid words. A correct point inside a multiword region does not establish that the full phrase boundary has been recovered. The game interface also constrains which spans a player can select, whereas model normalization covers all preceding words. Shared participants and sparse individual disagreement limit broader claims. Complete the existing game, specialist and writer-verification commitments independently.
