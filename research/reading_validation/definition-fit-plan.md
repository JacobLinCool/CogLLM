# Frozen analysis choices for term-specific explanatory evidence

Each complete word has a native Qwen end token. The proposer considers up to eight contiguous words within one punctuation-delimited segment. Its positive class is an exactly matched primary term. Sample up to twelve uniform negative candidates per positive (at least eight on windows with no terms) and eight boundary-near negatives in positive windows. Train logistic readouts with squared coefficient penalty, clustering equally by source file within each task and weighting the two outcome classes equally. Select the penalty from 0.03, 0.15 and 0.75 by the same class-balanced objective on the held-out psychology corpus. Preserve candidate prevalence, exact-span average precision and class-specific performance on complete test grids. No target position receives information from a later text token.

For each direct forward definition, form three supervised kinds of comparisons. A matched earlier term acts as an identity foil when it has no annotated link to that same definition. A same-term nondefinition interval with the same number of visible words acts as a timing foil when available; choose by closest interval proximity and stable pre/post ordering. Average the causal feature rows across an annotated interval for these two ranking losses. For an absolute reference, pair a hash-selected eligible word inside the definition with a hash-selected outside word for that term. The binary term-specific label indicates that a visible piece of text belongs to a definitional passage. These signals constrain different properties of the same linear head. Give total weights 0.30 to source identity, 0.35 to time ranking and 0.35 to binary membership, equally split within participating source files and binary classes. The candidate set and lexical/position controls receive exactly the same labels and optimization. Inference never sees a gold interval.

For the structural supplied-term primary, compute the mean contextual score over the final four eligible words through the annotated definition end. Compare every earlier established gold term, including the target only if its span is on the finite candidate grid. Targets omitted from the grid contribute zero to the full automatic result. For the document-only primary, select up to eight non-overlapping proposals by their text-only scores among all observed spans ending before that stop. Rank their sum of log term-proposal probability and log term-specific explanatory probability. Include any previously unseen candidate drawn from inside the arriving definition: their presence represents a deployment challenge. Report source recall among the eight candidates, actual end-to-end top-1, and the matching position/recency controls. The annotated endpoint is used solely to decide when to score the model, as in the supplied-term evaluation.

For the external study, predictions of remaining explanatory evidence are the maximum of the trailing four-word average explanatory probabilities strictly after the flagged term through abstract end. A matched lexical predictor and remaining-word/mention baselines use the same text and stops. For the document-only variant we gate the supplied-term value by an automatic on-arrival candidate decision: the term must be the most highly ranked span ending at that moment and must exceed the psychology threshold chosen to maximize F1. An undetected term receives zero automatic evidence. A fixed term field is required on the human side to join ratings. Report detected query coverage and the easier supplied-term analysis separately. Source and finished checkpoints are hashed before combining forecasts with test outcomes.
