# What aspect of a reader's choice can be predicted?

A response outside the named lexicon may reflect vocabulary, spelling, a different plausible guess or task compliance. Before opening the independent later responses, decompose the conditional log score into the Bernoulli log loss of selecting other and, on named choices alone, the conditional log loss for the exact named word. For a named answer, the latter equals its full negative log probability plus log(1−p_other). For an other answer the exact-spelling contribution is unidentifiable. Give crossed reader-by-material intervals for each same-reader advantage and their additive contribution in nats per observed answer. The decomposition uses the already fitted reader profiles and all four prespecified controls. It can reveal whether personal history helps distinguish actual named words in addition to a propensity to leave the lexicon.
