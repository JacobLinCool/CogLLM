# A published reader effect in the recoverable sentence swaps

The Italian source paper reports that mean reader coherence declined with increasing distance between two exchanged sentences. It also reports whole-corpus Krippendorff’s alpha of 0.32 for individual ratings. We recovered 18 authentic transpositions from 13 source documents in the anonymous official test, all from TED. Nine involve neighboring sentences, seven have one intervening sentence, and two have two intervening sentences. The weakly supervised training objective specifies the original order and uses no distance-dependent weights. [Original report, §3.1](https://aclanthology.org/2023.clicit-1.40.pdf)

Before opening the public test grades and without changing any fit, report the mean human grade for each of the three published distances and the observed difference between adjacent swaps and the pooled more distant swaps (9 against 9). Compute the same descriptive quantities from the original and structurally augmented responsive readouts, keeping the actual observed article version. Bootstrap original source documents, skipping resamples missing an entire comparison group and reporting how many draws remain. The direct observation consists of ratings of different passages in each distance category. Two examples at maximum distance do not support a reliable dose–response estimate for that category. These 18 items are a subset of the original Italian test and the specified analysis does not increase the broader project's number of independent replication cohorts.
