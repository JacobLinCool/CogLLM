# Study independence and multiplicity for the word, sentence and revision results

We enumerate 32 comparisons made by this part of the project. This synthesis is retrospective for the previously graded studies. We fixed the list and family size before opening the 2012 SemEval aggregate human ranks. Every row is directional, so a larger number always favors the stated model. The inventory includes failed primary claims and secondary contrasts that have appeared in the text. It covers the local-difficulty, reader-assistance, native word, sentence and revision studies. Trials outside that area, including discourse-boundary models and the main token-stock experiments, are separate programs with additional researcher degrees of freedom.

We use the Bonferroni projection of a 95% simultaneous envelope across all 32 comparisons. The two-sided marginal tail probability is 0.05/(2×32)=0.00078125. For a comparison whose paired 95% interval already includes zero, interval nesting proves it cannot have a strictly positive adjusted lower bound. The same is true if the entire 95% interval is unfavorable. For every originally positive comparison we will replay the original paired contrasts with the same resampling units, then compute the wider percentile interval. Reproducing the original center and 95% interval is mandatory before interpreting a wider interval. Directly report sparse cluster counts and finite tail resolution. This familywise adjustment does not require independence between contrasts. Its numerical accuracy inherits each study's original cluster-bootstrap assumptions and finite sample limitations.

Do not count two response endpoints, two controls or two papers reusing the same words as independent replication of one cognitive process. Separate the physical materials, participants, design-stage exposure and measurement occasions. Maintain a stricter distinction between superiority to a matched independently trained system, superiority to the strongest explicit reference, a genuine human manipulation, and agreement above chance. The calibrated percentages and dozens of ancillary sensitivity analyses retain their stated descriptive role.
