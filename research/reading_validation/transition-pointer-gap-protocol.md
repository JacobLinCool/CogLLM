# External name-link transfer on the official GAP test passages

Freeze all Phrase Detectives pointer models, features and source coefficients. Evaluate transfer to ambiguous pronoun–name relationships independently annotated in English Wikipedia for GAP. CogLLM has not scored GAP previously. The source pointer’s game predictions had not been seen when the test was registered. The corpus supplies reference identities; it does not measure reading time, subjective effort, spontaneous questions or the distribution of readers’ choices. [Dataset paper](https://aclanthology.org/Q18-1042/) · [Official release](https://github.com/google-research-datasets/gap-coreference)

Use every row in the publisher’s official test file, revision 83135f26025cdd705c04f77e940d0c9201116b80, sha256 1c35e36d5b14f6313ec3f6cd67b275de282595dd59e59390e00cfff9897a6819. Audit leakage by matching normalized Wikipedia titles and 20-consecutive-word shingles against all reading-game source, calibration and gold texts, plus excerpts from the previous narrative training books. Exclude overlapping articles before inference, report counts, and check repeated test passage groups. No GAP training or development data, model selection, candidate tuning or calibration. All public labels remain outside accelerator inputs. External predecessor location and response labels are extracted after preparing the raw article packet. Frozen-backbone pretraining exposure to Wikipedia and GAP is unknown.

Present each raw test passage in one causal Qwen forward. The models see only the text, deterministic past punctuation, and the same verified prefix-visible POS features as in the reading-game study. Score every identifiable personal pronoun with at least one earlier word. Neither name, its location, the source title nor the target annotation is marked. The primary test includes exactly one human-verified coreferent name and both competing names entirely before the designated pronoun. Both must have a unique set of certified earlier word positions based on their original offsets. Report exclusions for cataphora, neither/both referents, ambiguous or unavailable word geometry.

For the primary set report whether the top-ranked word anywhere in the earlier prefix falls inside the human-designated name span, negative log mass of the designated name, and correct two-name preference. The last metric compares the two annotated regions’ probability after the all-word forecast and gives ties half credit. Prefer mass averaged by region length as an additional check on token counts, independently of the primary summed-mass preference. Include the full source-trained POS/lexical recency control, aggregate versus separate native attention heads, uniform floor, and “choose the closer of the two flagged name spans” as an explicit evaluation-oracle reference. Compute paired whole-Wikipedia-article bootstrap intervals and report performance for masculine/feminine pronouns, the farther correct name, and the equal-word-length candidate subset. Report any evidence of differences between masculine and feminine examples without interpreting these strata as demographic groups. The official published benchmark uses its two supplied names, complete text and different coreference metrics; this prefix-only literal-span study cannot be compared to its published F1 as though the tasks were identical.
