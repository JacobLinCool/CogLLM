# Movie variation and unrecoverable source positions

The confirmed all-word pooled AP ranges describe these 39 movies. This secondary analysis adds movie-to-movie uncertainty using a separate estimand: give every movie with a recorded CharE error equal weight and calculate its AP difference. Keep the same production-Qwen source fit, calibration, movie forecasts, annotation masks and strong NER control. The pooled endpoints are already known before this analysis.

Within each movie compute the exact minimum and maximum achievable difference in tie-grouped AP. Use the same human assignments for both policies. A movie containing no human-marked character reference has no defined AP and is counted separately. Across eligible movies the sharp mean AP range is the mean of the sharp per-movie endpoints because source placements are confined to their own movies. Resample whole movie identifiers 10,000 times and report a two-sided envelope from the lower 2.5th percentile of mean lower endpoints to the upper 97.5th percentile of mean upper endpoints. This is an approximate cluster bootstrap for sampling movies; reader identities and alternative populations remain unobserved. Also report the number of movies whose entire AP interval lies above zero, below zero or includes zero. [Pooled identification results](transition-partial-results.md)
