# Reading a complete story with outstanding questions and deliberate suspense

Onea and colleagues released four original German narratives, annotations of questions arising from the plot, and continuous suspense ratings. The experiments address different judgments. Annotation teams could inspect a complete story and review the questions. Other readers drew a suspense curve on a screen of already visible text. Each released word value summarizes the drawing across that word. These ratings do not measure how hard participants found the prose to understand or identify the first moment they recognized a particular answer. The original study's aggregate association and the earlier project's question intervals are known. This is a new test of an encoder receiving the entire original story in one left-to-right pass. [Original article](https://doi.org/10.1017/langcog.2025.10057) · [Author archive](https://osf.io/m524v/)

## Do two competing open questions receive different evidence?

The primary narrative-identity test fixes a candidate closing line in an unseen story. Find an individual team's question whose definitely open spell ended between two successive lines with no intervening uncertainty, and another question from that team that was definitely open through the current line. The two opening lines must be distinct, precede the close, and have occurred before the comparison. Both alternatives therefore share the same incoming text, token count and history. Group comparisons within closing line, then team, then story. Withhold uncertain transitions from this primary response. Also display the number of closures for which an interval or a shared opening line prevents such attribution.

Fit a low-dimensional ridge-regularized logistic ranking of the closing versus surviving issue on the other stories, weighting the source stories equally. Score *all* earlier lines, using current and earlier line representations, native attention to that line, the mass assigned to its outstanding candidates, the amount the deployed readout resolves there, lexical evidence and distance. The annotated open/closed status is available only in fitting and scoring. Compare the reading model against distance/position only, a trained lexical/distance scorer, and the original unmodified pointer. Choose tied alternatives with half credit. Report all four leave-one-story-out folds with equal-story mean, rather than treating the four texts as a large corpus. A positive selective-attribution claim requires improvement over lexical plus distance in at least three of the four stories as well as in the four-story mean. This conditional endpoint supplies the two candidate onset lines and the assessment line at evaluation. It does not demonstrate that all three marks would be recovered independently in a new article.

## Does suspense follow the same trajectory as unresolved content?

For each aligned word assemble the mean standardized suspense rating from the released readers. Score change in consecutive visible-line means and within-story rank correlation in levels separately. Compare the deployed unresolved signal (without changing its weights), linear log words-read, and a train-only next-line forecast using textual progress, lexical change and surprisal. A secondary readout adds the original joint states, the four stock/flow channels and attention to earlier lines. Standardize and fit only on the other three stories against genuine word/screen-aggregated human measurements. Reader ratings or outstanding questions from the evaluation story are never inputs. Report reader split-half reliability, by-story results and average over stories. If screen alignment is unavailable retain only the outcomes with independently anchored words and disclose the lost scope. Report contiguous screen-block sensitivity; uncertainty from readers in one story cannot support generalization to unseen genres.

A separate endpoint tests each identifiable annotated question count at the original line level, counting definitely open and possibly open questions as interval bounds. Report the equal-story mean squared distance to the admissible interval, rank association for nonconstant counts, and accuracy of increases versus decreases restricted to determinate changes. Compare against constant and a causal progress-only regression learned without the evaluation story. Same-line expert labels cannot locate an exact subword realization. The data support text-level suspension of an issue and separately measured intentional narrative suspense. Keep both distinct from understanding and subjective effort.
