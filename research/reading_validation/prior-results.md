# A source-trained prior improved a low-data reading-level scale across repeated draws

Two educational corpora received the same calibration budget of 64 training sentences each. We repeated training with 32 deterministic draws on each corpus while keeping the official test sentences and their two ordinal grade judgments fixed. Borrowing the prior estimated from three other studies reduced ranked probability score in every draw of both corpora. The average prior-minus-matched difference was −.00481 on SCoRE and −.00452 on Wikiauto. The equal-corpus difference was **−.00466**, with a 95% interval of −.00559 to −.00376 when resampling both training draws and test items. Lower ranked probability score means the predicted distribution placed more mass near the observed ordered grade. [Replication specification](prior-replication.md) · [All 64 paired scores](../results/reading-validation-prior/replications/scores.json)

Both models used the same 64 sentences, causal input features, optimization and test items within each draw. The finite training pools overlap across draws and the test material had been seen in the earlier evaluation. This establishes repeatability of a particular low-data transfer result across draws on these two corpora. Gains from the project's broader adaptive joint weighting must be evaluated separately.
