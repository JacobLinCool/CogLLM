# Blind transfer from editing actions to a different expert's choice between rewrites

The DECOR study published three sets of 100 judgments by one leading author who did not provide the original annotations. For each original sentence and preceding context the evaluator saw two replacements and the original reason for the incoherence. The expert could select the first output, the second, or a tie. The three material sets compare a human reference, a Llama rewrite trained with reasons and one trained without reasons to GPT-4, with model identities blinded during judgment. The published aggregate results, all file headers, and text identities were inspected; no row's selection was opened before this protocol. The expert and choice endpoint are new. Every original essay belongs to the earlier DECOR sample, so generalization here concerns the measurement and decision maker conditional on holding each source topic out. [Original experiment, §5.3 and Appendix D](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.639.pdf) · [Labeled releases](https://github.com/BillyZhang24kobe/writing2coherence/tree/382b4fa086351202f5ac514fcd2b86a5479882e1/data/human_eval)

Use the frozen original topic rotation, the source optimizer, source normalization, fitted regularization, and reliability from the existing judgment-plus-replacement observer. Each held topic's expert alternatives stay out of all fit and calibration data of its original status/choice scores. Reconstruct the unchanged original assigned essay title and context with the candidate at its actual sentence index. Normalize whitespace identically to the original archive. Reuse a single causal Qwen encoding of each unique resulting passage, with no extra model question or generated answer. No issue label, reason, status, original author, system identity or choice may enter model input. Verify the candidate starts at the same character offset across its pair. Exclude the previously identified literal editing instruction from the main text-ranking analysis, retain its tally and report all choices secondarily. Keep identical candidate strings and report them. Fit no direct parameter to the new expert's choices before locking all candidate forecasts.

The primary transfer endpoint is pooled agreement with this expert's directional choices when two distinct actual sentence replacements were shown. Rank by lower sentence-end risk. A model tie receives half credit. Show all source observers, especially the fixed native joint model and matched lexical joint model. Report by the three original comparisons and for same-length and different-length replacements. The paired primary contrast is native joint minus lexical joint within the same original pairs. Report the proper binary log loss of the source risk logit difference alongside agreement. The single-expert intervals resample complete topics and essays, keeping all three competing pairs for a passage together. These intervals cannot establish reliability between different evaluators.

For the full tie-preserving outcome, zero difference in scores expresses equal directional odds and does not itself predict how often a judge chooses a tie. As a separate calibrated diagnostic, hold out one whole topic at a time and fit one three-way conditional choice observer on the other seven topics' *new expert ballots*. Model the human left/right/tie outputs using two symmetric directional utilities and an independent tie propensity, category-specific provenance effects when uniquely recoverable from the ungraded strings, and a common slope on the frozen textual utility difference. Compare model+provenance versus the same provenance-only and lexical+provenance calibration, and report source order bias, class prevalence, proper log loss and three-way accuracy. Estimate a directional-discrimination AUROC conditional on pair type and paired source. Resolve identity using the known human reference string and the across-arm shared GPT candidate. When the system origin of display order cannot be resolved uniquely, leave the origin effect zero and report how often this happened. Limit any conclusion to this one judge and human-generated/source-model alternatives. The original paper states that the evaluator also considered the diagnosed reason during choice; CogLLM only observes text.
