# How much do other game readers establish about an ordinary pronoun?

Estimate the same-game repeatability of four discourse choices and the achievable utility of selecting one earlier word with access to other annotators. For each pronoun with at least four distinct independent responses, withhold one reader's decision, learn the distribution of the four actions from the remaining decisions at that position with one-half vote per class, and score the held reader's four-action logarithmic and Brier scores. Also report raw between-reader exact action agreement. For the earlier-word task, choose a preceding word covered by the largest number of the remaining players' clicked regions. Give tied maxima their uniform expected hit, account for all nonlink responses, and abstain if no other player chose an earlier entity. This oracle has access to other humans at the same position. It cannot be used to forecast an unread article and does not establish an absolute cognitive ceiling.

Show broad original gold, all additional previously unscored articles, and the work-disjoint subset. Separately report concordance among choices conditioned on two players both linking backwards. Bootstrap complete works and give words equal weight within a work. Interpret measured between-reader disagreement and the model's errors separately. This is an external measurement-quality check and contributes no coefficient, release calibration, or tuning of the model.
