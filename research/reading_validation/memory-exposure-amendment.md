# Calibrating a new reading cohort without old checkpoint exposure

Before the attention adapter was run or either new cohort's outcomes were scored, the corpus-overlap audit located some Experiment 1 Frick stories in the historical token checkpoint's training materials. The two participant cohorts and their button responses remain new. The original calibration including historical token channels is retained. A parallel calibration removes those channels in every arm. This strict analysis uses only causal text measures and the independent counterfactual-premise readout, and excludes all Experiment 1 response rows from the matching family. We require this analysis for a claim of response generalization to article families unseen during supervised fitting. Underlying Qwen pretraining exposure to public text is unknown. [Overlap audit](../results/reading-validation-release/historical-exposure.json) · [Original design](memory-protocol.md)

The actual optimizer warm start is the converged frozen solution. Only the query and value projections of layer 24 have rank-eight updates (scale 2). AdamW uses learning rates 0.0002 for attention updates and 0.00002 for the reading projection. There are five matched triples per training minibatch and a gradient norm limit of 1. Validation loss controls patience and selection under the original protocol. Each model supplies all its own language and token observations from a single forward; the adapted readout applied to an article never requests a second pass through the original encoder.

The two experiments each have 12 experimental text families and separate fillers. One filler uses the same target sentence as Experiment 1 family 15, under a different source story number and different condition codes. We matched the experimental source story number as well as the target sentence. Experiment 2 and 3 exact stimuli are identical for the experimental nonillustrated pairs. In family 12, a hyphen in “fifteen-minute” differed from the Experiment 1 transcription; matching the critical earlier wording ignores punctuation. Participant responses were still excluded from this check. Texts, trial records and outcomes remain separate files.
