# Exact original exposure at each measured word

Inspection of text and mapping fields revealed that the English rating/cloze item file and the separately recorded original EEG exposure disagree at some positions through apostrophe normalization. The integer item and sentence IDs still coincide. For all claimed external EEG forecasts, restrict to target words where the entire prefix ending in the measured word is literally identical to the original EEG source shown by the investigators. This gives 1,546 of 1,726 noninitial target-word positions in the first extraction. The 180 other rows come from 27 source sentences and require a later separate exact-string replay for EEG. Human cloze and predictability analyses can evaluate the original rating/cloze word sequences independently, but methods compared on multiple human endpoints should use their common exact intersection in the primary comparison. As a sensitivity, exclude all sentence-final positions as in the prior studies. Erasure of final punctuation for HMM alignment or undetected text normalization is forbidden. The resulting estimates use the negative sign of the released N400 voltage and inverse 1–5 rated predictability, matching the original investigators' definitions.
