# Attribution when two scientific symbols await an explanation

## Scientific target

A reader encounters mathematical notation before a paper explains what the symbols denote. The linguistic target is the link between a visible symbol mention and the first directly linked description. Author-side annotations identify when a definition is available in text. They do not measure when any individual reader understands it. We test whether a causal model can attach incoming evidence to the correct outstanding referent and leave a competing referent outstanding.

## Data and blinding

Use the public Symlink competition data in the entrant’s archive. Retain original character coordinates, exact paper identifiers and direct primary-description annotations. Merge explicit symbol coreference. A source is forward-defined if its first mention ends before its earliest direct explanation and it has no prior or overlapping definition. Eligible dual cases require two distinguishable sources already present when the first defining region starts, with two nonoverlapping first descriptions. Retain papers without dual cases for the article-only task. Remove ambiguous text duplicates across papers from the pooled input. From the published training papers reserve every fourth by salted SHA-256 order for testing, every fifth remaining paper for calibration and the rest for fitting. Use the independently released three development papers separately, and leave the unlabeled official test set unused. In the independently curated SymDef corpus use complete unambiguous paper and sentence IDs from the train and test archives, restoring the provided math symbols in the released token order. Restrict to sentences with at least one mathematical expression, including negatives. Reject missing symbol placeholders or inconsistent reconstructions; exclude overlapping training papers. A fixed native-token maximum of 1,024 applies to every full paragraph or sentence. No crop at a gold annotation. ## Model and measurements

Fix a frozen Qwen3.5-2B native backbone and its two projected intermediate/final causal states. Each original text is presented once. Extract causal native attention at visible mathematical notation. Source proposals arise from the text's TeX delimiters, constituent identifiers and their repeated forms. Certify the lookup against original character offsets. The supplied-source diagnostic may make a pair of original symbol spans available; article-only models must generate both anchors and closing points themselves. Evaluate a model with distance/repetition/visible text features and a model adding native relation and paired state features. Fit pointwise interval-membership detection together with logistic ranking differences between candidate sources for the same incoming cue. Calibrate timing and abstention on the development papers. Include fixed recency, first-mentioned and literal name overlap baselines. Preserve gradients, fitting convergence and per-model forecasts before reporting outcomes.

Primary identity measure is the percentage of two-source families giving the true source a greater evidence score at its own description than the other already-visible source, averaging its two directions. This is conditional on the annotated observation interval. An exact joint hit on the article-only task requires the actual source span and a causal predicted closing token intersecting its first definition. Report precision, recall, F1, false marks, proposal coverage, and chance for assigning the first closure correctly while retaining a competitor until its description. Report both full scoring and covered-source diagnostics. Multiple examples from a paper travel together during resampling. Describe the units directly and preserve estimates and uncertainty if supervised approaches lose to proximity. Disclose prior source geometry inspection and separate issue/definition supervision from human mental effort.
