# Ordinary pronouns need a decision about whether to link

When any of four reading-game responses was permitted, the earlier-word pointer at its previously chosen 0.80 threshold made a correct suggestion for 54.6% of independent responses [48.3, 60.7%]. Its apparently stronger 91.6% precision is conditional on analyzing positions at which readers had chosen an earlier referent. On the broad task it offered links at 363 of 757 visible pronouns. Fifty suggestions lacked enough qualifying independent responses to adjudicate them. Across all offered pronouns the resulting sharp equal-word precision bounds were 50.5–64.3%. [Conditional result](transition-pointer-results.md) · [All-action scoring](../results/reading-validation-transition-pointer/action-gate/scores.json)

The follow-up trained a separate probability of choosing an earlier referent on 24,243 original decisions at 2,758 pronouns in 230 source works. Each position carried the fraction of original players choosing the earlier-link action; the other options included discourse-new and nonreferring mentions. The model multiplied this probability by the existing pointer's probability at its preferred earlier word. One causal forward covered each of the 286 original texts. Both convex fits converged, with maximum gradient below 4 × 10⁻⁸. The source-development set contained 1,448 actions at 161 pronouns in 12 works. [Source protocol](transition-pointer-abstention-protocol.md) · [Fit and weights provenance](../results/reading-validation-transition-pointer/action-gate/scores.json)

No source threshold achieved the required simultaneous 70% precision lower bound while keeping at least 15 suggestions in four works. At 0.60 the native product retained 31 pronouns with expected precision 64.2% and a simultaneous lower bound of 54.1%. At 0.70 only nine positions remained and at 0.80 none did. On external gold texts 4,880 actions at 631 pronouns contributed data. Binary reader-action Brier score was 0.2397 for frozen native states, 0.2360 for surface features, and 0.2402 for the constant source-development rate. The native-versus-surface difference was +0.0037 [−0.0041, 0.0117], where lower is better. The 0.80 native product made one suggestion among all 757 visible positions and half of the players at that position supported it. [All nine operating points](../results/reading-validation-transition-pointer/action-gate/selection.json) · [External scores](../results/reading-validation-transition-pointer/action-gate/scores.json)

Automatic marking in ordinary English remains unqualified. The separately scoped manual inspection asks the writer to select a known backwards-referring pronoun and then shows its proposed earlier phrase. Treating the earlier words and all three nonlink responses as units in one likelihood is a further test of whether evidence for a specific referent can improve this decision.
