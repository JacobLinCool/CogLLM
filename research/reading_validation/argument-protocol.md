# Can a reader-facing model locate which earlier statement a writer's evidence supports?

PERSUADE 2.0 provides individual argumentative components, their hierarchical parent, and human ratings of how effectively the writer expressed each component. Stab and Gurevych provide independently collected argumentative essays with directed support/attack relations, together with a second annotation of whether specific arguments contain enough evidence for their claim. These last two outcomes share essay material. All are judgments after reading. A judgment of insufficient support does not establish when the reader experienced difficulty. [PERSUADE release](https://github.com/scrosseye/persuade_corpus_2.0) · [Argumentative essay study](https://aclanthology.org/J17-4005/) · [Evidence sufficiency study](https://aclanthology.org/E17-1092/)

Use the original public PERSUADE training release. Partition whole prompt topics by deterministic hash into 60% train, 15% development with the rest held. Within each available topic take up to 120 distinct texts by a second salted hash, between 70 and 550 whitespace-separated words inclusive. In the Darmstadt study take every distinct qualifying English essay within the same length range. Remove cross-corpus exact or 20-word consecutive overlaps before outcome grading and disclose which essays were lost. Apply an additional native capacity limit of 1,024 model tokens to complete essays. The sole accelerator input is each whole unmarked essay and its hash; all text positions use original Unicode characters. At most one production encoder pass per essay. Retain causal middle/final sentence representations and the current sentence's attention to each earlier sentence. Check serving evidence against complete-prefix truncation. Report the native eligibility counts independently of outcomes.

A sentence becomes eligible after its visible end. Positive links originate at the sentence containing the last visible character of the human-annotated child component. All strictly earlier sentences overlapping its own earlier parent component are eligible correct answers. Link scores range over *every* earlier sentence. Emit the union of correct sentences for multiple legitimate links ending together, and count ambiguous, missing, intra-sentence, and forward links separately. In PERSUADE recover parents by unique matched text and type within the essay; rounded numeric identifiers alone are inadequate. In Darmstadt recover exact author BRAT character offsets, validate every literal substring, and grade explicit directed support links separately from attack links. Neither supplying the correct child sentence at grading nor supplying a corpus annotation to the encoder is equivalent to full-article discovery. Grade both conditional retrieval and detection over unmarked completed sentences. When the dataset has no annotated earlier relation, the latter outcome means no annotated earlier link at this position. It need not mean poor writing.

Fit the source conditional choice likelihood and sentence event likelihood with text-only form, recency, word overlap and overt discourse markers. Compare against always choosing the nearest earlier sentence and the augmented causal native representation. Fit effectiveness in its original ordinal three-category units. Grade predictions on withheld PERSUADE prompts and independently in the Darmstadt essays. Separately test whether low source-trained effectiveness predicts experts' insufficient-support decisions at the end of the actual corresponding argument; the two constructs and labeling schemes differ. Evaluate observed individual votes when released, majority outcomes separately, and disclose cases without uniquely aligned original raw paragraphs. The independent essay family, source topic and number of eligible sentences must accompany positive results. Quantify uncertainty by resampling whole independent essays; for PERSUADE report all held topics separately. The studies anticipate portions of the label definitions; their released outcomes have not been used to choose this subset or obtain model grades. This is an additional endpoint within a broader experimental program.
