# Which earlier requirement makes new information inconsistent, and do people notice?

In TRIP, people wrote everyday actions, edited one sentence to make a story implausible, and annotated the affected sentence pair and physical changes to entities. In complementary studies, readers reported inconsistency when an earlier premise changed and evaluated the coherence of natural paragraphs. These outcomes can constrain separate parts of a reading model. A label that two physical conditions cannot both hold does not measure how often a person will notice. Train the conflict and entity-state observations with their own likelihoods. Fit the reporting process with the observed human first-response risk sets. Distinguish the authors' chosen manipulations, annotator judgments of plausibility and actual online responses. [TRIP study](https://aclanthology.org/2021.findings-emnlp.422/) · [Public author release](https://huggingface.co/datasets/sled-umich/TRIP) · [Human inconsistency experiment](memory-protocol.md)

Use the published TRIP train/development/test split, preserving all variants of a story family. Primary evaluation uses the original plausible stories and the human-written single-sentence replacements in the authors' main paper. Keep the separate order/automatic-augmentation rows out of training and primary evaluation. Reconstruct which positions the authors' intervention changed from the two original sequences, and count how often a recorded breakpoint occurs at a later, literally unchanged sentence. Test both sides of the meaning change on that subgroup. Evaluate the prior-and-current pair jointly on the unmarked complete passage, the correct choice between two whole stories, and detection on the original single story including plausible negatives. Compare surface word identity, recurrence, position and length; add native sentence/word evidence; evaluate explicit remembered entity-state compatibility with the same inputs. The article is read in one causal forward. An additional fixed readout can accumulate state and report after that one pass. No candidate entity roster, counterpart text, gold positions, world-state labels or future sentence is supplied to the encoder or the inference-time readout. Keep scoring against the authors' verified coordinates separate from spontaneous reader location.

Assess transport to the existing Frick readers and their first reports at the unchanged target. The twelve previously studied materials make this a disclosed mechanism follow-up. Exclude the evaluated family when fitting any reporting parameters. A state found in a five-sentence household story may not carry to a later generic-to-specific noun in a different study. Grade both the reader's complete path and the magnitude of their response change as previously defined. Real report likelihood and false early reports are central. Include an oracle given the experimental state only for calibration diagnosis; compare with the previous outcome-blinded forecast.

A second independent endpoint concerns ordinary expository writing. The Italian DisCoTEX study collected native-reader judgments of four-sentence Wikipedia and TED excerpts, including reordered and substituted passages. Its public shared-task table contains the mean of the panel scores for each passage. Recover only its original labeled passages and retain the official 200-passage test. Test whether a readout over the original visible sequence predicts those mean judgments beyond word overlap, length and actual Qwen word surprisal. Grade original passage ratings as passage ratings; no location marked by a human can be inferred from them. Form groups using the source identifier when available and shared text when recoverable, and disclose missing test document/reader identities. Use the original pooled labels separately from the reader-judged current sentences in CoheSentia and DECOR. [Primary human study](https://aclanthology.org/2023.clicit-1.40/) · [Research data release](https://github.com/davidecolla/DisCoTex)

Before forecasting, preserve each dataset's raw source digests and the intervention/annotation geometry, check native word alignments against original Unicode offsets, and verify suffix invariance in separately truncated genuine passages. Commit the estimator and controls before inspecting new model grades. No individual predictions or random seeds are independent experimental units.
