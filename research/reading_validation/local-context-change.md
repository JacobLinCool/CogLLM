# Does a term score distinguish familiarity gains at the same measured starting level?

The independent Guo transfer found that frozen models rank terms by both pre- and postreading familiarity. Those results leave open how much postreading concordance is explained by prereading differences. After opening the primary results we specify a secondary analysis restricted to pairs of terms in the same abstract, for the same reader, with exactly the same prereading rating and unequal postreading ratings. A model succeeds when it assigns more difficulty to the term with the lower postreading rating. The personal prereading rating ties for every eligible pair. Compare the previously frozen lexical head, the PLABA span head, and the trained form comparator without fitting any new coefficients. Preserve the original weighting of reader–article units and crossed resampling. Report the count of eligible pairs, articles and readers, and the difference from the 50% tie reference. Matching an ordinal prereading rating permits residual pre-existing knowledge differences within a category. The data still do not identify the words or sentences responsible for a change.
