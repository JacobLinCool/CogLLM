# A source-trained word selector and design-aware comparison

Add 0.5 total background-prior pseudo-count per marginal category, estimate the target prior from source material, and combine likelihood ratios with prior odds. This role model runs at the sentence end. The source-derived lexical identity supplies a conditional softmax loss over all the words in that sentence.
