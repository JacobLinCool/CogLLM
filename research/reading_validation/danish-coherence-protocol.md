# Does the bilingual coherence readout transfer to naturally written Danish prose?

The native readout was registered on English incremental marks and Italian passage ratings before its Italian held-set grade. DDisCo is a separate Danish study. Two trained readers assessed the overall organization and comprehensibility of authentic encyclopedia passages and anonymous Reddit contributions on an ordered low, medium, high scale. The official archive records one grade per text and provides no separate judgments, reader IDs or sentence-level responses. The paper reports 100 test items per domain. The authors' downloadable table contains 100 Wikipedia and 101 Reddit items, all with distinct text. The Hugging Face copy's metadata also reports 201 official test items. Use all eligible items in the original downloadable release, with equal domain weights. [Source paper](https://aclanthology.org/2022.lrec-1.260/) · [Authors’ repository](https://github.com/alexandrainst/danlp/blob/master/docs/docs/datasets.md#ddisco) · [Dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/DDSC/ddisco)

**Primary claim.** In each original domain, does a fixed English/Italian-trained estimate rank an authentic Danish passage as more coherent when its published human grade is higher? Compare only two passages from the same domain with strictly different grades. Give each eligible pair within that domain the same weight. Ties in forecast get half-credit. Average the rates in the two original domains with equal weight. Random or solely domain-dependent ranking receives 0.5. A reliable within-domain advantage requires a positive lower paired 95% interval over 0.5. Report separately whether the native features improve on the Italian source-only form and form-plus-surprisal weights and on the Danish-supervised controls. Give the two domain estimates, Spearman correlation and outcome counts. Estimate the graph of uncertainty by independently resampling whole text-overlap groups within each domain for 10,000 draws with seed 378283. Pair all comparisons. No domain or length recalibration of the transported readout on Danish ratings. Between-domain separation alone never meets this endpoint.

**Source constraints.** Run each original, unmodified text through Qwen3.5-2B once. Reuse the Italian-fitting means, standard deviations, form, form-plus-surprisal, independent, fully shared and source-development mixture parameters, pooling the noninitial detected sentences by log-mean-exp. A one-segment passage uses its completed segment. Keep the same frozen operating bound of 1,024 model tokens and coverage conditions. Do not feed the grade, domain or fitting status to the backbone. Archive all article predictions before opening official Danish test labels. Public pretrained text exposure is unknown. The archive provides neither anonymous thread IDs nor original encyclopedia article IDs; audit case-folded shared strings of twelve words across every Danish test passage, all Danish train and earlier supervised CogLLM material. In the comparative source-supervised controls exclude **source training** groups that overlap any test text and collapse duplicate training inputs at the published-grade level. All eligible official test passages remain in both primary and supervised reference grades.

**References.** A domain-only comparator ties all within-domain pairs. Fit two Danish reading references on the Danish training portion only. The form reference predicts the 1–3 grade from author genre, word and sentence counts, the proportion of words exceeding six letters, Björnsson's Danish LIX, rates of common Danish conjunctions and adjacent lexical recurrence, with a ridge slope penalty of 0.01 and equal domain weights. The stronger lexical reference uses the same predictors and a TF-IDF word unigram/bigram ridge score with penalty 0.001 (mean loss) and an intercept. Use shared source-only scaling and no test tuning. Report Danish-trained controls as separate, target-supervised estimators. Rank the transported English/Italian output on its original scale. Display optional error on the 1–3 scale only via the prespecified linear endpoint map (1 + (predicted Italian mean − 1)/2); such absolute calibration is an extrapolation, separate from the primary ranking. Human ratings express the post-reading judgment of a passage. The dataset has no report of online timing, personal load, or the first troublesome sentence.
