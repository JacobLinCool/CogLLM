# A matched T5-Large character-span specialist

A rank-eight T5-Large adapter learned the book training targets, but ordinary greedy decoding almost always selected the majority response on movies. Across 1,661 held movie sentences, the decoder produced 1,654 well-formed clear-sentence outputs, five character outputs and two malformed outputs. Three generated spans matched original words, covering four marked words. On the 22,133 measurable original words, this recovered 4 of 413 positives with no false word marks (F1 .019). On 1,416 fully measurable sentences, it recovered 3 of 250 affected sentences, flagged two additional sentences and reached F1 .024. [Generated text and confusion matrices](../results/reading-validation-transition-t5/adapter/scores.json) · [Sentence outcomes](../results/reading-validation-transition-t5/adapter/sentence-scale.json)

The budgeted specialist updated 2,359,296 query and value parameters with the original source's effective batch of eight. Across 1,840 optimizer updates in five epochs, token-weighted development NLL fell from 15.95 to 0.402. Training took 39.8 L4 minutes, with exact checkpoints preserved across reclaimed allocations. The best development NLL occurred at the fifth and final epoch. The observations verify optimization progress over the specified schedule. A later convergence plateau remains unmeasured. An earlier full-parameter run lost its allocation after 100 updates and supplied no held forecasts. [Training trajectory](../results/reading-validation-transition-t5/adapter/training.json) · [Selected model receipt](../results/reading-validation-transition-t5/adapter/receipt.json)

Goyal, Li and Durrett reported .79 precision, .81 recall and .80 F1 on sentence-level character-error decisions, with .98 conditional span overlap, for their full-parameter T5-Large using a different test set and reference. Their public checkpoint's source-title membership is undetermined. Our adapter isolates an alternative modeling and decoding recipe within our book-to-movie split. At independently selected movie-blind operating points the causal CogLLM obtained .824/.936/.876 sentence precision/recall/F1, and its NER-plus-surprisal comparator obtained .753/.840/.794. The original paper's absolute scores cannot rank these systems on the same population. ## A stronger specialist with source-calibrated decisions

The same selected T5 now scored each target under both response categories and generated a word span conditional on the character-error category. This separates a competent ranking or span generator from the default decision to call almost every sentence clear. Source-only operating points were chosen by maximum F1 over 716 book sentences and 9,280 words. Each observer used its own thresholds. All 39 movie titles were held out. [Calibrated decoding protocol](transition-t5-decision-protocol.md) · [Book operating points](../results/reading-validation-transition-t5/type-calibration/book-operating-points.json)

| Held human response | Measurable units | Positive units | T5 precision / recall / F1 | CogLLM precision / recall / F1 | NER and surprisal F1 |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---:|
| Word selected as an unexplained character | 22,133 | 413 | .319 / .324 / .322 | .782 / .816 / .799 | .687 |
| Sentence contains such a character | 1,416 | 250 | .380 / .512 / .436 | .824 / .936 / .876 | .794 |

CogLLM's matched gains over the source-calibrated specialist were .477 [.422, .533] word F1 and .440 [.392, .492] sentence F1, resampling whole movie titles. Dropping T5's category decision and always forcing a character span increased word recall to .637 but lowered precision to .174 and F1 to .273. Across all 1,661 movie sentences submitted to T5, 1,464 targets were fully visible at the original 1,024-token truncation setting, 12 partially visible and 185 hidden. On the fully visible comparison T5 versus CogLLM obtained .329 versus .800 word F1 and .446 versus .876 sentence F1. These comparisons cover the fitted T5 adapter and the observed training and decoding recipe. They cannot identify how the published full-parameter specialist would perform on these exact titles.
