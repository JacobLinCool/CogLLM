# What remains unresolved after reading each token?

Textbook annotators identify a term and the interval explaining it. This permits a stronger localization test than locating an explanation after someone tells the model that a definition has just ended. We ask whether the model can open a concept at its own mention in the article, maintain the corresponding item, and close it when the explanation for this *same* concept arrives. No question, term, interval, passage length or answer is supplied. This analysis was specified before the textbook fit and scores. The data measure textual openings and explanatory content. Whether the student experienced uncertainty remains an independent behavioral question. [Definition protocol](definition-protocol.md)

At a word end, select the highest-probability span ending at that word. Open a fresh, uniquely indexed mention if its proposal logit meets the term threshold learned on psychology. Two different accepted spans remain distinct, including overlapping or repeated expressions. For every arriving word, evaluate the link score from the earlier concept's causal span and the latest four available words. Give each prior proposal the score σ(proposal)σ(link). The strongest earlier proposal alone may close at this position. An already-closed winner gives no second closure and cannot make a lower-ranked competing concept close. Use the first eligible crossing of a calibrated threshold. At a new mention add one open item to the stock. At a confirmed closure subtract exactly that item's unit. The stock can rise, stay constant, or fall without a sentence or paragraph reset. If an opening and closing coincide the ledger preserves both events even though the net curve stays flat. Between measured word ends the Qwen-token curve carries its previous value; distinct subword transitions have no human supervision here.

Tune the single closure threshold separately for position, surface, separate contextual, and joint contextual models by maximizing exact-source closure event F1 on all psychology passages. A hit requires the predicted term's exact span and an arrival inside one of the same term's annotated future definition intervals. Labels with several contiguous definitions for one term allow one first closure. Count a false alarm when the emitted event fails either criterion, even if some *other* term is being defined. Include articles and word positions without annotations, supply the denominator for every event, and credit each gold issue only once. Use a shared fixed threshold grid 0.01–0.99 in steps of 0.01, with the higher threshold preferred on a tie. Evaluate all held-out biology and physics windows. Report pooled and source-macro precision, recall, F1 and counts; stratify by subject, report recall conditional on being opened, and distinguish wrong source from wrong time. Compare exact-span and interval outcomes with position and trained lexical alternatives with source-file cluster intervals. Also include a zero-parameter exact-name-only closing rule applied to the same opened terms in a matched lexical baseline. [Joint training](definition-joint-protocol.md)

For competition, identify definition intervals with at least two previously annotated terms still without an earlier gold definition. For each eligible target, require that the correct source opens, that its first closing arrives inside its own interval, and that its competing issued term also opened and remained unresolved over that interval. Report the full denominator as well as the conditional denominator where both were detected. Finally reconstruct the actual residual number of annotated forward-linked terms at each word using alternative gold conventions of closure at region entry and exit. Report distance to the interval of possible counts, position-only floor, and how much of the original baseline's total variation is explained by length. Because any long or reverse definition can render target counts ambiguous, provide labeled-issue coverage and evaluate all admissible forward-linked cases in the source.

For the released research curve, freeze a hash-selected sample of 250 calibration and 500 evaluation windows with positive/negative articles in natural proportions, at source-matched position. Use its existing opening and resolution scores and displayed pointer spans with the same validation-only decisions. The native pointer has a fixed subword width and can therefore miss exact terminology by construction. Record both exact and target-overlap credit, positive and negative outcomes, and the date of extraction. The native curve can also be assessed for within-passage agreement with the lower and upper stock bounds independent of its arbitrary unit of mass. The quantitative external human end point retains Guo's pre/post reader ratings, with no gold time or concept closure interval. Its primary independent experimental unit is the paper.
