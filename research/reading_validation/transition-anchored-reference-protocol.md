# Preserve the reading-game prior and update it when a new word arrives

Let X be the earlier word a reader might select and q the target pronoun. The Phrase Detectives model gives an unrestricted word score a(i | q) learned from direct human clicks. The separately trained DPR observer gives d(i | q,k) after reading word k, for k ≥ q. At each word use the anchored utility a(i | q) + d(i | q,k) − d(i | q,q). Equivalently, multiply each prior word's odds by its relative change in the independent observer's odds, then normalize over every preceding word. The coefficient on this change is exactly one. This is a discriminative update: correlated evidence from two fitted observers prevents an exact Bayesian interpretation. At q, all earlier-word scores and every source-calibrated conditional click policy are preserved by identity. There is no feedback into Qwen, no second pass, no decoder generation and no gain fitting.

The observation motivating this new diagnostic was that the previous semantic DPR readout revised its prediction as an event unfolded, but assigned poor probabilities at the pronoun in Jones and Bergen. The already observed Jones and DPR endpoint results therefore count as retrospective even though no coefficient here uses them. Test anchored semantic evidence as primary. Include frozen no-update reader prior, source DPR semantic observer, reader-click pointer reapplied at event end, fitted step-only update, and native attention-only update acting on the identical reading-game prior. Retain both native and lexical human-click priors; the native prior is primary. For supplied referent regions use the log sum of unnormalized word potential within each region, treating them as coarsened word choices. An equal-word-count sensitivity computes the difference of mean word utilities within each phrase. Never truncate or repeat a reading in order to generate a posterior.

On the archived Jones stimuli, evaluate choice log loss and hard agreement at the final event word, family–reader clustered uncertainty, invariance under swapping mention order, and the original controlled region-five ambiguity-by-referent timing contrast. Compare end-of-event to its own prior. On the DPR official pairs the existing intermediates preserve all source and held evidence but only the lexical click prior is reconstructible without a second model call. Compare the new lexical-prior updates to the frozen lexical prior and unanchored semantic observer on the two authored alternatives, unrestricted earlier-word ranking, and same-prefix/equal-length clue onset. Reserve the independent experiment below for the primary external human test.

## Independent confirmation with judgments of ambiguity

Yuan, Malaviya and Yatskar publicly released actual questionnaires with five answers for each English item: definitely first, probably first, ambiguous, probably second and definitely second. The source is new to the CogLLM experiments. The public reported group effect motivated inclusion of the cohort. The new model has not predicted these stimuli. Keep separate the held-out material and the original two training corpora. Existing pretrained model exposure cannot be excluded. [Original EACL study](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-eacl.75/) · [Authors' database](https://github.com/LucyYYW/AmbiCoref/tree/69a6639cdaaa2b7bcaadd173da960b20c0335742) · [Prompted LLM comparison on this cohort](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1527/)

Freeze all code and both sets of source weights before the first native external run. Input to the encoder is the original sentence alone; remove only outer spreadsheet line breaks. The authors' names identify the two requested response regions solely when measuring the model, and the answer question and ratings stay off the accelerator. Require two distinct earlier non-overlapping source phrases and one uniquely identifiable supported personal pronoun after them. Exclude ambiguous geometry, sources sharing a full exposure or an eight-word phrase with supervised DPR sentences or the human-click training documents, and report all resulting counts. Preserve item/question collisions and aliases in the intake audit. No trial from this archive may update either readout, a threshold or temperature. Disclose possible overlap due to the frozen backbone's pretraining.

Declare two complementary primary human endpoints. (1) Among actual directional responses (definitely or probably either referent), average negative log likelihood of the named option; compare anchored semantic at the end to the same human-click prior, source semantic alone, elapsed-step control, and anchored native attention. Report correct-side agreement and Brier separately. Also report all five source categories itemwise, showing the mean predicted prior and final probability for each category. (2) Use 4p(1−p), the normalized variance of the two-referent forecast, to rank an individual explicit 'Ambiguous' action against one of the four directional actions. Report AUROC with ties, the baseline prevalence and average precision. The variance is a ranking signal for ambiguity and is not calibrated as the probability of choosing that button. Test whether the event improves ambiguity discrimination over prior alone and the trained surface/step controls. Report the five ordered category-specific average predicted probabilities of NP2 and the mean predicted variance for definitely/probably/ambiguous. Show direct proportions of each original category and respondent/questionnaire sampling uncertainty. Do not recode definite/probable judgments into invented numerical magnitudes or infer an unobserved instantaneous effort.

Give every distinct measured stimulus equal weight and preserve its full distribution of individual responses. Cluster uncertainty by original response-sheet row and by sentence family after replacing supplied names with placeholders and normalizing punctuation, person pronoun and case. A released sheet is a questionnaire assignment; participant identities across sheets are unavailable. Report a template-family weighting sensitivity, and a descriptive breakdown by source construct (ECO/ECS, IC, TOP) where exact source membership can be established, by number of subsequent words and by whether the pronoun is terminal. Also report the subset with both source noun phrases one word long to exclude region-length bias. On source-defined minimal pairs sharing a prefix, compare all preceding predicted probabilities before the first changed word and report external human directional and explicit uncertainty changes where pairs can be matched exactly without access to responses. Distinguish independent judgments of completed sentences from unobserved reading times or exact human resolution instants.
