# Reading a pronoun chain to recover the name

At the time this analysis was specified, no trained antecedent-pointer predictions or performance estimates had been inspected on the reading-game or GAP texts. These sources observe two different kinds of human link. A reader in Phrase Detectives can choose the most recent word “she” for a later “her”. GAP identifies the annotated person's name. This distinction motivates one fixed transformation of the primary all-earlier-word probabilities. No labels, weights, thresholds or external names enter the transformation. [Reading-game protocol](transition-pointer-protocol.md) · [GAP protocol](transition-pointer-gap-protocol.md)

Let p(q,i) be the original conditional probability of earlier word i at a personal pronoun q. For each earlier pronoun i with a previous-word distribution, replace its point mass by that pronoun’s already computed distribution over terminal earlier words. All other words retain their original mass. If the first word is a pronoun it has no earlier candidate and remains an unresolved terminal. In increasing word order the distribution is

$$P_q = \sum_{i<q} p(q,i)\begin{cases}P_i & \text{if word } i \text{ has a prior-word pointer},\\ \delta_i & \text{otherwise.}\end{cases}$$

All paths end because every edge points back in the original text. A terminal can be any earlier ordinary word, including another mention of the same person, a determiner or a verb. The algorithm does not know the two GAP names or any annotated mentions. Compute the transformation for the frozen headwise model and the frozen lexical/POS baseline on their complete sets of unmarked pronouns. Preserve one original backbone forward per article. As an implementation check, require that all derived mass is supported in the earlier prefix and sums to one within numerical tolerance.

The main transfer question is whether this reading of the headwise model increases (1) open-prefix top-word containment in the human-designated name and (2) the probability of favoring that name over the other source name, averaging articles equally. Report raw versus terminal scores for each model, and native versus lexical under identical terminal propagation. Include name-region log mass, mean correct-name probability, a matched-length check and results for the farther correct name. Calculate 10,000 paired whole-Wikipedia-article bootstrap intervals. Scores on the 1,107 qualifying pronouns conditioned on whether another scored pronoun falls strictly between the start of the earlier of the two names and the target pronoun are descriptive. Keep the original GAP benchmark and outcome definitions distinct.

On the gold reading-game texts, predict whether the top earlier word is in (1) the reader's actual clicked region and (2) any earlier mention of the expert-adjudicated entity selected by that reader. Report top-three containment and mean entity probability. Retain every input pronoun with human clicks even when the reader clicked a previous personal pronoun. A literal pronoun-only region can consequently receive exactly zero terminal probability; report how often this occurs and the original-click performance of the untransformed source model. Any transfer improvement indicates name-level reference recovery under the external annotation design. Human cognitive effort, reader questions and future resolution are separate outcomes.
