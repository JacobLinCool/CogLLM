# Predicting unresolved terminology on its natural prevalence

The previously tested integer ledger overproduced unresolved mentions. The new question is whether a probabilistic, source-indexed count can anticipate the sparse period between an actual concept and its explanation. Biology and physics were examined when diagnosing this failure and will be labeled a retrospective comparison. The independent prospective test is W00, the authors' public term/definition token annotations in ACL workshop texts. No W00 outcome, aggregate result, tuning decision or model prediction has been used for the candidate. [DEFT diagnostic](definition-results.md) · [W00 original data](https://github.com/YipingNUS/DefMiner/blob/75d3fdd843478d91f963daf6d65e31f8306fa9b6/W00_dataset.zip)

Reuse the frozen DEFT source and link readouts. At each completed printed word, propose the single highest-scoring one-to-eight-word source ending there. A causal existence probability is calibrated against the true prevalence of that exact forward-defined source, using all training word endings, including documents and positions with no definitions. Logistic regressors use only logit of the existing term score, span length, segment boundary, and passage position. Each earlier proposal retains its own probability. Given a real source, estimate its explanation hazard from its own contextual link strength, causal age since the source and relative change of link strength. Fit this conditional hazard from direct interval-censored observations. The probability mass of the first event in the annotated window is the survival to region entry times the probability of at least one event within that interval. Treat separate human terms as distinct risks. No other term's explanatory evidence can affect an already opened mention. All surviving mass is propagated without resetting at paragraph boundaries. The predicted stock is the sum of per-mention existence probability times that mention's probability of survival. Report expected stock, median stock under independent Bernoulli indicators, and actual interval-specific mass. Retain all earlier probabilities; any stated numerical pruning bound must be checked.

Train the sparse calibration and hazard on original social-science DEFT training files, with source-balanced likelihood and squared ridge penalties from 0.001, 0.01 and 0.1. Select on the original psychology split. Existing feature extractors have seen DEFT training; out-of-discipline validation is therefore essential. Position-only and trained lexical models get the same natural-prevalence calibration, censoring likelihood and hyperparameter choices as the semantic head. Compare all with the original integer ledger and the constant zero curve. Use mean absolute and squared distance from the range of counts allowed by entry/exit at the labeled definition interval. Report counts/distributions across word positions with and without remaining material, item-level localization/ranking and false alarms outside defined cases. A zero signal is a strong comparator for absolute errors on sparse prose. Assess proper squared conditional event-prediction error on positions where a source is certainly pending or certainly closed, and report average precision alongside prevalence. Distinguish a score ranking from its numerical calibration.

W00 supplies pre-tokenized sentences with TERM, DEF and other labels. Use every nonempty matched released source row (the paper-level count can differ from the archived line count). For relationship and timing scores, an unambiguous single contiguous term followed by a contiguous definition identifies a valid source; contiguous same-term repeated fragments and multiple possible terms without explicit relation IDs cannot establish which issue closed. For these cases report counts and exclude ambiguous positive material from occupancy evaluation. Retain all annotated negatives, deduplicate identical canonical sentences before scoring, and remove exact text overlap with DEFT. Unseen W00 words are read in their released order, including original source token whitespace. Report paired intervals under repeated sampling by sentence and a sensitivity stratifying by source section. The W00 archive does not supply stable paper IDs. Correlated sentences from the same paper may make sentence-level intervals too narrow. Region position is weak supervision, and this structural measurement does not certify a reader's mental-state trajectory.
