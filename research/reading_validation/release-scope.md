# What the CogLLM evidence supports

The November 23 report adds estimates of next-word expectations, human precision, controlled scientific headings and an independent summary-comprehensibility assessment. [Integrated report](manuscript.md) · [Matched heading intervention](heading-adaptation-results.md) · [Experimental source and data index](reproduction-index.md)

An editor can submit an English passage and inspect three separate estimates. The background of a completed sentence estimates ease on the biomedical annotation scale. An underline proposes difficult wording relative to other wording in that sentence. A continuity alert marks a sentence whose relation to the preceding text resembles breaks readers or editors have identified. The three marks come from different human tasks. A passage can contain a difficult term and still follow logically from its context. The additional token trace is a research output whose individual cognitive interpretation remains open. ## What “single forward” certifies

The verified writing interface runs Qwen3.5-2B revision `15852e8c16360a2fea060d615a32b45270f8a8fc` with portable-reader checkpoint `c1704d4436d767fa43bdb59e9216698514e1f6bd1d45cad4b60d0f018d84abb5`. The display accepts an English passage up to 2,048 model tokens with no measured reader profile. It shares one causal backbone pass across the readouts. Reopening the experimental trace displays the cached result. A phrase ranking compares possible spans across a completed sentence and therefore uses information from later words within that sentence. Appending information after a completed sentence preserved verified earlier readouts within numerical tolerance. Running another draft, or adding reader information to an actual model input, constitutes a different input. The backbone produces numerical states and attention summaries at visible token positions. Readouts transform these into word scores, sentence ratings, or competing-issue signals. These are measurements of the program. Agreement with a human rating, prompted keypress or corroborating evidence span must be tested separately. In particular, directing attention back to a passage does not establish that a particular reader retrieved it. The app's displayed checkpoint linked both issues in 0 of 8 controlled two-issue families, which were author-constructed. On separate causal-plausibility data, the stricter COPA both-order test reached 38.0% versus 37.6% lexical. Supplied anchor spans recovered both controlled cue locations in 12 of 12 reused single-issue families, while selecting whole earlier sentences recovered 0 of 12. These outcomes distinguish an available cue from automatic discovery and from a reader's actual recognition time. An earlier matched backbone comparison evaluated 9,284 texts each with Qwen3.5-2B, LFM3-1.7B-Base and Gemma 4 E2B. A newly fitted readout for each backbone achieved CompLex mean absolute error of 0.0806, 0.0856 and 0.0872 respectively, versus 0.1096 for the common control. All three missed the size of the Frick experimental first-report response. These fits establish some transfer of the readout method across architectures at selected endpoints. ## Reproducibility and the remaining claim

An interval label explains why a sharper token curve alone cannot settle the timing question. Suppose a previously identified issue is still open when a two-token clue begins. Let the conditional probability of resolving it at the first or second token be $h_1$ and $h_2$. If the annotator reports only whether it resolved anywhere in the clue, the observable probability is $1-(1-h_1)(1-h_2)$. Both trajectories $(0.5,0)$ and $(0,0.5)$ give exactly 0.5 for the observable. They place the entire decline at different words and would have identical likelihood on arbitrarily many repetitions of this interval-only task. Shorter crossing intervals, genuinely timed reports or an independently justified mechanistic constraint would distinguish such trajectories. A zero difference under suffix perturbation verifies that the prediction respects reading order. Both trajectories pass that check. [Interval analysis and human measurements](identity-results.md)

The writer application has model revisions, checksums, serving coefficients and an HTTP and numerical replay. Reproducing a research score requires the original source release at its pinned revision, its response and inclusion rules, and the saved GPU features or a fresh feature extraction. Re-evaluating material used during design remains a retrospective check even when the computation can be repeated exactly. The project reports results for separate checkpoints and observation heads. Combining their best estimates into the performance of one released curve would change the estimand. No new participants took part in this work.

To substantiate an individual load trajectory, existing human records would have to connect, within the same reading episode, a newly reported unresolved idea, its identified referent, and subsequent evidence that the reader resolved or retained that idea. The temporal resolution of the records bounds the resolution of the claim. Current evidence supports sentence-boundary prompted reports, retrospective term difficulty, reader-specific familiarity and conditional semantic links in their respective settings. It does not yet support reporting a token score as a calibrated probability that the particular user is confused at that instant.
