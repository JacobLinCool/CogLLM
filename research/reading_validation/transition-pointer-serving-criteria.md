# When the English pronoun link may appear in an ordinary passage

The existing optional pointer locates a word conditional on someone choosing an earlier referent. An ordinary pronoun can also be discourse-new, nonreferential or have another analysis. The first all-action audit found only 54.6% article-balanced precision for the conditional-only threshold. The extended serving policy asks about all four human response types. It returns zero suggestions when no pronoun passes the source-calibrated threshold.

These serving rules were fixed after release of the conditional-only and source-level results, before fitting or inspecting the new all-action gate. Select the native gate if its source-development rule qualifies. If only the independent surface-gate plus native-location policy qualifies, use that policy and disclose its components. If neither qualifies, keep the unconditional observer research-only. For ordinary reader-facing links, also require an external article-group 95% lower precision limit of at least 70% at that single source-selected operating point. This is a release criterion. It does not change the prediction rule or suppress the numerical report if the intended transfer fails. A gold failure does not promote a different model. [Source threshold and broad denominator](transition-pointer-abstention-protocol.md)

A suggestion means that a particular word lies inside an earlier phrase a randomly selected player would click. The measured estimate is per article over eligible, adjudicated pronouns under a reading game. Keep its language, domain, denominator, threshold coverage and false suggestions explicit. The product of the two model outputs ranks suggestions. It is not an externally calibrated individual probability. Preserve optional manual investigation of antecedents where the writer already knows a phrase refers back. Do not present any link as a measure of cognitive effort.
