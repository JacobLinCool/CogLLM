# Full-risk observation likelihood for question lifetimes

For question i appearing at source s, let a be its last definitely open line and b the first later confirmed absence. Set h(s,t) to the conditional resolution probability by the end of line t, given the question survived the preceding line. An observed closure contributes the product of survival probabilities from s+1 through a, multiplied by one minus the survival product from a+1 through b. This covers exact closures when b=a+1. If no absence is observed, the final product is omitted and the last confirmed-open line is the censoring time. For a known pair of active questions the conditional choice uses h alone. For retrieval without an advance cue, rank every (s,t) by the source's expected number of openings times its survival through t−1 times h(s,t). The unresolved stock at t is the sum of expected surviving counts over all s≤t. Its probability conservation identity is checked for every original line.

One source story, then one of its annotation teams, receives equal weight in fitting. Divide each team's weight equally across observed lifetimes (resolution model) or visible lines (opening counts). Fit a regularized logistic hazard and a Poisson opening intensity. Learn question-word projections on source stories, using a deterministic 384-dimensional signed hash of German words, word pairs and character trigrams, and a ridge of .02. The teacher's wording is a training response. Use L2-normalized native representations from Qwen input embeddings, middle layer and final layer; concatenate the current visible line with a decaying (.75) past setting. At the current line include duration, source/target word-form characteristics, lexical overlap, native attention measures, and the present affinity relative to earlier recurrence. Use source-moment scaling and a .02 ridge on standardized hazard slopes. Give the entire additional 384-dimensional opening feature block the same prior squared scale as one lexical group by normalizing its standardized coordinates by the square root of its width. These choices were fixed before native forecasts or fitting. No item-specific threshold is tuned.

Independent L4 reruns preserved every original source position, word end, line and saved reader state bit for bit. Floating-point reductions in the saved traces and pairwise attention measures differed by at most 9.54 × 10⁻⁷ and 2.38 × 10⁻⁷, respectively. Before fitting any outcome, the ingestion test was corrected to require exact coordinate and state agreement and 10⁻⁶ absolute and relative numeric tolerance on these reductions. The original archive supplies all shared measures and the second archive supplies only the additional semantic summaries.
