# Recoverable T5-Large specialist after an interrupted full fine-tune

The original source-only full-parameter T5-Large job disappeared after 100 verified optimizer updates and before a development epoch, checkpoint or movie forecast reached local storage. No complete T5 comparison was available from that run. This registered revision fits a parameter-efficient specialist to the same observations. The published full-parameter span-generation system remains a separate historical reference. [Full-fine-tuning protocol](transition-t5-protocol.md) · [Original T5 comparator and task units](transition-snac-prior-model.md)

Use the same source sentence reconstruction, 2,938 training sentences, 716 development sentences and 1,661 movie sentences. Keep the verified google-t5/t5-large revision, fixed seed, 1,024-input/80-output limits, greedy generation, token-level cross-entropy, Adam settings, 1e−4 learning rate, effective batch of eight, five epochs and development-NLL selection. Freeze the base weights in BF16. Add rank-eight, scale-two LoRA updates to the query and value matrices of all 24 encoder self-attention, 24 decoder self-attention and 24 decoder cross-attention blocks. Optimize only the resulting 2,359,296 FP32 parameters. Dynamic microbatches fit at most 3,072 padded encoder tokens and eight examples. A worst-source-length training preflight measures memory on the actual L4, reducing the budget to 2,048 or 1,024 only when necessary to stay below the registered 21 GiB ceiling. Record the chosen budget and preserve it on resumption.

After initialization and every 25 optimizer steps, atomically save a compact checkpoint containing the current and best adapter, Adam moments, schedule position, partially accumulated epoch statistics and PyTorch CPU/CUDA RNG states. Alternate two slots and publish the SHA-256 digest of the active checkpoint. The external controller preserves the newest verified file; a resumed allocation validates its source, base, protocol and optimizer position before continuing. Verify restoration with two identical provisional updates and discard both before scientific fitting. Keep the movie outputs entirely hidden until the five source epochs and best-epoch restoration complete. Final evaluation uses the fixed original word/span mapping and the previously declared Qwen/NER comparisons. These are matched comparisons to the adapted LoRA-trained specialist. They do not benchmark the inaccessible original authors’ item split.
