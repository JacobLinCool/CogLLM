# Estimating separate responses to the same continuation

Use the published Michaelov norms in their original direction, from 1 (very plausible) to 5 (very implausible). Each is an item-level rating repeated in the archive across electroencephalographic rows. Count each sentence once for this endpoint. For the EEG, average the available predefined electrodes within participant and stimulus before calculating an item mean. Score each context equally. For the five Szewczyk studies learn a baseline-voltage slope from training participants' trials in the corresponding source study without giving this covariate to the text model. For baseline-corrected Michaelov readings no such slope is needed. For the timed endpoint use each sentence's mean log time across recorded readers; the released dependent variable already combines the critical word with two spillover words and trims observations. Report available trial counts separately from unique materials.

Compare form (log length, prior exposure of the observed word, current prefix index, word frequency, and number of native subwords), native Qwen full word surprisal added to form, and the full array of specified single-pass expectation features. Publish three additional form-plus-surprisal controls, using the original authors' fixed Pythia 2.8B, RWKV 3B and Mamba 2.8B predictions at the identical word. These models are comparators and do not enter the CogLLM feature set. At each held context compare all pairs with different human values; compute leave-context estimates and 10,000 paired bootstrap draws over contexts. Report mean squared error, mean absolute error and the rank ordering separately. Each EEG study remains its own outcome. For across-study EEG transfer, exclude shared word sequences or indistinguishable sentence stems from the source of that test fold; reserve trained within-study comparisons on separate context groups. Known timings and plausibility grades are not sampled at arbitrary subword positions. Unlabeled subwords have no item loss. These source and estimator details were finalized before reading any held model scores.
