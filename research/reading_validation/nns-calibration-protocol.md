# Can an editor interpret the percentages over English words literally?

The optional English learner view displays the percentage of readers expected to mark a word as unknown in a single sentence. Ranking words within a sentence does not assess the magnitude of those percentages. This retrospective audit uses the same locked 200 sentences and the same model. The original experiment's ranking, proper scores and partial identification bounds are already public. No probability, training choice or word inclusion will change as a result of the audit. [Original validation](nns-results.md) · [Authors' study](https://aclanthology.org/C16-1069/)

Use each aligned, designated word and all its genuine responses. The pooled and proficiency-specific models make predictions on identical response rows. For pooled display collect six intervals for the original, unrounded probability: below 1%, 1–5%, 5–10%, 10–20%, 20–40% and 40% or higher. Report the count of words, materials, positive judgments and total judgments, mean predicted probability, empirical fraction, and the paired empirical-minus-predicted difference. Repeat over the proficiency-specific predictions and at each of the six actual CEFR levels. An overall mean alone cannot reveal whether high scores are overconfident. Calculate the overall and bin-specific errors as a ratio of sums of marks and responses; this estimates calibration for a randomly chosen recorded response in the relevant stratum. Also report the observed coverage of all prefix-certifiable printed words in each pooled-risk bin, and the rate of any mark among judged words, because the experiment presented only designated words for selection.

Take ten thousand ordinary bootstrap resamples of whole sentence material families with seed 215817, carrying all readers' votes, levels and words in a family together. For the stratum's rate and paired error, divide the weighted numerator by the weighted denominator anew within each resample. Label bin intervals descriptive when fewer than ten different sentence families contribute or when more than five percent of resamples are empty. These intervals quantify the uncertainty from the sampled sentences; the absence of persistent reader identifiers precludes sampling independent people across sentences. Do not treat a true zero mark as missing. Do not infer a human probability for printed words outside the study's survey locations.
