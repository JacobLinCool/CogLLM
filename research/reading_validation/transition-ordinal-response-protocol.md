# Learn the five-button human observation law across entire linguistic constructions

One possible referent distribution can yield different five-category reports. People can disagree with each other while each confidently chooses a side, or individual people can explicitly report ambiguity. The independent locked AmbiCoref study forecasts ambiguity without fitting any human ratings from that corpus. Keep that confirmation intact. This separate supervised experiment asks whether the ordinal questionnaire response can be decoded from the single-pass signals when the test construction is absent throughout training. Its design was written before receiving external native AmbiCoref forecasts. [Zero-shot confirmation](transition-anchored-reference-protocol.md) · [Original rating design](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-eacl.75/)

Factor the probability of one observed five-way response into three real choices. First predict the probability of choosing the ambiguity button. Among directional responses predict which option was chosen. Among responses with a chosen option predict whether it was expressed definitely or probably, with the chosen side supplied to the confidence decoder. The resulting five probabilities sum to one, and the three conditional binary losses sum exactly to a proper categorical log loss for each original response. Include all five labels in their original units. No proxy numeric certainty labels are generated.

Create four outer folds by original construction (Experiencer as embedded Object, Experiencer as embedded Subject, Implicit Causality, Transfer of Possession). Use author verb tables and verified stimulus patterns; disclose unsupported assignment. Give all material of the held family to testing. Repeated lexical skeletons and displayed sentence strings within that family never appear in supervised training. Also split questionnaire sheets deterministically by row number modulo four. For each material-family fold and each sheet fold, train only on responses from the other three constructions and other three questionnaire folds. All 16 fitted cells have completely held-out test material and questionnaire assignments; original worker identifiers spanning sheet rows are absent from the public source. Retain only cells with demonstrated support. Score every human response once, average within sentence then across sentences, give all four constructions equal weight in a sensitivity, and use group-wise uncertainty over held sheets and linguistic skeletons. Four construction-level results remain primary evidence about replication; item intervals cannot replace the four independent construction groups.

The fixed controls are a training-only five-category frequency prior, a direct two-way belief-to-questionnaire observation law using only the final anchored referent log odds and their absolute value, and a surface/step observation law using visible length, grammatical position, candidate lengths and the pretrained lexical, step and pronominal preference controls. The primary observer adds the native click prior, the source-trained semantic and native attention changes between the pronoun and final word, their absolute magnitudes and directional disagreement, and the largest intermediate shift and remaining discrepancy with the final reading. No model learns text-, scholar-, or family-identity lookup tables. Use ridge .01 with standardized slopes and unpenalized intercepts, logistic estimation on model-specific observed subsets, proper categorical probabilities and maximum absolute source gradient 10⁻⁶. Report five-way log loss, multinomial Brier, ambiguity AUROC/AP, directional log loss and calibration versus the control arms in each complete held construction. Separate this cross-validated supervision result from untouched, source-only external evidence; no conditional or calibrated profile here promotes an ordinary author-facing score.
