# Original expert editing choices and earlier-phrase recall

This allocation shares one frozen Qwen3.5-2B snapshot between two outcome-blind studies. The first phase replays 55 original Frick stories as unmarked text with the previously registered nominal referent extraction. Its 10,730 printed word endpoints can be compared with independently archived reader reports on the earlier part of the same story. The second phase encodes 348 original and edited passages presented for independent expert comparison. The source expert-choice study retains all 100 original material families, the complete set of 300 comparisons and its own prospective scoring plan. No human choices, reader identities or study-outcome tables are sent to the accelerator. Each study supplies its own native receipt, content digest, observation geometry and calibrated source weights where applicable.
