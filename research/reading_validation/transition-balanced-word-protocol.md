# Balanced word screening for English narrative summaries

We will evaluate the two previously source-calibrated word policies on every admissible placement of the released SNaC character span annotations. The exposed movie titles also served earlier identification experiments. This is a retrospective examination of operating decisions on this cohort. Source book development data alone selected the word cutoff separately for the calibrated character head and NER-plus-surprisal.

A possible labeling chooses one exact occurrence per recorded reader span. Multiple selected spans contribute their union. Within each original segment we will enumerate all such labeling masks. Each movie word stays in its original position. For each method we will report sharp bounds on precision, recall, F1, and false flags per thousand exposed words. Paired differences use the same possible labeling for both predictions. For each possible true-positive-population size, dynamic programming will propagate extremes of linear true-positive contributions. We will summarize both the pooled population and the average of the 39 title-level differences, with whole-title bootstrap envelopes. Metrics with an empty denominator will score zero and their denominators will be disclosed. We will verify the enumeration against brute force on a small example and directly reproduce the released article-word probabilities and both flag decisions on a single ordinary encoder call per passage.
