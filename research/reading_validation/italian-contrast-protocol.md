# Training the common reading state to respond to lexical-identical order changes

The public Italian study supplies paragraph-level coherence ratings, and the English story study supplies actual local reader marks. Neither guarantees that a trained model will react to sentence order when facts and vocabulary stay fixed. Before opening new Italian or Danish native forecasts or human test grades, we registered one auxiliary source-restoration objective. Existing predictors and comparisons are frozen. The intervention supplies weak evidence about the order in a source document and offers no new individual human observations. [Source study](https://aclanthology.org/2023.clicit-1.40/) · [Observed source geometry and held restoration procedure](italian-order-interventions.md) · [Human observation models and split](coherence_order_protocol.md)

**Materials.** In the *official Italian training portion*, 62 originals and 51 authentic two-sentence transpositions reproduce all four author sentences verbatim. Retain the pre-existing source-group fit and development roles of their observed original passages. For each intact original generate a single source-hash-selected transposition using the same deterministic algorithm that selected the held interventions. For each observed transposition recover the actual original order. Each pair contains exactly the same four sentence strings, including punctuation. Audit the 113 alternative texts against every target-language held passage and its known alternative. Exclude a source group from auxiliary training if its material shares twelve consecutive normalized words with a held text. Exclude source training pairs that share a document or such direct material with development variants. Use only the original article and its counterfactual as text inputs to the backbone, one pass each.

**Training.** Use the already specified native features, source normalization, source splits, actual human losses, source weighting and penalties of the fully shared bilingual fit. Denote the fitted contextual difficulty before either human observation link by d(t)=x(t)β. The article score is H=log(mean(exp(d(t)))) on the source segmentation. Add a single auxiliary loss of weight 0.25. Its first component minimizes log(1+exp(−[H(swapped)−H(canonical)])) on source training pairs. Its second component applies the identical paired logistic objective to d at the *first disrupted noninitial sentence* compared with the identical sentence in the original context. Require exact native sentence coordinates, a predecessor in both contexts, and a complete scored word for the localized component. Give each component half of the auxiliary weight, normalize both by log(2), and weight original source documents equally within each component. An identical-word pair supplies one structural observation even if both of its versions have many sentence boundaries. Use one optimization with the existing ridge and scaled human losses. Retain the independent native fit, the old joint fit, and this structurally supervised joint fit. No tuning of auxiliary weight or feature sets on targets. Adapt the mixture of the independent and augmented joint estimates using the true corresponding development-set human loss. Verify analytic derivatives and convergence by the pre-existing thresholds.

**Tests.** Compare the augmented and original adaptive predictors on the already specified 200 Italian human-rated passages, original English test stories, and independent English essay marks. With frozen Italian normalization and calibration, assess the augmented predictor on the external authentic Danish within-domain ordered human judgments. Separately compare correct original-order choices and same-sentence first-disrupted-link performance on the certified word-identical held interventions, retaining the original and deliberately perturbed subsets. Require separately (a) reduction in Italian human rating MSE over the original adaptive model, (b) improved correct ordering of lexical-identical held pairs, and (c) retroactive/independent cross-language shared evidence. Give paired cluster intervals and a simultaneous interval covering the four core superiority contrasts (Italian human error, English incremental loss, Danish human concordance, Italian structurally recovered choices). Report each numeric result including stagnation and regressions. A gain in web-source ordering alone cannot show that the model tracks reader difficulty.
