# Estimator for the noun-phrase intervention

This implementation specification precedes fitting the new study or comparing its item means to any forecast. The published aggregate directions are known. Each family supplies one observation for each combination of split and pseudoword conditions. The five family folds balance each phrase-length stratum. Assign families in SHA-256 order of a fixed prefix plus family ID, round-robin within each stratum. Verify that identical case- and punctuation-normalized sentence strings occur only within a fold. Material overlap with earlier fitting sources will be reported separately for every full or twelve-consecutive-word match and strong five-gram matches to the actually used heads. In case of overlap with an actual source-trained predictor, exclude the entire family from the primary comparison and list it.

The text baseline uses the same twelve length, frequency, and orthographic features as the frozen educational readout. The extended arm adds the expected educational grade, the mean and 90th percentile of CompLex scores over words with observed native end tokens, and the final 24 coordinates in the existing whitened educational projection. The intervention-only control uses indicators of the twelve complete experimental cells. Two additional models append the same cell indicators to the surface and extended designs. These arms describe how much can be learned when the intervention is known.

For each fold standardize continuous and indicator features using training sentences only, with unit divisor when variance is zero. The intercept equals the training outcome mean. Add the within-family-centered feature and response rows to the globally centered feature and response rows. The two blocks have equal total weight, with every family weighted equally. Solve the two-output squared loss in one closed-form ridge fit, with 0.05 times the squared slope norm per response and an unpenalized intercept. This coefficient applies to the mean squared loss on the concatenated blocks and is fixed across arms and folds. Preserve unconstrained continuous estimates, their range and training numerical residuals. At inference, neither variant partners nor known conditions are inputs to either text-only arm. The population-mean control returns the training mean of each outcome.

For an intervention, calculate each observed and predicted difference as changed minus unchanged, holding the other factor fixed. Form a signed-direction agreement per informative comparison and average over the other factor within family. Exclude a family from that contrast when its two observed differences are both tied. Bootstrap the family-level values within length strata using the registered seed and 10,000 joint draws, retaining the same draw for each estimator. For a stratum's interval bootstrap only that stratum. MSE is averaged first across the four variants and then across families. An endpoint's positive gain is comparator MSE minus estimated-model MSE. Include a secondary contrast-MSE diagnostic in native squared units by replacing each trial by its within-family change. The zero-shot educational outputs retain expected educational-grade units; the frozen lexical score is a 0–1 lexical difficulty index.

A source sentence's modified cloze question or its alternatives never enter the model. Each test sentence contributes one prediction in original units for each trained response. Selecting a response head is a choice of scientific endpoint. The two heads share text features, and a multi-output ridge with separable losses has the same solution as two ridge fits with those features.
