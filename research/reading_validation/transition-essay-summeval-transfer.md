# Frozen essay observers for independent whole-draft judgments

The complete external protocol controls source-story grouping, thresholds, comparator selection and testing. This component contributes two previously trained essay observers. The independent data owner retained all item ratings before feature extraction. The readout averages a fixed model for each of eight source-topic rotations, then averages the complement of sentence incoherence risk across certified, noninitial stops of a complete news summary. The paired control applies the same aggregation to the source-trained position, lexical and surprisal features. A draft with no certified eligible boundary supplies no essay score. Competing drafts must both be scorable. [Registered external outcomes and group split](summeval-protocol.md) · [Source measurement and results](transition-essay-reasons-results.md)

The native head reads the same stored middle and final states, native token relationships and token log losses as the other study heads. The source essay model saw its original assigned topic before the essay. The external news-summary observer sees the original summary display string alone. Each noninitial score uses only original tokens through that sentence and requires token identity with the corresponding standalone prefix. The source ensemble's per-fold native and lexical probabilities replayed every one of the 1,352 archived held essay stops within 7.2 × 10⁻¹¹. A text-only overlap audit compared all 1,548 distinct raw news strings with the complete earlier CoheSentia and DECOR extraction packet. Casefolding and Unicode word normalization produced 1,511 and 796 distinct word sequences and 41,300 and 88,706 distinct contiguous 12-word sequences. Exact full-text and 12-word intersection sizes were both zero. This audit cannot identify which text appeared in pretraining of the frozen backbone. [SummEval release](https://github.com/Yale-LILY/SummEval)

On the locked 50-story confirmation cohort, the imported essay observer ranked the higher expert-coherence summary in 53.65% of unequal within-story choices (95% interval 50.46–56.87%). Its matched lexical observer reached 52.77%. The paired gain of 0.88 percentage points had a simultaneous interval of −1.01 to +2.79 points. The comparison used 1,692 eligible noninitial boundaries in 753 distinct scorable drafts. A separate observer fitted on summary ballots reached 70.00%, against 70.31% for a matched form and word-probability observer.
