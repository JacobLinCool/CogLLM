# Learning causal antecedents in plain prose

We fitted a 1,930-coefficient readout that compares a new statement with earlier sentences or earlier spans. The frozen backbone processed each original sequence once, with genuine causal attention across the available prefix. Training used human-authored causal choices from e-CARE, with a hash-selected alternative order and additional two-statement endorsement, foil and repetition views. COPA was held out as a separate study. Its correct choices measure which pair of events is more causally plausible. Human token-by-token difficulty was unobserved. ## What changed when the source of a question was known?

The same fitted weights were replayed on historical, author-constructed contrasts. Each earlier question-like event had a short annotated source, such as the phrase “a sharp vibration” within a longer statement. Supplying that span allowed the model to locate both the early and the late explanatory cue in all twelve families. The released model previously located six of twelve. Letting the model instead track each complete earlier sentence located both timing contrasts in zero of twelve.

For sixteen branches with two simultaneous issues, supplied spans produced the first separation inside the cue in ten branches. Five met all four branch criteria for timing, sham-adjusted magnitude, correct-target advantage and limited collateral response. No family passed on both branches. With automatic whole-sentence sources, two branches separated inside the cue and none met all four criteria. Searching across the complete automatic source inventory led to twelve abstentions across the sixteen branches. The small set was used in earlier research cycles. Its results identify a mechanism worth testing prospectively, particularly the need to estimate the span of the open concept. [All timing tests and full-candidate diagnostics](../results/reading-validation-causal/historical-scores.json)

The e-CARE source supplied 8,993 independent training items and 1,007 calibration items. The largest final objective-gradient component was below 0.000008 across the three selected heads. Calibration used 479 training-source human foils or generated repetitions; the 90th percentile of their maximum apparent evidence gain was 0.471. We did not infer a human confusion threshold from these foils. The inference pass used middle/final states and native global attention, and the largest numerical discrepancy against the native unbatched implementation or truncated prefix was 0.00000245. The 29,973 distinct passages contained 742,620 tokens, processed in 1,249 batches plus three technical forwards.
