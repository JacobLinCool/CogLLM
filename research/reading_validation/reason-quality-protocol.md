# Does sufficient explanation require a different observation from an effective sentence?

A high effectiveness grade and a reader's decision that an argument contains enough evidence are operationally different. The source-trained PERSUADE effectiveness score transferred poorly to the Darmstadt argument-sufficiency panel. This prompted the following new primary question: does directly estimating evidence sufficiency transfer to the *local sufficiency* judgments of three other experts reading arguments from online debates? The Darmstadt observations have already been studied in the project; the Dagstuhl expert panel and Webis logical comparisons have no inspected CogLLM scores. [Original Darmstadt study](https://aclanthology.org/E17-1092/) · [Expert ratings of argument quality](https://webis.de/data/dagstuhl-15512-argquality.html) · [Pairwise logical quality judgments](https://webis.de/data/webis-argument-quality-20.html)

Recover every original expert rating of local sufficiency, arrangement and clarity for the primary expert corpus and verify its mean against the author archive. The first endpoint asks whether premises warrant the conclusion, the second whether the text is arranged well, and the third whether the text is clear. Each is observed for the entire passage and cannot supervise a particular word. Fit the source on the original authentic Darmstadt paragraph and on published Webis comparisons of logical argument quality (with an explicit tie category). For the source paragraph use individual yes/no votes where available and the final published binary decision separately where independent individual responses are unavailable. Never count a majority and its three underlying votes as four independent answers. No test votes enter the estimator. Assess the direct source transfer to the completely held expert corpus by human ordinal mean rank and by ordering paragraphs within a topic. The calibration from the source binary probability to a native three-level scale is unknown; do not interpret the binary probability as an expert ordinal rating. Include a second panel holding out entire debate topics for weakly supervised adaptation from source paragraph judgments to same-scale expert measures. Declare separately any labels used in the adaptation. The people assessed completed arguments; their observations contain no subword reading time.

Use each original passage once as a causal Qwen input. The model receives no argument type, assigned grade, topic ID, audience guess, or test conclusion supplied separately. At the sentence end summarize its words and their probabilities and its relation to all prior sentences. Surface controls must include argument length, number of sentences, marker words, lexical overlap, and within-argument relations. Compare native features with equal source material and the same head. A paired source vote trains the difference of the two independent article scores. Learn the source-specific scale and tie propensity of this comparison; do not average incompatible stars and pairwise wins. When estimating a shared idea of sufficiency from paired logical quality and directly rated sufficiency, allow an additional task-specific deviation and compare with separately trained readouts. Keep the number of directly measured source topics, withheld test topics, texts, exact overlaps, and independent raters visible. The test vocabulary and expert judgments predate this backbone; pretraining exposure is unknown. The public archive excludes some of the 320 texts described by the original article, so the number of recovered records and unique arguments governs the reported denominator.
