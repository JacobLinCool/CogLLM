# Does a good Italian coherence estimate largely distinguish TED speech from Wikipedia?

The original investigators report higher coherence ratings for Wikipedia than for TED talks across perturbations, and a subsequent study describes genre as more influential than the construction perturbation. That later team obtained genre and perturbation metadata outside the public Task 2 release. Before opening the 200 held Italian human scores, we will measure how much of the available signal can be accounted for by the article's source genre. The original model, splits and primary comparisons remain fixed. [Human experiment](https://aclanthology.org/2023.clicit-1.40/) · [Separate model study and unreleased metadata](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-3877/paper12.pdf)

1. Recover the official Task 1 *prompts* from their named TED and Wikipedia source files. The training prompts contain three printed sentences. The separately supplied target can come from elsewhere and contributes no tokens to this audit. Compare Task 2 passages with those prompts using original shared strings of twelve case-folded words. Infer a source genre from direct material overlap only when (i) at least 24 target-word positions are covered by matching twelve-word spans from a single Task 1 prompt, and (ii) all such matches agree on the genre. Recover a source document only from uniquely matching document IDs in Task 1 training prompts; Task 1 test IDs are also anonymous. Count how many target documents can actually be linked, and whether matched test items now share a document. The main official 200-passage result and the group sensitivity remain distinguishable.
2. Train a genre classifier solely on the authors’ Task 1 source-labeled training prompts. Remove every Task 1 source-document group whose prompt shares twelve consecutive words with any Task 2 test material or a Task 1 blind-test prompt. Do not use coherence ratings, target sentences or externally guessed genre labels. Use a regularized English/Italian-independent logistic bag of Italian words and word bigrams, a fixed training vocabulary, sublinear frequency, source-sample inverse document frequency, and per-prompt length normalization. Validate the classifier on the official Task 1 blind-test prompts with known source genres. Report held classification accuracy, balanced accuracy, log loss and counts. Grade its Task 2 genre predictions separately where direct links identify genre, quantifying coverage.
3. Compute a style reference that predicts mean Task 2 human coherence from the article's Task 1 genre probability. Estimate its intercept and slope on the same Task 2 fitting groups as the primary study. A matched extension adds the independent form model's completed passage estimate with source-only calibration, using training-group out-of-fold form predictions if the model is refitted. Keep these controls outside the primary model weights. Compare forecast MSE and Spearman to the native representation model on exactly the same 200, and calculate the rank correlation between observed coherence and model coherence **within each genre**. Use directly identified genre when available; report predicted-genre analyses separately with the validation error and threshold (posterior ≥0.5). If either recovered group has fewer than ten documents, label its result descriptive. Coverage of Task 1 genre cues and accuracy for reader coherence are distinct endpoints. Estimating the response to a particular sentence reversal still requires source intervention metadata or direct paired judgments.
