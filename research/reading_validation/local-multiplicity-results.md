# Eleven of 32 comparisons retain a positive interval across the word and revision studies

Fourteen directional comparisons had a positive lower 95% limit when assessed individually. **Eleven** retained a positive lower limit in the 32-comparison Bonferroni audit. They came from **six source collections**: PLABA, Kerwer, Guo, the Agrawal–Carpuat OneStopQA response study, the Paetzold unknown-word study and SemEval-2012. Several comparisons reuse the same people or texts. Their number therefore counts surviving contrasts, while their scientific meaning depends on the measurement and the reference. The synthesis is retrospective for the first 29 comparisons; all 32 were listed before the SemEval target was opened. Three initially positive differences no longer had a strictly positive lower limit after multiplicity correction. On SimpDA, rank agreement over the trained editing comparator changed by +9.9 points [−1.0, 17.9]. Selecting both winners when simplicity and preservation opposed each other changed by +13.3 points [−0.1, 24.9]. On the Paetzold substitution task, the shared contextual fit improved proper log loss over a like-for-like independent fit by 0.00103 nats [−0.00045, 0.00253]. The two-task sharing experiment met its original per-task criteria. The broader simultaneous audit retains evidence of improved unknown-word loss and leaves the gain in substitution loss uncertain. On ReLay, cross-study sharing for simplicity had an ordinary interval spanning zero; on EMTeC the stock added no reliable information beyond native word probabilities and other readouts on either registered human endpoint. All intervals in the table are paired and use the original study's resampling units. Tail probabilities are 0.05/(2 × 32) = 0.00078125. We computed wider intervals for every individually positive estimate and all controls within their designated replay groups. For the remaining rows where the ordinary interval already included zero, interval nesting suffices to rule out a positive adjusted lower bound. A blank wider interval in the data table indicates that decision. The 10,000 bootstrap draws place roughly eight draws in each extreme tail. Twelve PLABA components, twelve Kerwer texts and eleven Guo readers limit precision even with many observations per unit. The audit covers these 32 contrasts, with further researcher degrees of freedom across the wider project.
