# Measurement model for argumentative links and judgments of support

Source records are fitted using the authorized public training essays and the registered topic split. Completed sentences are obtained by punctuation or printed paragraph breaks. The previous sentence and the set of every earlier sentence are always candidates. The conditional choice likelihood is the negative log probability of the union of acceptable earlier sentences. A second binary likelihood estimates whether a sentence ends any annotated backward link. A third three-category cumulative logit likelihood uses one observation per human-rated argumentative component ending at the current sentence. We give each contributing training essay equal total weight within each measurement. Source annotations select outcomes; the model's inputs are the untouched essay, source-trained inverse document frequencies, visible surface features and the frozen encoder. The text-only and native arms have identical source cases. Fit with a fixed .01 slope penalty on standardized inputs, positive ordered threshold spacing and no penalty on the intercept. Verify finite forecasts and convergence numerically before unsealing the held response grades.

The surface comparator includes relative position, sentence and paragraph distance, shared content words weighted by inverse document frequency, opening and argumentative markers, and recent sentence recurrence. The additional native predictors comprise causal middle/final states, pooled lexical embeddings, current-to-earlier attention, similarity and aligned products between sentence vectors. The end of a multi-sentence component identifies the finished observation. We grade retrieval with the marked child's sentence supplied and, separately, rank all eligible sentence positions by estimated link probability; joint predictions also require their selected earlier parent to be correct. For independent structural evaluation report support links alone, attack links separately and observed backward links collectively. A fully unlocalizable annotated child precludes treating every other position as a known negative in that essay. Where several rated components end together, preserve every rating.

The PERSUADE effectiveness scale has levels ineffective, adequate, effective. Transfer to Darmstadt averages the three-level model's expected normalized ineffectiveness across the original body paragraph's sentences. Evaluate ranking of the independently judged insufficient paragraphs and individual votes; the source estimate does not have probability units for this new endpoint. Include paragraph length and prevalence. Grade the original argument corpus as a common material panel for the two Darmstadt outcomes, with essay-cluster intervals and explicit quote/paragraph match counts. The argumentative essay paper is [Stab and Gurevych, 2017](https://aclanthology.org/J17-3005/); this corrects its URL in the hash-registered protocol. The published insufficient count is 348 of 1,029 arguments. A non-empty sufficiency-violation annotation denotes insufficient support. [Sufficiency study](https://aclanthology.org/E17-1092/)
