# Which two sentences make a story physically inconsistent?

On 351 human-written edits of everyday stories, CogLLM found both the earlier action and the later conflict in **55.0%** of cases, averaged over the 153 official held story families. Word surprisal with trained surface controls gave **38.9%**. The paired advantage was 16.1 percentage points, with a 95% family-bootstrap interval of 10.5 to 22.1 points. The input was one unmarked story. A sentence received its score when the reader reached its end. These coordinates come from human authors who constructed and annotated the conflicts. For 193 edits the authors changed an earlier sentence and kept the later breakpoint word for word. In those cases the calibrated model raised the probability of the correct source–breakpoint event in 90.5% of the family-weighted comparisons, versus 74.4% for surprisal. At the chosen operating point, it also flagged **33.3%** of the 153 unedited stories. Returning the top pair on every story and deciding whether any pair merits a flag are different editorial decisions. Across 35 gradable attribute–time combinations, predicting known physical conditions before and after actions yielded .244 nats per observed binary state versus .463 for the training prevalence of that condition. The difference was −.219 [−.247, −.192]. Of 77,594 released attribute entries, 77,196 aligned to printed words. A missing value incurred no truth loss. The state predictor used three source-family folds to generate out-of-fold training inputs to the event readout. Four official test families shared a long passage or multiple sentences with training or development. Excluding those four left the joint-location result at 55.0% versus 38.4% on 149 families. The audit examines supervised-study overlap. Backbone pretraining exposure is unknown. ## Transfer to people reading for inconsistencies

In the independent third Frick cohort, 233 readers saw two versions of the same 12 story families. Changing an earlier specification raised first reports on the identical later sentence by 31.7 percentage points. A report model using the household physical-state predictions estimated −0.1 points [−0.3, 0.1]. Its full first-response-path loss was 1.805 nats, versus 1.502 for the earlier model calibrated to those readers' task on separate families and cohorts. Recognizing that water has frozen supports a specific editorial capability. It does not establish how often a person notices a later reference to a different object. [Reader reporting model](state-reports-estimator.md) · [Individual-cohort endpoints](../results/reading-validation-state-transport/reports-native/scores.json)

The optional physical continuity check in the English Gradio app shows the highest-ranked earlier and later sentence together when the story exceeds the development threshold. It accepts five to seven sentences about everyday actions. On ten registered stories and one original example the production route used exactly 11 encoder forwards. Independent prefix checks changed earlier probabilities by at most 2.7×10⁻⁶.
