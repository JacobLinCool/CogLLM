# Observation and comparator details before scoring

These decisions were recorded after examining the public file schemas and before any model forecasts or test comparisons. We retain the original local protocol and digest.

The authors provide raw-scale 1–5 item means for the 160 families in `analysis/160_coh_ratings.csv`. The same file has conditions e and f added for their third experiment. We use only a–d from Experiment 1. The preprocessed `exp1_prereg_data.csv` already excludes readers and trials under the authors' criteria. We verify that each target-word string and region aligns with that version's stimulus. We follow the authors' word-time winsorisation at two sample standard deviations from each participant × condition mean, take the mean of log milliseconds within reader and region, then average across readers within each variant. To preserve outcome separation, we estimate the clipping limits using calibration families alone and apply those limits to both partitions. A test-only preprocessing sensitivity may be listed as secondary if performed.

Three additional comparisons establish attribution. We report the training mean, the four lexical controls plus ordinary Qwen surprisal, and the integration readout without the Gradio transition column. These are secondary comparisons. Ablation features and sources remain fixed before testing. Correct signs are defined as lower scores in the low-coherence context for ratings, and longer predicted times in that context for word reading. Because every target has high and low contextual versions, an intercept or conditional prior cannot separate the pair. A data file retaining only positive named difficulty spans would not support these false alarm metrics.
