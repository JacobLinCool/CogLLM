# Reconciling Provo's reported response counts

All original forecasts and source fits had been locked before this audit. The first score attempt stopped on a mismatch between the sum of published alternative counts and the stated number of respondents. Checking every original word ID before comparing any scores found 769 such discrepancies among 2,688 released positions. Summed counts exceed declared respondents by one at 665 positions, two at 83, three at 12, four at seven, five at one, and 21 at one. Example QID2211 has 42 counted responses and a denominator of 41. The original four-file archive supplies an aggregated predictability table. It lacks the individual norming records needed to identify which particular response is redundant or which denominator is incorrect. Distinct rows sometimes share a spelling. There is no basis for deciding which rows to delete by looking at model performance.

Use the aligned word positions whose visible alternative counts, including any blanks, sum exactly to the published number of respondents for the primary evidence. Retain only consecutive verified positions for measured word-local changes. Score the entire aligned table as a separately labeled sensitivity using the actual mass of the released rows and explicitly preserve discrepancies. Do not recreate an individual vote by arbitrarily dropping or fractionally reweighting a response. Compare across the same model forecasts in both analyses. Generate the predetermined illustration from all published counts and mark its denominator-discrepant points, so selection continues to depend on article geometry alone.
