# Joint measurement of cloze choices, expectability ratings and N400 negativity

The earlier shared rank-two model reduced prediction accuracy for the two behavioral outcomes. This follow-up asks whether heterogeneous observation laws and a shared covariance learned from source sentences improve performance without imposing a fixed low-dimensional response space. The previously published results on these 205 sentences are known. This is an internal method study. It also supplies a frozen-prior independent transfer test using an earlier Spanish EEG experiment, whose overall condition effects were known before this registration. No new participants or annotations are required.

Use the exact 1,546 positions shared by the original EEG presentation and the separately issued stimuli. Retain the original five document folds used in the earlier experiments. Cloze supplies a count of respondents failing to give the target. The public experiments used Laplace smoothing, and the investigators released the smoothed correct probability, the difference from the largest non-target probability, and entropy. For each possible sample size per list, solve for integer response counts and the number of distinct answers. The correct-answer count must be unique across every compatible integer partition. Treat a mean 1–5 rating as a Gaussian measurement with its published sampling variance and a learned between-item variance. Identify the rating sample sizes independently from the integer sum and sum of squares of the scale responses. Use separate Gaussian residual variance for the N400 aggregate and a beta-binomial likelihood for the bounded, overdispersed word choices. Never represent the same response multiple times by duplicating a scalar averaged label on native subwords.

Apply training-only scaling and principal components to the shared text representation; retain up to 24 standardized native directions, five scaled print features and causal Qwen whole-word surprisal, plus an intercept. Fold fits use the same predictor representation and training rows in every arm. Fit a fixed diagonal Gaussian prior, an empirical diagonal prior, and an empirical full-covariance prior over the three response-specific coefficient vectors. Include the source's small surface-plus-surprisal comparison. Full versus diagonal empirical priors determines whether learned inter-endpoint coupling improves prediction. Hyperparameters and covariance regularization are fixed before fitting. Estimate Gaussian noise scales, cloze concentration, and coefficients from training folds. Select uncertainty by resampling complete English sentences with all three outcomes preserved. Primary metrics are per-word held predictive log density on each measurement law, Fisher-free paired differences and per-endpoint effect intervals. Report the macro-average log-density gain and all three disaggregated effects, rank correlations and convergence checks. Compare to the unconstrained separate and rank-two models using their previously reported scale-specific results. Fix coefficients and abstain from newly estimating model parameters on external outcomes for any cross-language claim. Correctly measuring word surprisal, a choice distribution or an ERP remains distinct from identifying one person's unobserved doubt.
