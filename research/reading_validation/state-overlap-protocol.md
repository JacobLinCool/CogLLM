# Separating repeated wording from a novel physical story

The official TRIP split assigns each original story family to one partition. Before evaluating the recovered native forecasts, screen every ordinary held version and its original twin against all train and development articles. Compare complete normalized words and ordered twelve-word windows using the earlier study-exposure audit's normalization. Also look for at least two distinct exact original sentences of four or more words. Classify an entire held family as exposed if any of its original or edited articles satisfies any of these criteria against a development or training version. Report tested and excluded families and source IDs so the exclusion remains auditable. Use the published split for the primary estimate and recompute the article-only first-event metrics on all remaining families. When no family trips a rule, certify that this sensitivity grade coincides with the primary estimate. These literal comparisons cannot rule out paraphrases or unknown backbone pretraining.
