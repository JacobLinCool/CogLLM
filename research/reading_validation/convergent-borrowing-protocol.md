# Learning the amount of physiological information to borrow from other readers

The contextual model with empirical task covariance improved on separate contextual heads under a ten-sentence EEG budget but lost to calibrated surface features. Both results were known before this follow-up. We test a single model derived from this discrepancy. The question is whether prior directions learned from inexpensive English behavioral responses and a separately measured Spanish EEG cohort help recover the physiological response when the target lab observed only ten sentences. This is a method refinement on an examined English corpus. Confirmatory evaluation requires an additional acquisition or independent study.

Keep the existing sparse protocol’s five held-sentence splits, ten training EEG sentences in each split, complete behavioral training responses, exact printed-word inputs and signs. Preserve every test word. Enter the surface cues and whole-word native surprisal as fixed effects. Construct independent variance components from (1) residual contextual principal components, (2) the two word directions learned on the same fold’s observed English cloze and predictability responses, and (3) the frozen Spanish word-level N400 readout. Residualize each component against the six surface cues using training-sentence features only. Apply a unit trace-per-row normalization learned from those features. Estimate nonnegative variance components and measurement noise by Gaussian restricted marginal likelihood on the ten observed EEG sentences. Allow the fit to set a component variance to zero. Integrate fitted coefficient uncertainty in the held predictive variance. An independently fitted English EEG readout never enters its own inputs.

Use the full predeclared covariance model as the primary estimator. Compare it with identical fixed effects and residual context alone; with surface-only restricted maximum likelihood; and with the native physiological component reserved while English behavioral component remains available. As a temporal negative control replace the independent Spanish N400 direction by its earlier EEG window from the same texts and measured readers. Report each estimator’s held predictive log density, correlation, mean squared error and coverage by printed word. The primary contrast is the full contextual, behavioral and Spanish physiological covariance versus a surface calculation without random directions, over all 1,546 original aligned words. Also report full-versus-context residual and full-versus-behavior estimates. Resample original held sentences with paired forecasts. Interpret a positive internal study conditional on this fixed ten-sentence schedule. The external nine-laboratory reader study remains independent of these English model choices.
