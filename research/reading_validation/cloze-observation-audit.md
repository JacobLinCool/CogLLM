# Which cloze opportunities have an observed response?

The author-released processing pipeline counts one opportunity per retained respondent, including an empty spreadsheet entry. Our initial beta-binomial model reproduces that published opportunity denominator and estimates the probability of supplying the target word. A direct audit of all eight cloze and eight rating workbooks, including the authors’ spelling-correction tables and comprehension-based respondent exclusions, confirmed the exact target count and list size for all 1,726 words. The maximum discrepancy in rating means and standard deviations was \(1.34\times10^{-15}\). There were 18 blank entries among 135,923 full-source opportunities. On the 1,546 EEG-matched vocabulary positions there were 14 blank entries among 121,736 opportunities, hence 121,722 observed completions. Each blank concerned a different item. The audit retained item totals, anonymized list sizes and file hashes.
