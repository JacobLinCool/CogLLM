# Learn whether a reader would select a previous referent

An observer trained exclusively on preceding-referent clicks must allocate one unit of probability somewhere in the earlier text. Its .80 source threshold transferred to 91.6% conditional precision but only 54.6% when the reader was also allowed to decline a link. A separate factor can learn the missing human action: whether this player chose any preceding entity at all. The original broad gold result and GAP name result were known when this repair was registered. [Applicability audit](transition-pointer-unconditional-protocol.md) · [Game schema](https://github.com/dali-ambiguity/Phrase-Detectives-Corpus-2.1.4)

For every visible personal-pronoun position having at least four independent original decisions, retain DO, DN, PR and NR. Collapse a player's identical repeated choices, drop that player's conflicting repeats and exclude invisible or multiply located markables as before. Derive the fractional target proportion of players who chose a previous entity. Fit a binomial cross-entropy on this observed response, averaging players within pronouns, pronouns within each training work, and works equally. Exclude remaining observations from the loss rather than treating them as negative. Keep the existing 230 source training, 12 development and 44 gold texts disjoint by work. All gold prognoses are made before testing the new gate. No material from Jones and Bergen or GAP is used to fit either module.

Use one fixed positive-ridge logistic model per source information set: (1) a form/position/POS observer at the current pronoun, looking only backwards; (2) the same observer together with the complete frozen Qwen middle and final word-end states. Keep ridge .01 on standardized slopes, no penalty on the intercept, and native-block scaling sqrt(48/hidden-width) as in the [direct-state protocol](transition-direct-protocol.md). Require a maximum absolute source gradient below 10⁻⁶. Publish both fits and the source-development rate/intercept-only baseline. The gate for either observer multiplies its estimated probability of a previous-referent choice by the source pointer's unchanged maximum word probability. This product is a ranking score for a region hit, because multiple words can inhabit an individual clicked phrase.

Keep the forced link, original .80 pointer confidence and fixed product .80 as prespecified baselines. Calibrate the combined native and lexical policies independently on all source development choices, using the original nine-threshold grid, 15-query/four-work minimum, maximum-error bootstrap band, 70% precision target and highest-coverage tie-breaking rule. The measured precision counts a suggestion as wrong when a player chose DN, PR or NR. If no source threshold qualifies, that arm cannot be sold as a calibrated high-precision policy. Report forecasts on every source/test pronoun, uncovered positions, selected and unselected counts, proper Brier/log loss for the gate, broad gold coverage, selective PPV, false suggestions per 100 queries and sharp equal-word bounds over all offered positions. Compare the raw and gated observers at published operating points; do not select a gold threshold. Link conditionality and human reading effort remain separate constructs.
