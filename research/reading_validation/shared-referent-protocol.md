# Learning where a reference points and how its interpretation changes

Can an article-only model use the same representation of earlier text to explain both a reader's antecedent click and the effect of a later disambiguating word? This follow-up uses the previously examined official source and target studies. Their earlier results motivated the analysis. No new-reader or blind discovery claim attaches to these targets.

## Observations and estimators

Phrase Detectives supplies actual linked clicks. At a pronoun, the probability of each clicked range is the sum of a categorical distribution over every earlier word. Weight votes within a pronoun, then equalize pronouns within their source work and equalize works. Definite Pronoun Resolution supplies pairs of authored sentences that reverse the intended antecedent. Each answer supervises only the two alternatives, at the end of that sentence. Give alternatives their log probability mass and include a matched-twin reversal term. No other preceding word receives a negative label from this source. Retain the original official source, development and test groups. Exclude a fixed hash-selected fifth of authored training pairs from optimization for a source-only diagnostic.

For an earlier candidate word s, a pronoun q, and the latest word t, keep a frozen human-click prior at q and estimate two small bilinear corrections. The first connects the word representation at q to the earlier candidate. The second connects the change in representation from q to t to the same candidate. A joint model penalizes disagreements between the two candidate mappings. The private comparator estimates a click mapping on direct clicks and a revision mapping on authored choices with the click mapping held fixed. A separate carried-prior comparator has zero revision weights. Fit only source observations with double-precision gradient checks and report the optimizer residual. If the target assessments favor different estimators, retain all results.

Use a fixed random sign sketch of middle and final word-end representations. Learn a 24-dimensional centered and scaled basis on source words only. Native attention and contextual similarity at q enter through the original source-only click prior. Inference requires one causal Qwen3.5-2B exposure per article and no generated answer. The signed GPU packet contains only text and observable word boundaries. Capture for every eligible pronoun determined by spelling. Gold ranges, pair membership, response alternatives and outcomes are inaccessible to the extractor.

## Evaluation

On held click articles, report the probability assigned to true clicked ranges and whether the highest-scoring word lies inside. On the official authored twins, report signed first-change direction on equal-length identical-prefix pairs, terminal two-choice likelihood, and unrestricted earlier-word top hit. At the last unchanged word the two interpretations must be numerically unchanged. On the independent Jones reader experiment, score each probability against the individual response, with item and reader uncertainty. Compare each new forecast with the original source-only click prior and with the earlier strongest source-trained forecasts available for the same endpoint. On AmbiCoref, if available in the window, also compare with the human five-category responses under the earlier experiment's category rule. Archive code, source fit, all decisions and unsuccessful contrasts. These associations measure reference interpretation, a specific part of reading comprehension.
