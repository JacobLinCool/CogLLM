# Does word-level semantic integration transfer to natural reading in a different language and laboratory?

Train one causal, multivariate wordwise readout using visibly aligned Spanish word EEG. The source contains 43 readers and 432 printed contexts with 21 positions. For each article, frozen last and middle projections, native whole-word surprisal, the source model's opening, closure and pending outputs, and causal change in contextual states are available in a single forward pass. Use the automatically filtered 300–500 ms negative voltage at each word as the primary training response and 100–200 ms as a prespecified specificity control. The earlier localized Spanish group contrast was known. English response values have not been inspected at registration. Only filenames, header fields, experimental design and original printed lexical geometry have been examined.

Apply the Spanish coefficients without any updates to 205 independent English novel sentences from the UCL corpus and to noninitial word positions with linked measured responses. Frank et al. measured voltage in 24 English readers. A separate study collected wordwise human cloze probabilities and explicit judgments. The authors' licensed combined table includes original N400, other ERP components, cloze, judgment and behavioral means, corpus word frequency and multiple source language-model surprisal controls. Compare surface word length, absolute word position and piece counts; these plus true Qwen word surprisal; and the richer frozen contextual readout. Train a ridge model on the Spanish EEG only, with all Spanish contexts, fixed training-only feature scaling and penalties. English scale and centering may differ, so primary transfer is rank correlation with higher English N400 negativity. Bootstrap complete English sentences; within-sentence median Spearman and an ordinary sentence-demeaned association control passage between effects. Report trained model minus Qwen word surprisal and the best supplied published model, including uncertainty and conditional correlations. Preserve the behavioral responses as separate tests. External signal directions follow the original released definitions, verified in source analysis scripts before scoring. If the public aggregate table supplies standardized rather than raw microvolts, report ranks and identify units without assigning microvolt values.

As a second, distinct instrument, train a joint response model on held-in English sentences for the three observed endpoints (actual N400, human cloze surprisal and the same second cohort's rated predictability). Each outcome uses native units/explicit missingness, training-only transformations and scale factors. Compare separate, shared reduced-rank, and the analytic full-rank regularized fits under five disjoint sentence folds. Fit all normalization within each training fold. Use the same prespecified model family for surface, Qwen surprisal plus surface, pretrained GPT2–XL plus surface, and states plus Qwen surprisal and surface. Demanding tests require improvement over both human-insensitive baselines on independent sentence groups. Report negative results, including how much of the measured N400 can be explained by human cloze and frequency baselines not available from running article text. No article-only result in this project identifies the precise subjective instant an individual no longer feels confused.
