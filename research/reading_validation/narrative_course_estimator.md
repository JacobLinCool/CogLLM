# Original-story estimators and reporting units

Train only on the other three stories for each forecast. Use fixed standardized linear readouts with ridge 0.02 for pairwise log odds and 0.1 for continuous outcomes. In the contrast between two open issues the previously annotated starting lines enter as candidate indices. The model scores every earlier line at every completed line. Estimate each training contrast's importance successively by giving equal weight to each story, annotation team, closure line, resolved question and eligible competing question. Compare trained form and full semantic models, the strict recency rule and the mass the unmodified joint reader resolves from the candidate source throughout the current line. Use fractional credit for ties. Additionally ask whether the highest-scoring source among *all* earlier lines corresponds to any confirmed closure's opening line, conditional only on evaluation at a closing line. This second endpoint removes the supplied competing pair.

For each certified lexical word, measure visible counts and word form, reuse of the word in the current prefix, and word/line surprisal; then add the four deployed readout channels, native attention summaries and the two learned jointly trained projections at that position. Give source stories equal weight. For suspense, fit to the across-reader standardized mean at each word plus differences of adjacent lines which both have word ratings. Each study-level loss uses its training-only response variance, with equal weight assigned to levels and line differences. Fit one continuous readout so the predicted difference is always the change of that same curve. Score the original graph and the new readouts by word-level Spearman correlation and correlation of successive line differences. Treat the four original stories as the number of independent materials. Include split-half reader agreement and resampling of complete screens to show measurement variability within each story.

For outstanding questions, regress interval distance at the end of an annotated line. Count definitely open questions for the lower bound and add all uncertain-open ones for the upper bound. Use equal study/team/line weights and a training-only feature scale. Project predictions to the nonnegative number line at evaluation. The original joint model's curve has a distinct measurement scale and is shown both directly and with a source-only affine count calibration. Conditional source ranking only supports claims about annotated opening lines. Two independent questions that share an opening line are indistinguishable under this choice of source unit.
