# How many author-facing character alerts can be trusted?

The default threshold fixes a 5% false-alarm target on development books. Each flagged movie word currently has a 34% chance of matching a reported character error. An author may want fewer suggestions with more evidence per alert. This additional operating-point study uses the released Qwen word readout, the same 88 training books, 22 development books from 11 book titles, and the known 39 external movies. All movie forecasts and the earlier default result have been seen. No movie label enters threshold selection.

Form a threshold grid from the unlabeled training-book scores at quantiles 0.80, 0.85, 0.90, 0.925, 0.95, 0.96, 0.97, 0.98, 0.985, 0.99, 0.9925, 0.995, 0.9975, and 0.999. A score must strictly exceed the candidate threshold. Compute development precision and recall using unambiguous human labels, counting at most one positive per word. Accept only thresholds with at least 20 flagged words from at least five different book titles. Resample the 11 complete development titles in 10,000 joint draws, including both generated versions of each title. Construct a one-sided simultaneous lower precision bound by subtracting from each observed precision the 95th percentile of the maximum centered downward deviation over the eligible thresholds; bootstrap draws with no alerts take precision zero for this calculation. Truncate bounds at zero. The bounds are approximate with 11 resampling units.

Among eligible thresholds whose lower bounds are at least 0.70, select the greatest observed development recall, breaking ties by the higher lower bound then the higher threshold. If none qualifies, this procedure does not authorize a 70%-precision mode. Apply the source-selected policy once to the original movie word cohort. Report clustered uncertainty in precision, recall, F1 and false alarms, flags per 1,000 eligible words and summaries receiving any flag. Report the fixed default on this identical sample. A source precision guarantee does not transfer automatically across the book/movie shift.
