# A steadily rising line can follow suspense while missing where readers changed their minds

Four complete German stories offer two observations on identical prose. Annotators marked each line at which a specific plot question was open or provisionally open. Separate readers drew a suspense curve over all the words visible on each screen. We preserved the complete originals, including one 12,425-token story, and scored each story in a single forward pass. Across the four source-held folds, the learned curve had mean within-story rank correlation **.711** with suspense. The word's ordinal position alone reached **.723**. After taking differences between successive lines, the correlations fell to **.056** for the learned curve and **.028** for word position. Readers agreed much more strongly on these local changes: median split-half correlations were .500, .598, .298 and .473 in the four stories. Splitting a small number of stories into hundreds of thousands of ratings does not create new independent texts. There were 222 question columns across seven story-by-annotation-team records, 202 definite closing events and 2,051 usable comparisons with other concurrent questions. Repeated appearances of the same origin line cannot distinguish two questions sharing their starting line. The highest-scoring earlier-line result is conditional on a real closure occurring at the current line. Counts and decisions about a plot come from annotations with retrospective access. The separate 457,494 aligned suspense values are tied to visible screens. These observations provide distinct tests of narrative understanding, and neither measures an individual's word-by-word comprehension difficulty. The four originals contained 3,542, 12,425, 5,973 and 2,164 model tokens. On one NVIDIA L4 the run took 25.8 seconds for four story calls and two technical controls. Peak allocated device memory for the largest story was 10.19 GB. The short dense implementation and the bounded implementation differed by at most 2.8×10⁻⁸ on the original curves. Appending the genuine suffix changed those prefix curves by at most 5.3×10⁻⁵. This is a research runtime for a full story.
