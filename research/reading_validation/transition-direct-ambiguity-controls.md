# Antecedent-choice controls before external scoring

A system can distinguish “it” from “he” without tracking competing referents. In addition to the prespecified within-document comparison, repeat it within the same exact casefolded pronoun form and document, with document-equal weighting and uncertainty by whole document. Keep only strata identifying at least two pronouns with different human disagreement probabilities. Report the number of independent documents and pairs for both tests. When a released pronoun has only unanimous preceding-entity interpretations it has zero disagreement and is still included. Record both the number of independent players and the number choosing an antecedent. Compare first-mention and score recognition only on human observations available to that task.
