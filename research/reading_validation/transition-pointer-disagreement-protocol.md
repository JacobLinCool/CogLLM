# Can uncertainty about the earlier word predict disagreement between readers?

The registered [click learner](transition-pointer-protocol.md) is trained to locate the prior region chosen by a player. Its entire distribution over earlier words is available in the same article forward. This test uses that distribution to forecast a second response: the probability that two different readers make different choices. The book-only crossover study is already known. No reading-game or GAP click-forecast result, or result from the separate silver-supervised gold disagreement fit, had been inspected when this analysis was specified. [Earlier response audit](transition-game-reliability.md)

Evaluate two manifestations of disagreement in the same eligible reader sample. For each pronoun, count each eligible player once as in the original pointer study. The **literal** outcome is whether two independently selected players chose different exact preceding spans. The **entity** outcome is whether their chosen spans belong to different source-adjudicated referents. Use all ordered pairs of different players, with the without-replacement correction. Aggregate outcomes per query; the dependent pairs are not independent observations. Require four unambiguous literal-region clicks. Source silver retains probabilistically aggregated entity chains, while gold has expert-adjudicated chains. Independently recompute the silver development targets from the original XML and require that their region counts exactly match the sealed pointer training API. The gold test contains 366 pronouns with 2,196 region choices; this support is slightly narrower than the 373-pronoun stand-alone disagreement test.

For the literal outcome summarize the raw word distribution by 1 − Σᵢ pᵢ². For the entity outcome add the raw probabilities of all earlier occurrences of an identical casefolded word form and compute 1 − Σᵥ pᵥ². Both development and gold expose the complete raw distribution at each assessed pronoun. Unlabelled prior-pronoun distributions are unavailable for some silver development prefixes, so this calibration uses the raw distributions throughout. The independent [terminal-word transfer experiment](transition-pointer-chain-protocol.md) evaluates chain composition on the complete gold prefixes. Different names for one person or identical words used for distinct people will limit this text-only proxy. Forecasters receive no marked spans or entity chains. These disagreement indices summarize competing interpretations represented by the model. They differ from estimates of time, fatigue or mental effort.

Compute both indices for the native headwise observer and the lexical/POS observer. Include a previously fitted positional-recency observer on the same features as an additional baseline. For calibration to actual reader disagreement use only the 12 disjoint silver development texts. Fit a monotone logistic map to each prescribed index and response type. Standardize the index on development data, weight each source article equally and each pronoun equally within each article, fix the slope penalty at 0.01, and leave the intercept unpenalized. If its unconstrained optimum has negative slope, use the exact boundary optimum, the development mean. In addition preserve uncalibrated within-text ranking so a flat calibrator cannot mask the original signal. The constant is the source article-weighted development rate.

On gold report the expected Brier loss against a randomly chosen pair of actual readers, probability bias, and within-text rank concordance among unequal observed rates with paired 95% whole-work intervals. Give each text equal weight within a release and each release equal weight overall, keeping within-text comparisons within individual excerpts. Report numbers of informative texts, releases, independent voters, and nonunanimous queries by response. Compare native against lexical, recency and the source constant. A useful common signal requires a favorable native comparison with valid uncertainty on the actual observable. This secondary study does not claim that word entropy literally equals human ambiguity.
