# Comparing character-error detection at sentence and word scales

This retrospective audit fixes scoring before the matched generative specialist returns any movie outputs. Earlier Qwen and NER word predictions on these movies were already known. All operating thresholds, movie masks and source Punkt sentence boundaries remain those of the existing experiments.

Use the original complete, unambiguously mapped movie sentences from the book-to-movie study. A positive sentence contains at least one word intersecting a human CharE selection. A positive word in the fixed causal Qwen or NER readout triggers a sentence alert. For T5, a well-formed generated CharE category triggers a sentence alert, including predictions that cannot be matched to the target text. Score category precision, recall and F1 with 10,000 whole-movie bootstrap draws.

For localization among correctly detected positive sentences, report both (1) sentences in which the sets of predicted and human words intersect, divided by all correctly detected positive sentences and (2) contiguous predicted word spans that intersect a human word, divided by all emitted contiguous spans in those correctly detected positive sentences. Also report the same two numerators and denominators across all alerted sentences. This explicit definition handles sentences with more than one predicted mention and unmatched generated spans. The paper gives its overlap definition in words without detailed many-to-many matching rules, so its overlap score is a historical reference. Report original published values and matched movie values in separate rows, showing both evaluation groups. No movie observation changes a threshold.

The original paper's Table 6 evaluates a sentence's error category against two randomly chosen readers and compares with a held-out reader. Table 11 uses the original three-reader aggregation. Its reported test size is about 1,800 sentences across its source split. Our source JSON lacks their split and reader identifiers. These constraints prevent paired testing against their released model or exact recreation of either pool. [Original study, Sections 5 and C.3](https://aclanthology.org/2022.emnlp-main.29.pdf)
