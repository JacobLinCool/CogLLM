# Transfer a directed coherence judgment to a raw-article readout

We froze this additional secondary model before obtaining the CoheSentia and DECOR GPU forecast packet or computing benchmark results. The architectural question is whether an instruction-conditioned language judgment can act as a prior when estimating the frequency of a human-reported coherence problem. The deployed student receives only article text and original sentence positions. Its backbone is evaluated once for all positions. A fixed teacher receives the directed serialization described in the companion protocol and contributes only on the training split. Its scalar calibration is fitted on those same training human responses. Consequently the teacher supplies a prior learned with the training labels. Its pseudo-observation is not counted towards human sample size or independent evidence.

For a training boundary with k marks among n readers and calibrated teacher probability q, replace the training numerator and denominator with k+q and n+1, respectively. This gives the external teacher the fixed weight of a single additional reader. Fit exactly the existing article-only integration design, penalty, normalization, stopping tolerance and candidate backbone. Estimate the same 90th percentile negative-reader threshold on development stories. The student never uses teacher scores on evaluation examples as input. We compare its AP with the undistilled article readout using paired document bootstrap intervals. No mixture coefficient, threshold, feature or configuration will be chosen after held-out scoring.
