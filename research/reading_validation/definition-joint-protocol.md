# One shared training objective for concept proposals and explanatory links

We will compare one joint model with the separately trained contextual proposal and evidence heads from the frozen definition protocol. This experiment is registered before feature retrieval, fitting, or any DEFT or Guo outcome score. A wrong antecedent can be a fluent noun phrase which the proposal head likes, yet fails to explain the arriving clause. Positive term labels alone do not penalize this selection error.

Start with the separately fitted semantic heads. On training data, cache the highest-scoring competing candidate under their joint probability among up to eight nonoverlapping automatically proposed spans as of the final definition word. Exclude the true phrase and other labeled positive targets of the same definition. Give the target's complete term and link feature vectors and those of its competitor to a binary-choice task. The automatic endpoint ranks a span by log σ(term) + log σ(link), so minimize the corresponding pairwise softplus, propagating gradients to both readouts. Reserve half the training weight for the original term and relationship objectives (one quarter each) and half for this new selection loss. Use source-balanced comparisons, anchoring latent output weights to the separate fit with L2 penalty; choose the anchoring coefficient on the registered psychology calibration data from 0.03, 0.15, 0.75. Preserve the finite-difference gradient test and stationarity logs. The Qwen backbone remains frozen and the article still requires one forward. Ridge penalties and development-derived detection threshold are the only fitted hyperparameter choices.

Report the joint-vs-separate difference in exact full-article link selection, term AUPRC, supplied-term discrimination, and external postreading familiarity concordance with paper/source clustering. The separately fitted semantic primary and its original gate remain unchanged. This experiment tests interaction between two labels; it cannot by itself establish broad multi-study optimization.
