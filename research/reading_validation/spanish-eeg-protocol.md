# Independent physiological check at the original Spanish word boundaries

The experiment recorded BrainVision scalp voltages at 250 Hz during 21-word triplets and collected a separate forced-choice meaning decision afterward. We registered the behavioral study first. This additional analysis follows inspection of the article, public directory, recording headers and the order of stimulus markers on a single example, before reading voltage values or opening behavioral outcome arrays. The authors report a larger N400 for a category word after an unrelated clue, and a null condition effect on their ultraslow measure. Their physiology used manual ICA and interpolation. This independent channel-average reanalysis uses fully specified automatic rejection and cannot reproduce all their preprocessing choices. [Peer-reviewed study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2026.117159) · [Raw data, trigger files and original analysis](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/AVY4W)

## Original word and scalp coordinates

Restrict to published included readers with one complete 144-exposure behavior log and an available matched EEG recording. Decode each recording's own channel labels, calibrations, binary format, orientation and sampling rate. Validate the 48/48/48 High, Low and Real order against its E-Prime log at all 144 original trials, rejecting a whole recording on ambiguous alignment. Original first-word trigger is 11, 12 or 13 by assignment. Each within-trial sequence has the six remaining words of sentence 1, all seven words of sentence 2 (starting 21, 22 or 23), and all seven of sentence 3 (starting 31, 32 or 33). The first experimental target is the seventh word (trigger 7); the target superordinate is word 18 (trigger 9). Ordinary word onsets carry trigger 5. Preserve the actual timestamp for each word, including different delays around sentence boundaries. Use anonymized technical sources on host only. Stage at most three raw recordings at once, release temporary copies after extracting and hashing, and keep the compact word-level measurements with source hashes.

Use the mean of the seven distinct centroparietal channels reported by the authors (Pz, P3, P4, CP1, CP2, C3, C4), referenced to the mean left/right mastoid. The primary window is mean 300–500 ms minus mean −100–0 ms at the observed event. Positive reported values indicate larger negativity. A 100–200 ms window controls temporal selectivity. On each epoch reject ocular deviations exceeding 100 μV or scalp deviations exceeding 200 μV relative to the same pre-word baseline, also reject flat records and missing/nonfinite/out-of-range windows. Use direct voltage-window means. The 100- and 200-ms averaging intervals span integer numbers of 50 Hz cycles. Report trial attrition and a stricter 75 μV ocular sensitivity. No manual ICA or exploratory electrode/time-window search. Published physiological findings and new estimates remain separately identified.

## Inference

The positive-control contrast compares low- minus high-information negativity at word 18, across the exact same first and third sentences; a matched negative temporal control examines the common pseudoword at word 7 before the second sentence differs. The model question is whether the low-minus-high effect in model whole-category surprisal predicts variability in the low-minus-high N400 effect *across the same target families*, holding the identity of the word that was read fixed. Use Spearman correlation with paired target-family resampling, and a separate resampling of readers, recomputing cell means. Include all target families with at least three retained readers per condition. Also compare the restricted native category-onset probability and the frozen static input-embedding control registered for the semantic study. Report estimated voltage and model effects, nulls, and readers/words as separate units of analysis. Use the preregistered grouping and a paired sign-flip test on item differences for the group contrast. These measurements reflect semantic integration at a known word and reader responses after a passage. Inference about an individual’s moment of confusion requires additional evidence.

As a secondary temporal analysis, report the same condition contrast at all 21 original word indices, marking the matched first sentence and the matching third sentence. Retain the familywise 95% simultaneous uncertainty band from max standardized deviations in a family bootstrap. Inside the second sentence the actual words differ across conditions, so word 18 is the strict matched-word physiological test. Whole-word native surprisal for all words is computed by summing piecewise causal self-information, marking the first word missing whenever no prior token is available.
