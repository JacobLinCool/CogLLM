# Predicting expert sentence breaks across complete essay topics

DECOR contains 1,352 annotated sentence stops, 100 original student essays and eight writing topics. Expert judgments document whether a new sentence connects to the preceding writing. When a sentence received a negative coherence evaluation, the source also recorded up to seven semantic reasons. The seven labels can overlap. This retrospective analysis follows an unsuccessful general-domain transfer from story judgments. All DECOR essay scores were previously inspected under a separate frozen observer. The eight topic-disjoint tests evaluate an explicitly essay-trained observer and preserve the earlier external result. [Original published study](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.639/) · [Earlier study](transition-external-results.md)

Assign the eight distinct topics a fixed order by SHA-256 of their original topic text prefixed with cogllm-essay-reason-v1. In fold i reserve topic i for testing, the next topic cyclically for decision calibration, and all six remaining topics for fitting. No essay, prompt or annotated sentence enters two roles in a fold. Each topic and then each essay receives equal training mass. The source-ordered calibration topic alone sets a binary threshold by maximum F1. Report source development rates, per-topic tests and false alarms per 100 unmarked sentences.

Use the archived, single causal Qwen pass over the article to extract local word difficulty, earlier contextual relationships and projected current-sentence states at the end of each sentence. No answer question or annotated cause enters the article. Fit a matched direct binary logistic baseline to the same features, a surface-plus-surprisal binary baseline, and the previous frozen story score. For the typed observer model seven potentially co-occurring events with separate logistic probabilities in the shared frozen representation. Their complement product gives the probability that the transition is marked for any reason. A coherent sentence constrains the clean event. A completely typed marked sentence contributes the log probability of its exact seven bits. The single marked sentence lacking positive usable codes contributes only the marginal likelihood of a mark. The source does not identify one specific cause at that location. Penalize slopes by ridge .01 and require finite objective with maximum absolute gradient below 10⁻⁶.

Evaluate pooled proper log loss, average precision, AUROC, calibration, precision, recall and false alarms. Report the same losses within original essays, with uncertainty that resamples full topics and essays within topics. Held reason-specific AUROC/AP and probabilities remain secondary. Compare the new control and typed classifier on identical material. The article-level inference method remains unchanged. No new reader response to revisions, or exact word at which readers lost the thread, is measured here.
