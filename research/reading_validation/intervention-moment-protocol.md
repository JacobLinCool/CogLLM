# Calibrating a reading trajectory to the effect of a change in the earlier premise

The first-report model substantially understated the effect of changing an earlier description at a later, verbatim-identical sentence. A model trained on average trial likelihood can fit the many ordinary sentences while shrinking this within-material difference. We test whether incorporating the *measured intervention* in the loss changes that result. The working hypothesis came from the preceding inspection of these twelve families. The complete third cohort's responses remain outside fitting; the experiment is a repeated-material mechanism study.

For a text-only sequence give sentence j a first-report hazard h_j, conditional on the reader reaching j without reporting. Its unconditional incidence is F_j = h_j times the product of previous survival probabilities. Each learner receives the entire ordinary reading path through the authors' randomly assigned condition. No condition, target sentence, alternative version, future words, response from the held reader or target-word identity is supplied when applying the saved readout. At training time, the design identifies which two articles share the same critical wording and which earlier premise changed.

In each outer story fold, train on Experiment 2 trials from the remaining families. Build sentence features with a noun retriever trained on other-family author annotations and the fixed HyperLex directional knowledge ratings. Use directionality, pair concentration, regional role and their interaction in a risk model. Compare an identical estimator using only the first-report likelihood with one adding the squared error of the model's within-family difference in unconditional incidence at the randomized target. Weight the latter by its finite-sample variance, with one typical family observation's inverse precision as the weight cap. Give one vote per family. Keep the sign of the observed effect, including small or reversed effects. Both estimates are obtained from the same people and treated as a composite utility, never counted as independent experiments. Fit no parameters to the third cohort.

Compare the new estimates to the existing calibrated case-mixture and the original readout. Report observed and predicted absolute probabilities, direction of effects by family, probability of a premature report and strict path likelihood on Experiment 3. Use the previous crossed intervals and ±10 percentage-point equivalence criterion. The source-only diagnostic can decompose representation and calibration by substituting the true source role and condition. Show the oracle separately.
