# Calibrating a source-linked count across textbook subjects

The original fractional ledger assigned larger scores to words with a pending concept than to confidently empty positions, but its total mass was too large in the already examined biology/physics corpus. Additionally, each component likelihood gives equal weight to its source files and the interval-censored hazard knows which term will be explained. Neither directly optimizes the count observed over all text positions. This follow-up is retrospective for DEFT biology/physics and prospective for the unread W00 outcomes. The first result and its weaker absolute performance remain part of the report.

Estimate a single nonnegative evidence calibration coefficient for each of the position, surface and contextual existence–survival estimators. Fit it to psychology passages using the observed range of possible counts at every completed word. Use all unannotated and annotated passages. Minimize the source-balanced mean squared distance to the gold range under the fixed transformation c(t)=α c_raw(t). Propagate the *same* factor to every new mention's opening probability and subsequent surviving/closing mass, keeping factor α in [0,1] to maintain calibrated item units in this sparse system. Output causal unscaled and calibrated traces. Resolve an exactly flat minimum by the smallest coefficient. Include a validation-fitted constant-curve baseline and the zero baseline. Once α is frozen, evaluate both DEFT and W00 with no domain-specific refitting. For the natural single-article application a final article length, genre name or gold starting location is unavailable.

Report expected-count interval squared/absolute error, mean curve height at certain positive and certain empty words, source-macro uncertainty and prevalence-adjusted average precision. An improvement over the older integer stock is insufficient to claim language-specific gains. Require separately that the calibrated contextual candidate outperform the strongest calibrated prefix-visible control. Where W00 source-document IDs are unavailable report both item-resampled and source-section estimates. Use median and calibration analyses to assess the effect of sparsity.
