The planned article bootstrap treats the three segments as distinct. Its precision can be optimistic when their interpretation errors are correlated. Unknown reuse of individual game players across paragraphs adds another source of dependence. [Public source](https://github.com/dali-ambiguity/Phrase-Detectives-Corpus-2.1.4)

For disagreement concordance compare pronouns within each individual text as in the original analysis, then give each text equal weight within work and each work equal weight overall.
