# Numerical implementation of the Spanish semantic-learning study

Register the behavioral scoring program before opening any anonymized answer keys or voltages. The lexical/position control estimates the frequency of each alternative being chosen relative to its number of presentations, with one pseudo-count for each of the four choices, multiplied by training-only response-slot counts. The oracle multiplies no text feature into its training-only probability of selecting the experimenter-designated correct option in each assigned condition. Cross-fit temperature in [0,100] by bounded scalar minimization including its endpoints. Credit exact top ties fractionally. Estimate trial-weighted log loss and independent equally weighted reader and lexical-family paired comparisons, and use the more conservative of the two clustered permutation tests for the human primary endpoint. Retain originally published keypress omissions outside choice scoring.

For EEG, recover matched high and low cell means from distinct counterbalanced readers. Require at least three signals per condition and family. Independently bootstrap entire readers and recompute the within-family assignments. Compute the simultaneous time band on the common complete 21-position family set, scaling deviations by the original standard errors. Brain and model scores are oriented so that higher values indicate more post-clue difficulty under the unrelated continuation. Interpret family correlations and across-group differences separately.
