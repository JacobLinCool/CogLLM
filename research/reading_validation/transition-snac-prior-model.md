# The published SNaC specialist

Goyal, Li and Durrett had already trained a strong model to localize unexplained character references in generated narratives. Their T5-Large reads the complete target sentence with its preceding context and autoregressively generates an error type and a string describing its span. Their CharE precision, recall and F1 were 0.79, 0.81 and 0.80, respectively, with 0.98 conditional span overlap. They also report human CharE precision 0.88, recall 0.71 and F1 0.79 in that evaluation. The paper defines word-level crowd agreement separately (CharE Krippendorff’s α 0.69). Each metric has a different unit from a ranked causal score at successive word ends. [Sections 4.4 and 5, Tables 5 and 6](https://aclanthology.org/2022.emnlp-main.29.pdf)

The authors report 4.2k/230/1.8k train/development/test sentences. Their GitHub repository supplies three root files (README, example TSV, and inference script). The public data JSON identifies books, movies and source annotation segments without a train/test key. The released span checkpoint directory contains configuration, weights, tokenizer data and training arguments. The training-arguments archive supplies the names train.tsv and dev.tsv without item identifiers. These sources do not establish that our 39 test movies were excluded from that checkpoint's training set. The original published performance is therefore a historical reference. [Official repository](https://github.com/tagoyal/snac) · [Public checkpoint folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1iWHiNKdRZpQKnkR-UbgYxYpMiXrqW7_D)

The original evaluation prepends up to 921 words of previous context to the complete target sentence, separated by T5’s first sentinel. It then truncates the combined input to 1,024 model tokens and generates up to 80 output tokens. Table 9 specifies five epochs and Adam at learning rate 0.0001 with linear decay. The archived training arguments confirm batch size two with four accumulation steps, zero warmup and zero weight decay. The authors’ public implementation covers inference. They do not publish the sentence-specific training targets or original tokenizer-boundary split files alongside it. [Official scorer](https://github.com/tagoyal/snac/blob/main/snac_evaluate.py) · [Appendix C.2](https://aclanthology.org/2022.emnlp-main.29.pdf)

The distinction for CogLLM is the ability to score every original word from one causal article pass, together with a word-level external false-alarm and recall audit. The basic observation that a model can find these span labels is present in the 2022 study. An aligned re-fit can compare thresholded original word membership on the same books-to-movies split even though a generated set of spans does not provide a continuous probability at each word. [Book-to-movie protocol](transition-snac-protocol.md)

## Sentence denominators and existing editor alerts

Table 6 uses the union of two randomly selected crowdworkers’ annotations as its reference and evaluates the remaining human separately. The parenthesized model precision of .86 belongs to the original three-reader pool. Table 11 reports that original comparison in full. Type precision and recall count sentence-level decisions for each error category. The overlap column counts overlap with the correct error span. The paper does not enumerate its exact CharE confusion matrices or its alignment policy when multiple mentions of the same type occur in one sentence. These source denominators differ from word average precision and from an operational threshold’s false alarms. [Primary paper, §5, Tables 6 and 11](https://aclanthology.org/2022.emnlp-main.29.pdf)

| Evaluation group and method | CharE precision | Recall | F1 | Localization |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Published two-reader reference, full-sentence autoregressive T5-Large | .79 | .81 | .80 | Reported overlap .98 |
| Published two-reader reference, held-out human | .88 | .71 | .79 | Reported overlap .98 |
| Published original three-reader reference, T5-Large | .86 | .74 | .80 | Reported overlap .99 |
| Our movie sentences, locked causal Qwen word alerts | .454 | .996 | .624 | Word intersection in 247/249 correctly flagged sentences |
| Our same movie sentences, locked NER plus surprisal | .495 | .972 | .656 | Word intersection in 240/243 correctly flagged sentences |

Our audit used 1,416 unambiguously mapped sentences containing 22,133 words in 39 held-out movies. Readers marked an unexplained character in 250 sentences. The Qwen threshold detected 249 and also marked 299 of the 1,166 negative sentences (25.6 false alerts per 100). Among correctly flagged sentences, 296 of 405 contiguous predicted spans touched a reader-marked word. Across all 548 flagged sentences, 296 of 799 predicted spans touched a reader-marked word. The NER control detected 243 and falsely flagged 248. The Qwen operating point was selected for word detection and yields higher recall and more false sentence alerts here. The original specialist's test included approximately 1,800 sentences from a different source split. The paired comparison in this table is between our two frozen policies on identical movie sentences.
