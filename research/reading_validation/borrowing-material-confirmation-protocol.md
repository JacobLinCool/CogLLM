# Confirmation on previously ungraded articles

The third internal material split prompted a global parent-article correction after scoring. We test the fixed material-family bootstrap again on another set of archived reading materials. Both earlier split comparisons, the initially scored third split, and the discovery of article sharing informed the choice to run this confirmation. Its algorithm, controls, analysis, eligibility and retrieval order are specified before calculating the new evaluation scores.

Eligibility is determined globally before reading any fourth-split evaluation outcome. A parent article identifier is eligible only when every appearance, in every source, was used exclusively for expert fitting in all three earlier partitions. Each source nominates the first one third of its eligible families (rounded to nearest, at least one when possible) by SHA-256 of one fixed salt and the original global family ID. Limit nominations so every measured endpoint retains identifiable fitting observations. Take the union across sources. A deterministic family-level safeguard restores one training family if an endpoint would otherwise have none. Prior third-split evaluation material is completely quarantined. Retain the globally consistent third-split roles on other families. Preserve unrelated validation and calibration observations and verify each global family occupies one active role. Some sources have no eligible family under this rule. Report every exclusion. Previous fitted models saw the new families during optimization. Reinitialize and refit both experts for each of the two original seeds. Keep the native observation likelihoods, stopping criterion, sufficient statistics and exact same previously fixed estimator with its default count of samples. Fit scaling on retained expert-training rows.

The tests concern observed trial likelihood under family-bootstrap-calibrated expert sharing versus (i) the equal private/shared pool and (ii) the original target-specific Beta(2,2) mode. The first tests learned sharing against a fixed setting. The second asks whether the new estimator improves on an already-adaptive strong baseline. Cluster results by connected source group, merging the RACE and both OneStop source families when represented. Use the previously specified paired two-level connected-group and material bootstrap as the primary 95% uncertainty interval, simultaneously adjusted for two contrasts. Retain the source-only, seed-specific and source/material estimates for transparency. Give all 24 sources equal inclusion opportunity under the same rule and equal weight to the evaluable source components. With absent universal reader identities, inferences remain conditional on these populations. Keep this fourth result separate from the reported third and earlier partitions. A null or reversed result limits generalization and is not a criterion for another split.
