# Where a new sentence departs from its context

## Question and scope

Does a causal text score identify a poor connection between a two-sentence preamble and a fixed third sentence? Carter and Hoffman manipulated the connection while keeping the target sentence identical within predictability condition. They measured whole-passage coherence on a 1–5 scale in a separate norming group and timed readers advancing through individual words in the target sentence. We chose the study for these two independent tasks. The published methods and aggregate results informed our choice. A search of the project research records found no previous Carter and Hoffman run. LARRC and Frick have existing project scores. The low-coherence condition includes shifts of topic, new information and contradictions. The manipulation does not isolate an omitted causal premise. [Study, sections 2.1–2.2](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2023.105637) · [Public archive](https://osf.io/a8j4c/)

We will use Experiment 1 to avoid multiple opportunities for choosing favorable experiments. The authors selected 160 items with a clear norming contrast from 200 candidates. This restricts claims about ordinary naturally occurring transitions. We will keep all available four-version items that match exactly within each target-predictability pair, identify any mismatches before numerical scoring, and report attrition. Unavailable data will be identified explicitly. We will retain the primary evaluation even if the compact candidate fails.

## Freeze before reading item-level outcomes

We will assign whole content families to two partitions by sorting SHA-256 of the original item ID prefixed with 'cogllm-carter-independent-item-v1:' and use the first 60% as calibration and the rest as held out. All versions sharing a target stay together. Exact duplicate target prefixes across IDs will be merged before splitting. The selected existing model is the Qwen3.5-2B fixed-weight joint checkpoint with seed 1729. Its archive SHA-256 begins c1704d4436d767fa. All candidate families and ridge penalties below are fixed before scoring. Test outcomes do not determine weights, feature scaling, thresholds, exclusions or reported comparisons. ## Causal forecasts

One encoder pass per passage supplies the deployed per-token transition channel and middle and final hidden states, native attention and target-token probabilities from Qwen's tied output projection. All readouts may access only words through the endpoint being scored. We forecast at each target word and summarize the completed target sentence for the norming endpoint and the precritical region for timed reading. Target prefixes are matched by normalized visible text through the critical word boundary. A precritical prediction cannot use the upcoming critical word, condition labels, author's scores, participant identity or study item ID. No question prompt is provided. We test the following candidates.

* The lexical comparator uses log preamble and target word counts, target unique content word overlap with the preamble, and the contiguous word overlap between the end of the preamble and target. The exact target matches across coherence conditions.
* The deployed comparator uses the existing Gradio transition channel averaged over the target interval. A larger mean should predict lower coherence. Its one-dimensional linear calibration uses only calibration families. The deployable checkpoint stays frozen.
* The single-pass integration readout combines lexical controls, mean color-channel transition, word surprisal with left context, word-level attention to preamble tokens, and cosine similarity between content-token representations in the preamble and revealed target at the middle and final layers. This model tests whether an explicit comparison of the current information against the preceding sentences improves the existing readout. The first-order ridge model uses no condition, treatment-specific intercept, lookup table, upcoming words, or trainable backbone weight.

For each predictor we standardize features using calibration means and standard deviations alone and minimize mean squared prediction error plus 0.01 times the squared standardized coefficients. The intercept is unpenalized. The human scale is bounded [1,5] for whole-passage norms. Outputs are clipped to that range. The reading-time model is fitted independently on the calibration item means for the precritical interval, in log milliseconds. Each item and version receive equal weight. No penalty search is planned.

## Evaluation

The primary outcome is the improvement in held-out root mean squared error on the item-level mean whole-passage coherence ratings. We require improvement over both the lexical comparator and the calibrated deployed transition channel. The full scale is 1–5. We also report the paired low-minus-high context effect for human and model, pairwise condition ordering, and correlations. For false alerts we define an editor alert as a score below 3, the midpoint of the published coherence scale. We will report (a) the proportion of experimentally high-coherence controls alerted, (b) sensitivity on low-coherence controls, (c) false alerts among human mean ratings ≥3 and missed alerts among ratings <3, with denominators. The published manipulation is an imperfect proxy for each individual's judgment.

The separate online outcome is mean log reading time in the original precritical region of the third sentence. Use Experiment 1 and the author's exclusions where available. Record timing aggregation and units exactly from the data. Compare predicted and human low-minus-high effects in matched word windows, along with relative prediction error. If workable word-times are available, display exploratory by-position summaries without choosing the best word or claiming it is the instant comprehension failed. All interval estimates use 10,000 paired family bootstrap samples with seed 9271. They quantify uncertainty across passage families conditional on the sampled readers. One passage-level norm constrains only the score after its entire text was seen. A word-length or transition spike on its own provides no measurement of subjective effort at that word.
