# Following a concept until its explanation appears

In unseen biology and physics textbooks, the model could sometimes identify an earlier term and associate the correct later explanatory words with it. It was much more reliable after independently recognizing the term than at finding all terms in a new passage. Among 1,939 forward-defined terms in 2,477 held-out passages, it proposed 581 exact source spans. Of those, 485 received a first closure inside their own annotated definition interval. Across all automatically emitted closures this gave 485/1,627 precision (29.8%), 485/1,939 recall (25.0%) and F1 of 0.272. Exact source and timing were both required. The labels record what the text explains. The semantic model's F1 advantage over the lexical model averaged 0.166 over source files (paired 95% bootstrap interval 0.147–0.186). For both fits the original article is the sole model input. At each completed word, a causal readout can propose a new term, associate explanatory evidence with one previously proposed term, and close that specific item. The resulting count stays constant between completed words. A single-text forward produces Qwen states and native attention for all positions. The separate fit was also best on psychology calibration (F1 0.389, joint 0.317). Supplying the end of each definition made localizing the earlier term easier. At 534 annotated definitions with competing earlier terms, the contextual model chose the exact term 46.5% of the time (source-cluster interval 40.7%–52.7%), compared with 17.4% from surface form and 19.7% from position. Its gain over the lexical model was 29.0 percentage points (22.4–36.7). The joint model achieved 38.6%. With the gold earlier term supplied, several narrower tests had near-ceiling recency baselines. In the whole-passage test, only three natural cases contained two annotated, still-unexplained terms throughout the required interval. Neither contextual model achieved the simultaneous two-term criterion. Data remain too sparse for a general selective-persistence claim. The integer stock overestimated the number of outstanding terms. At each word, annotators constrain the possible count between closing at region entry and at region exit. Mean distance from that interval was 0.250 for the separate contextual fit, 0.163 for joint fitting and 0.037 for an always-empty ledger. Most positions in these short source windows carried no pending definition. Consequently, correctly matching more individual events did not establish calibration of the stock magnitude. In Guo's authentic scientific-abstract reader study, 1,838 informative term pairs from 81 papers and 11 readers compared familiarity gains within the same paper, person and initial familiarity level. The textbook-trained separate link head, given the term, ranked gains with concordance 0.494 (paper-cluster 0.448–0.539). The joint head reached 0.516 (0.469–0.561). Automatic proposals detected 14 and 8 of 581 target terms, respectively. The archive measures before/after familiarity and lacks the exact instant of resolution. The research team had examined this study for earlier readouts before registering this analysis. [Reader study source](https://github.com/talaugust/PersonalizedJargon) · [Reader comparisons](../results/reading-validation-definitions/reader-scores.json)

On 12 previously used researcher-authored early/late controls, a separately trained causal explanation head driven by an automatically chosen prior phrase recovered 9/12 contrasts, compared with 0/12 when representing the whole prior sentence and 12/12 with explicit source spans. Its stronger two-issue selective test succeeded on 4/16. This diagnostic provides a mechanism for improving regressions with representation choice. Independent human textbook and familiarity measurements carry different evidential weight.
